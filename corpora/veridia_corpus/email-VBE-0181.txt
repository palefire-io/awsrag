Tom,

Following our exchange on ticket VBST-3910 and the change to manual approval
on our tenant, I have been asked by our Medical Devices Safety Officer to get
Veridia's formal position in writing.

The question she wants answered is narrow and I will put it as plainly as she
put it to me:

"On what basis does the manufacturer consider a tenant configuration setting,
made during onboarding in 2022 by a member of staff who has since left, to
constitute authorisation for a software change to a Class C in-vitro
diagnostic medical device deployed in an NHS trust in 2025?"

She is not being difficult. Our trust's medical device management policy
requires that changes to medical device software are subject to our change
control process, and we did not know a change had occurred. That is a gap in
our controls as much as anything, and she is quite properly asking how it
arose.

For completeness, our position is not that auto-update is wrong. There are
good arguments for it, particularly for security fixes, and I would not want a
policy that leaves instruments unpatched for months while a change board meets.
Our position is that we should have known.

What would help:

1. Veridia's formal statement on the regulatory status of firmware updates
   delivered through VeriConnect, and how consent is obtained and recorded.

2. Confirmation of what notification exists today when a firmware update is
   applied to an instrument under auto-update. My understanding from your
   ticket response is that there is none. If that is right, please say so
   plainly rather than describing what is planned.

3. Whether Veridia's own technical documentation for the IVDR submission
   addresses software update distribution, and whether the Notified Body has
   commented on it. I ask because if they have, our MDSO would rather know now
   than at our next inspection.

I appreciate (3) may be commercially sensitive and I will accept a "we cannot
share that" if that is the answer.

Sarah, I have copied you as I understand this sits with Regulatory rather than
Product.
