[2026-03-04 12:15] Ruth Amankwah (Data Protection Officer):
Ms Nwosu, hello. Ruth Amankwah, Veridia's DPO.

First, a point of clarity that matters for how you handle this: for the
purposes of UK GDPR, your trust is the controller and Veridia is the
processor. The obligation to respond to the request is yours; our obligation
is to assist you, which we will.

Second, please do not send patient identifiers to us again through the support
ticketing system. I have redacted them from your message above and purged them
from the ticket store, and I have logged that action. The extraction runs
inside your own tenant and you do not need to give us the identifier at all.

What VeriConnect holds relating to a patient:
- result records (analyte values, flags, scattergram and histogram data)
- the patient identifiers transmitted with the order (NHS number, local
  number, name, date of birth, sex where supplied)
- the specimen accession and collection/report timestamps
- authorisation records: who authorised the result and when
- any free-text comments added to the result
- audit events referencing the result

Retention on your tenant is set to 7 years.

How to extract it: you need a user holding the dpo role, which carries
gdpr.export. Administration > Data Protection > Subject Access Export. Enter
the identifier, select the date range, and the system assembles results, audit
events and comments into a portable bundle (JSON plus a human-readable PDF).

Do you have a user with the dpo role? I can see your tenant has the role
available but I cannot see who holds it without an access request of my own.

[2026-03-04 16:20] Ruth Amankwah (Data Protection Officer):
Then the first step is for your tenant admin to assign it, to you or to
whoever will do this work. It should not sit with the laboratory manager
long-term - the dpo role carries erasure as well as export and separating it
from laboratory operations is the point.

Once assigned, the export itself takes a few minutes.

Three further things you will want to know:

1. Scattergram and histogram data is included in the JSON bundle but is not
   meaningfully readable by a data subject. You are entitled to describe it
   rather than reproduce it; the PDF renders it as an image.

2. The bundle will include the names of the staff who authorised the results.
   Those are third-party personal data. You will need to make your own
   decision about redaction under the exemption for third-party data; most
   trusts we work with redact staff names and retain the fact of
   authorisation. The export does not redact automatically.

3. If the request also asks for erasure, note that laboratory results are
   subject to retention obligations under your own records management policy
   and under accreditation requirements. VeriGuard's erasure workflow marks
   records subject to a retention obligation rather than deleting them, and
   records the reason. It will not silently destroy something you are required
   to keep.
