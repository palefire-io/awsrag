[2025-12-15 14:20] Marcus Chen (QC Applications Specialist):
This is a good question and I would rather you asked it than didn't.

Short answer: a shift of that size at a within-revision lot change is at the
larger end of normal but it is within normal. It is not a signal of the kind
we saw in the FSN-2025-003 episode, where the shift was progressive over days
rather than a step at changeover, and where it kept going.

The distinction that matters:
- A STEP at changeover that then stays flat is lot-to-lot variation. Every
  reagent has some. It is why we ask you to establish your own mean and SD.
- A DRIFT that begins some days after a cartridge goes on board and continues
  is a stability problem. That is what the section 7.3 threshold is really
  looking for.

What I would like from you, to be sure:
1. Continue running both levels daily and send me the WBC and NEUT% values
   from the next 10 working days.
2. Tell me the pierce date of the new cartridge.
3. Confirm nothing else changed at the same time - calibration, control lot,
   firmware.

If the shift is flat over ten days, it is lot variation and you can adjust
your target if you wish. If it continues to climb, we look harder.

[2026-01-07 13:40] Marcus Chen (QC Applications Specialist):
Flat is the answer. That is lot-to-lot variation, not instability.

Two suggestions:

1. Re-establish your control mean on the new lot from these ten days plus the
   next ten, and use your own mean rather than carrying the previous lot's
   forward. Carrying an old mean across a lot change is why the shift looks
   alarming - you are measuring the new lot against the old lot's centre.

2. If you want an early warning for the stability failure mode specifically,
   the thing to watch is not the QC mean but the background WBC count over the
   life of a cartridge. In the FSN-2025-003 episode the background began
   climbing around day 5 and kept going. A background that is stable from day
   1 to day 90 is the reassurance you are looking for.

Nothing further needed here. Thank you for the careful question - most sites

--- Resolution ---
No fault. Step shift of +0.85 SD (WBC) and +0.6 SD (NEUT%) at a within-revision
RGT-L3 lot change, flat over ten subsequent working days. Normal lot-to-lot
variation, not a stability signal.
Actions: customer to re-establish control mean on the new lot; advised to chart
background WBC count over cartridge life as the specific early warning for the
FSN-2025-003 failure mode.
See also: SOP-VBS-002 s.7.3, VBB-0047, VBI-0087
