-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
To define the procedure for changing a VeriLyse reagent cartridge lot on a
VeriScan 400 or VeriScan 400 Pro, and to define the verification required
before patient testing resumes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Applies to RGT-D5, RGT-L3, RGT-CL and RGT-RT cartridges on VeriScan 400 and
VeriScan 400 Pro instruments.

Does not apply to the VeriScan 200; that platform has a separate procedure
(SOP-VBS-002-VS2) and different reagent qualification. Note in particular that
RGT-L3 revision D must not be used on a VeriScan 200.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. DEFINITIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Lot change            A new cartridge of the same product and the same
                      formulation revision.
Revision change       A new cartridge of the same product but a different
                      formulation revision (e.g. RGT-L3 revision C to D).
Crossover study       Parallel running of old and new material to quantify
                      any shift.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. BEFORE CHANGEOVER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Plan the changeover for a period of low workload. Do not change a lot
     mid-batch.

4.2  Confirm the new cartridge is the correct product for the instrument.
     RGT-RT is VeriScan 400 Pro only and will be rejected with E-302 on a
     VeriScan 400.

4.3  Confirm the cartridge is within its unopened shelf life.

4.4  Inspect the cartridge for damage: cracks, a distorted or punctured
     septum, leakage, or crystalline deposit around the outlet. A damaged
     cartridge must not be fitted; it may raise E-311 or, worse, admit air and
     produce E-112.

4.5  Record the outgoing lot number, the incoming lot number, the date and the
     operator.

4.6  MANDATORY: check the incoming lot number against the field safety notices
     in force before fitting. The list is on the customer portal and in
     VeriConnect under Notifications > Safety. As at this revision, RGT-L3
     lots L3-2025-0440 to L3-2025-0460 are recalled under FSN-2025-003.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. FORMULATION REVISION CHANGES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Identify the formulation revision from the suffix after the lot number
     (e.g. L3-2025-0471/D) and from the label identification band. RGT-L3
     revision D carries a blue band; revision C carries a grey band.

5.2  For the RGT-L3 revision C to D change specifically:
       - No recalibration is required.
       - No change to QC target values or ranges.
       - No instrument setting change.
       - A formal crossover study is NOT required.
       - Both QC levels must be run and accepted after changeover (section 7).
     Reference: VBB-0047, which includes method comparison data.

5.3  For any future formulation revision change, Veridia will state in the
     product change notification whether a crossover study is required. Do not
     assume that because the C to D change did not require one, a later change
     will not.

5.4  Do not mix revisions within a cartridge bay. Do not decant between
     cartridges under any circumstances - the RFID tag is matched to the fill
     and a topped-up cartridge will report incorrect remaining volume and may
     permit use beyond the stability limit.

5.5  Where the laboratory's own accreditation requires a crossover study for
     any reagent change regardless of manufacturer guidance, follow the local
     requirement. A suitable protocol is at Appendix A.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHANGEOVER PROCEDURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Place the instrument in Standby. Do not power it down.

6.2  Open the reagent compartment. Note which bay corresponds to which
     reagent; the bays are keyed but the labels fade with cleaning.

6.3  Withdraw the outgoing cartridge vertically. Do not rock it; rocking
     enlarges the septum puncture and can shed septum material into the
     needle.

6.4  Fit the new cartridge, pressing home until it seats. Do not over-torque
     a screw-collar cartridge; over-torquing is a recognised cause of E-311.

6.5  The instrument reads the RFID tag and displays the product, lot and
     expiry. Confirm the displayed lot matches the label. If it does not, stop
     and raise a ticket with photographs.

6.6  Run Reagent Prime for the changed reagent (Service > Maintenance >
     Reagent Prime). This purges air introduced during the change. Omitting
     the prime is the usual cause of E-112 immediately after a changeover.

6.7  Run a background count. Acceptance limits are in SOP-VBS-001 Appendix A.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. VERIFICATION AFTER CHANGEOVER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Run both QC levels (normal and at least one abnormal). For RGT-RT
     changes on the Pro, recalibrate the reticulocyte channel first - this is
     enforced by the instrument from firmware 4.1.0 - and then run both levels
     of VT-RET-2.

7.2  Acceptance criteria are as for a routine QC run (SOP-VBS-001 section 7).

7.3  Additionally, compare the QC result to the mean of the previous 20 points
     on the same instrument. A shift of more than 1SD in the same direction
     across three or more analytes, even if all results remain within range,
     should be investigated before proceeding. This is a weak signal but it is
     the signal that would have detected the FSN-2025-003 lots earlier at
     several sites.

7.4  Record acceptance. Do not resume patient testing until QC is accepted.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. IF A LOT IS RECALLED WHILE ON BOARD
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Stop patient testing on the affected instrument.

8.2  Remove the affected cartridge and quarantine it, labelled with the date,
     the instrument serial number and the date it was first pierced. Do not
     discard it - the manufacturer may require its return for investigation.

8.3  Fit an unaffected cartridge, prime, run a background count and run full
     QC before resuming.

8.4  Determine the exposure window: the period between first pierce of the
     affected cartridge and its removal. Every patient sample analysed in that
     window on that instrument is within scope of the retrospective review
     required by the notice.

     VeriConnect Professional tier and above: Reports > Retrospective >
     Reagent Lot Exposure generates this list. From firmware 4.4.0 the
     instrument can also export the list directly for the last 400 days.

8.5  Follow the specific instructions in the field safety notice. They take
     precedence over this section.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. RECORDS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Retain the lot changeover record for the period required by the
     laboratory's accreditation, and in any case for not less than the shelf
     life of the reagent plus one year.

9.2  Minimum record content: date, time, instrument serial, reagent product,
     outgoing lot, incoming lot, formulation revision, operator, prime
     performed, background result, QC result, acceptance decision and
     reviewer.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
APPENDIX A - CROSSOVER STUDY PROTOCOL (WHERE REQUIRED LOCALLY)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A.1  Select a minimum of 40 residual patient specimens spanning the reportable
     range, including at least 8 with abnormal WBC and at least 8 with
     abnormal PLT.
A.2  Analyse each specimen on the outgoing lot and then on the incoming lot
     within 2 hours, in the same order.
A.3  Compute Passing-Bablok regression and Bland-Altman bias for each analyte.
A.4  Acceptance: slope 0.95-1.05, intercept within 0.25 of the analyte's LoQ,
     and bias at the medical decision point within half of the laboratory's
     total allowable error.
A.5  Where a shift exceeds these criteria, do not put the new lot into service;
     raise a support ticket with the data before proceeding.
