[2026-01-29 11:30] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Here's what I can establish.

1. TIMESTAMP MISMATCH - explained.
   Your two Pro instruments were on firmware 4.2.1 from 3 February to 19
   March 2025. That firmware emitted drifting timestamps, up to tens of
   minutes ahead of real time, growing with instrument uptime. The QC chart
   x-axis uses the instrument-supplied report time, so charted points from
   that period sit later than they actually occurred. This is documented in
   VBB-0041 and investigation VBI-0091. Result values are unaffected - only
   the times.
   This explains the points that "appear at times that don't match".

2. MISSING POINTS, 11 March - explained.
   There is a gap on 11 March 2025 between roughly 07:00 and 11:00 UTC. That
   is the SSO outage (VBB-0044, VBI-0094). Your staff could not log in, so QC
   entered at the instrument during that window was uploaded, but QC that
   would normally have been entered through VeriConnect was recorded on paper
   and never entered. Those points aren't missing from the chart - they were
   never in it.

3. OTHER MISSING POINTS - NOT EXPLAINED.
   You have 14 QC runs present on VS4-0812's local log between 24 February and
   9 April 2025 that never reached VeriConnect. They are not clustered around
   either of the above. I cannot account for them.

[2026-01-29 14:00] Steve Ogden (Laboratory Manager):

[2026-02-04 15:20] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
I have spent a day on point 3 and I have a partial answer at best.

What I can see: the instrument's upload queue holds results locally when the
cloud agent can't reach us and transmits when it recovers. The queue is 2,000
messages. Your MRI-site network had a series of short outages in that period -
I can see the reconnection events in the agent telemetry.

What I would expect: queued QC uploads to arrive late but to arrive.

What actually happened to those 14: I don't know. The agent logs for that
period have aged out (13-month telemetry retention, and we are now past it for
February 2025). I cannot reconstruct it.

What I am not going to do is offer you a theory dressed up as a finding. The
honest position is that 14 QC runs were performed and recorded on the
instrument, were not uploaded, and I cannot tell you why.

What I can offer:
- The instrument's local audit log and QC records for that period are intact
  and exportable. They are the primary record and they are complete. For UKAS
  purposes the instrument record is arguably the better evidence anyway.
- A written statement from me, on Veridia letterhead, describing exactly what
  I have described above including the unexplained part. If an assessor asks
  why the cloud record is incomplete, "the manufacturer investigated and
  provided this statement" is a more defensible position than a guess.
- We have since added upload queue depth monitoring, so a queue that isn't
  draining now raises an alert. That did not exist in early 2025.

--- Resolution ---
Three contributing causes, two explained:
(1) timestamp misalignment from firmware 4.2.1 (VBI-0091, VBB-0041);
(2) a QC entry gap on 2025-03-11 from the SSO outage (VBI-0094, VBB-0044);
(3) 14 QC uploads from VS4-0812 between 2025-02-24 and 2025-04-09 that never
reached VeriConnect - NOT EXPLAINED. Agent telemetry for the period had aged
out beyond the 13-month retention and could not be reconstructed.
Patient results unaffected and complete (customer is on integration topology
A). Written statement issued to the customer for their UKAS evidence pack.
Upload queue depth monitoring has since been added.
