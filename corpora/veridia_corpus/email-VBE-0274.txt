Dear Ms Lindgren,

Our quality management system requires that we hold current conformity
information for every in-vitro diagnostic device in the laboratory, and that we
review it annually. Our review is due at the end of this month.

For the VeriScan 400 Pro (we operate seven) I currently hold:
  - EC Declaration of Conformity under Directive 98/79/EC, dated 2021
  - Your statement of January 2025 that technical documentation was submitted
    to Notified Body 0086 on 30 January 2025
  - Datasheet DS-VS400P-003 stating "assessment in progress"

I would be grateful for a current written statement covering:

1. Whether the Notified Body assessment has concluded, and if not, the current
   stage and the expected timeline. I understand you may not be able to give a
   date; the stage would still help.

2. The specific transitional provision under which the devices are currently
   placed on the market, with the article reference, and the date by which
   that provision expires for a Class C device of this type.

3. Confirmation that Veridia will notify us if the transitional position
   changes, and the mechanism by which that notification would reach us. I ask
   because our experience of firmware bulletins reaching the right person at
   this hospital has been mixed.

4. Whether the reagents (RGT-D5, RGT-L3, RGT-CL, RGT-RT) are covered by the
   same submission or assessed separately, and their classification.

5. What happens to devices already in service if certification is not achieved
   before the transitional period expires. I do not expect this to occur, but
   our risk register requires that I have asked.

6. Whether the VeriPrep SP-1, as an accessory, has any IVDR obligation of its
   own.

A related question that is not strictly regulatory. Switzerland's position
following the lapse of the mutual recognition agreement means that we require a
Swiss Authorised Representative and Swissmedic registration for IVDs placed on
the Swiss market. Could you confirm Veridia's arrangements, including the
identity of your CH-REP? Our previous correspondence on this is from 2023 and I
would like it refreshed.

I appreciate this is a substantial request. It is the same request I send to
every IVD supplier in January and most of them manage it.
