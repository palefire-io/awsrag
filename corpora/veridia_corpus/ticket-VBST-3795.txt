[2024-12-19 13:20] Danny Whitcombe (Support Engineer):
That's the answer, I think. Your new tubes are about 2mm shorter overall than
the ones you were using. The r1 feed mechanism wants 73.5-80.0mm and the
barcode window is positioned for a tube sitting at the top of that band. At
74.8mm the label top edge is dropping below the scanner window, which gives
you the P-101, and the cap has less for the feed pawl to push against, which
gives you the jam.

We've seen this at a handful of sites now. Three things you can do today:

a) Ask your label printer to shift the label template up by 3mm. That alone
   usually fixes the barcode reads.
b) Load alternate carrier positions on affected racks. Doesn't stop jams but
   stops a cascade.
c) If you can get any of the old tubes back, use them until we sort this
   properly.

Longer term we have a tube adapter (SP1-ADP-05, set of 10) that lifts the tube
2.5mm and restores both. I can get you a set out. There is also a hardware
revision coming next year that widens the tolerance.

[2024-12-19 14:31] Danny Whitcombe (Support Engineer):
No risk to results. This is purely a tube handling problem in the prep
station. A tube that jams doesn't get processed at all, so there's no
possibility of a wrong result - you just have to recover it manually. And a
tube that fails the barcode read goes to the exception rack rather than being
run against the wrong patient.

The thing to watch is turnaround time and the manual handling, not accuracy.

[2025-01-09 16:40] Rob Ellery (Field Service Engineer):
Attended site. Gripper jaws were visibly glazed and the left jaw had a flat
spot - replaced with SP1-MEC-160. Feed pawl tip showed wear but was within
tolerance so left in place. Cleared a small amount of shed rubber from the
guide rail.

Confirmed the tube geometry with the customer's current stock: mean capped
height 74.9mm across 10 tubes. That's below the reliable band for r1.

Ran 60 tubes with adapters fitted after the jaw change - zero jams, 1 barcode
retry, no failures. Ran 20 without adapters - 2 P-105 and 1 jam. So the
adapters are doing real work and the site needs to keep using them.

Advised Steve to log every jam with date, rack position and tube type. Told
him about the r2 revision expected Q3 and that a retrofit will be available.
He'd like a quote when there is one.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: short-cap 5mL tubes (mean 74.9mm capped) below the reliable
handling band for SP-1r1. Compounded by gripper jaw wear.
Actions: SP1-ADP-05 adapters supplied; gripper jaws replaced under
FSV-2025-0114; label template raised 3mm; site added to r2 retrofit interest
list.
See also: VBB-0038, ticket VBST-3812, ticket VBST-4098.
