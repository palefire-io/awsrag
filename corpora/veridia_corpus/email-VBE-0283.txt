Dear Ms Renard,

Thank you for your question about French health data hosting requirements. I
want to give you a complete answer including the part that is unhelpful.

> Nous devons confirmer que VeriConnect est conforme aux exigences
> d'hebergement de donnees de sante (HDS) applicables en France.

The direct answer: Veridia does not hold HDS certification, and neither does
AWS eu-west-1 as we consume it for the VeriConnect service.

The longer answer, which I think matters for your assessment:

1. AWS itself holds HDS certification for certain of its services and regions,
   including eu-west-3 (Paris). VeriConnect is not currently deployed in
   eu-west-3 and our architecture is not currently within the scope of an HDS
   certification.

2. Whether HDS applies to VeriConnect in your specific configuration depends on
   what VeriConnect holds for you and in what capacity. If your deployment is
   what we call topology A - instruments report directly to your own LIS and
   VeriConnect receives a parallel copy for QC and fleet management - then the
   question is whether that parallel copy constitutes hosting of health data on
   behalf of a French health establishment. I am not qualified to answer that
   question under French law and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Your DPO
   and your legal counsel will need to form a view.

3. What we can offer today:
   - Hosting in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) or eu-west-2 (London) as well as
     eu-west-1 (Ireland), on Enterprise tier with VeriGuard. None of these is
     France.
   - Our full technical and organisational measures documentation.
   - ISO 27001:2022 certification (GB-ISMS-2024-0417).
   - SOC 2 Type II report under NDA.
   - A data processing agreement with the EU standard contractual clauses where
     relevant.
   - Pseudonymisation of patient identifiers, which materially changes what
     VeriConnect holds. If your instruments are configured not to transmit
     direct identifiers to VeriConnect - which is supported, and which several
     customers do specifically for this reason - then the data VeriConnect
     holds may fall outside the scope of the question entirely. This is worth
     exploring before the compliance question, because it may dissolve it.

4. What we cannot offer: HDS certification, or a deployment in eu-west-3, on
   any timeline I can commit to. There is internal discussion about French
   market requirements and I am not going to represent that discussion to you
   as a plan.

I would suggest a call between your DPO, your legal counsel, myself and Camille
Roux. If the answer is that VeriConnect cannot be used in your configuration
without HDS certification, I would rather establish that clearly than have you
discover it during an inspection.

I should add: you are the third French customer to raise this in eighteen
months. I am recording each of them, and the accumulated weight of that is the
most useful thing I can do to move our internal position.
