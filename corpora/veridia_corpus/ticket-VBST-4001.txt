[2025-03-11 08:51] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
Root cause identified. Your identity provider rotated its SAML signing
certificate this morning. VeriConnect was holding metadata cached from your
tenant onboarding and had never refreshed it, so it is rejecting assertions
signed with the new certificate as untrusted.

The failure is fail-closed, which is the correct behaviour - we are refusing
signatures we cannot verify rather than accepting them. But the underlying
gap, that we never refresh the metadata, is ours.

Fix is to refresh the metadata for each affected tenant manually. That is a
supported operation and takes about eight minutes per tenant including a login
test. There are 17 tenants. We are prioritising by size and by whether the
site has any alternative access.
