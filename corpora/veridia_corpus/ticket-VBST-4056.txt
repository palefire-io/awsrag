[2025-04-30 16:15] Hannah Bright (Support Engineer):
That's your answer. 34.1 C is above the specified operating range and well
above the point where the retic heater loses control.

Two things worth knowing:

1. The published operating range was revised this month from 15-35 C to
   18-32 C (bulletin VBB-0052). Your afternoon reading is outside the revised
   range, and would also have been marginal against the old one.

2. Your firmware is 4.2.3, which raises the W-031 ambient warning against the
   OLD 15-35 C thresholds. So the instrument has not been warning you, because
   34.1 C is inside 15-35. Firmware 4.4.0 will align the warning with the
   revised range. Until then, the absence of a W-031 does not mean the ambient
   is acceptable.

Where is the instrument sited? A 6 C rise between the room and the instrument
usually means direct sun, a heat source underneath or beside it, or restricted
airflow at the rear.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: ambient temperature at the instrument reaching 34.3 C in the
afternoon (direct sun plus restricted rear clearance), above the revised
18-32 C operating range. No instrument fault.
Actions: instrument relocated, window blind fitted, rear clearance corrected
(FSV-2025-0238). VBB-0052 supplied.
NOTE: firmware 4.2.3 does not warn at this ambient because W-031 still uses
the superseded 15-35 C thresholds until 4.4.0.
