Please don't - this comes up regularly and it's completely understandable. The
two models share the same chassis, the same screen and the same badge position,
and the difference is one word on a small plate. We have a section in the Pro
datasheet specifically about telling them apart because of how often it
happens.

For what it's worth, "the Pro" as an informal name for whichever analyser is
the newer one is something I've heard at several sites.

Where does that leave you:
- You can't do reticulocytes on this instrument. The E-302 is the instrument
  correctly refusing a cartridge it has no bay or channel for.
- The RGT-RT stain you've ordered can be returned. I'll raise that - it's
  unopened and within date, so a full credit.
- If reticulocyte counting is something you need, that's a conversation with
  your account manager about a 400 Pro, either as an additional instrument or
  as a replacement. Peter Doyle covers you.

--- Resolution ---
No fault. Customer believed they operated a VeriScan 400 Pro; instrument
VS4-0402 is a VeriScan 400 with no reticulocyte channel. E-302 was correct
behaviour rejecting an RGT-RT cartridge.
Actions: RGT-RT stock returned for full credit (RMA-2025-0884); account
manager engaged regarding a Pro; instrument labelled locally; account record
annotated.
See also: spec-veriscan-400-pro-v3.txt "Relationship to the VeriScan 400",
lookup-error-codes-veriscan-400.csv applies_to column.
