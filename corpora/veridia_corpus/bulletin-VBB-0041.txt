=====================================================
VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - FIRMWARE BULLETIN
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0041
Date Issued: 2025-03-19
Type: Mandatory Firmware Update
Affected Product: VeriScan 400 Pro (primary), VeriScan 400 (precautionary)
Affected Firmware: 4.2.1 and 4.2.2
Remedy Firmware: 4.2.3
Related documents: VBI-0091 (investigation report), log-firmware-releases.csv

SUBJECT: HL7 result timestamp drift under firmware 4.2.1 - update to 4.2.3

-----------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------
Firmware 4.2.1, released on 2025-02-03, contains a defect that causes the
timestamps written into outbound HL7 ORU^R01 messages to drift forward relative
to real time. The drift accumulates while the instrument remains powered and is
reset by a power cycle. Measured drift reached 47 minutes after 14 days of
continuous uptime on the reference instrument.

Firmware 4.2.2 (2025-02-27) was an incomplete correction and was withdrawn on
2025-03-03. Any instrument running 4.2.2 must also be updated.

Firmware 4.2.3 corrects the defect in full and is available now.

-----------------------------------------------------
WHICH FIELDS ARE AFFECTED
-----------------------------------------------------
  OBR-7   Observation Date/Time            AFFECTED (primary symptom)
  OBX-14  Date/Time of the Observation     AFFECTED
  OBR-22  Results Rpt/Status Change D/T    AFFECTED
  MSH-7   Date/Time of Message             AFFECTED (smaller magnitude)

The on-screen result timestamp, the printed report timestamp and the instrument
audit log are NOT affected. They are generated from a different clock read.
This is why the discrepancy is often first noticed as a mismatch between the
instrument display and the LIS or VeriConnect record.

Numeric analyte values are NOT affected. This is a timestamp defect only. No
result value has been altered.

-----------------------------------------------------
HOW THE DEFECT PRESENTS
-----------------------------------------------------
  - VeriConnect QC charts show points plotted ahead of when the QC was
    actually run, and Levey-Jennings x-axis ordering may interleave
    incorrectly where instruments have different uptimes.
  - LIS turnaround time reports show implausible or negative intervals between
    collection and result.
  - Some LIS middleware rejects messages where OBR-7 is in the future relative
    to the receiving system's clock. Sites have reported these as E-503
    (HL7 ACK not received).
  - Delta checks against previous results may behave unexpectedly where the LIS
    orders results by OBR-7.

VeriScan 400 (non-Pro) instruments running 4.2.1 exhibit the same code path but
a much smaller drift (typically under 90 seconds over 14 days) because the
result assembly cycle is shorter. The update is nonetheless recommended for all
instruments on 4.2.1 or 4.2.2.

-----------------------------------------------------
ACTION REQUIRED
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Identify affected instruments. In VeriConnect: Fleet > Instruments, filter
   Firmware = 4.2.1 or 4.2.2. On a standalone instrument: Settings > About.

2. Update to firmware 4.2.3.
   VeriConnect Professional/Enterprise: Fleet > Firmware > Deploy, select
   4.2.3, choose instruments, schedule outside testing hours. Requires the
   firmware.deploy permission (Enterprise tier).
   Standalone: download 4.2.3 from the customer portal to a FAT32 USB stick,
   Service > Firmware > Update from USB. Do not power cycle during the update
   or E-512 will be raised.

3. As an interim measure before the update can be scheduled, power cycle the
   affected instrument at the start of each working day. This resets the
   accumulated drift to zero and holds the error under approximately 4 minutes
   for a normal working day.

4. Review historical data if turnaround time or QC timing is used for
   accreditation evidence. Veridia can supply a per-instrument drift
   reconstruction on request; contact support with the instrument serial number
   and the date range. Instruments enrolled in VeriConnect since 4.2.3 report
   drift telemetry automatically.

5. No action is required in respect of previously reported patient results.
   Result values are unaffected. Where a laboratory judges that a corrected
   timestamp is required for a specific record, results may be re-transmitted
   with result status C from the instrument's result archive.

-----------------------------------------------------
ROOT CAUSE (SUMMARY)
-----------------------------------------------------
Full detail is in VBI-0091. In brief: the 4.2.1 refactor of the HL7 timestamp
assembly routine replaced a direct read of the real-time clock with an
accumulator seeded at boot and advanced by the result-cycle scheduler tick. The
scheduler tick is nominally 100 ms but is extended by optical integration on
the Pro's reticulocyte and extended-flagging paths, so the accumulator advanced
faster than wall-clock time. The defect was not detected in verification
because the automated HL7 conformance suite runs against a freshly booted
instrument and completes in under 40 minutes.
