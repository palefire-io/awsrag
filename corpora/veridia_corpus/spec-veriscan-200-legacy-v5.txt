INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Automated haematology analyser for the in-vitro quantitative determination of
haematological parameters including a three-part leucocyte differential in
whole blood collected into K2 or K3 EDTA. For professional laboratory use.

NOTE: three-part differential only (granulocyte, lymphocyte, mid-cell). The
five-part differential is available on the VeriScan 400 and 400 Pro.
