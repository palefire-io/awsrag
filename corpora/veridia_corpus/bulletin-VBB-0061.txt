VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - FIRMWARE BULLETIN
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0061
Date Issued: 2025-09-02
Type: Feature Release (optional update)
Affected Product: VeriScan 400, VeriScan 400 Pro
Firmware version: 4.3.0
Supersedes: 4.2.3

SUBJECT: VeriScan firmware 4.3.0 - feature release

-----------------------------------------------------
NEW IN 4.3.0
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Extended flagging algorithms (VeriScan 400 Pro only)
   Adds four new research-use-only morphology flags: BLAST?, NRBC?,
   PLT-CLUMP? and FRAGMENTS?. These flags are advisory and must not be used as
   the sole basis for a clinical decision. The existing validated flag set is
   unchanged. New flags may be disabled per-site under
   Settings > Flagging > Advisory Flags.

2. Reagent on-board countdown
   The instrument now displays remaining on-board days for each cartridge
   against the 90-day limit, and raises W-035 at 7 days rather than at 3.
   Countdown begins at first pierce, not at cartridge insertion.

3. VeriPrep SP-1 host protocol version 2
   Faster handoff and per-tube status reporting. REQUIRES SP-1 firmware 2.3.0
   or later. An SP-1 on an older firmware will raise P-407. See VBB-0055.

4. Ambient temperature logging to VeriConnect
   Ambient temperature recorded with every run and available under
   Reports > Environment on Professional tier and above.

5. Drift telemetry (carried forward from 4.2.3)
   Clock drift between the real-time clock and NTP reported to VeriConnect
   daily. Introduced in 4.2.3 following VBI-0091 and retained.

-----------------------------------------------------
FIXED IN 4.3.0
-----------------------------------------------------
  - E-302 RFID read failures at low ambient humidity (28 reports since 4.2.3).
  - Body fluid mode: dilution factor field accepted a trailing space, which
    caused a spurious E-715. (Pro only.)
  - QC scheduling: a QC run scheduled to start within 60 seconds of midnight
    was assigned to the previous day.
  - Printed report: sample ID truncated at 18 characters instead of 24.

-----------------------------------------------------
KNOWN ISSUES IN 4.3.0
-----------------------------------------------------
  - Instruments enrolled in VeriConnect before firmware 4.1.0 may need to
    re-enrol after updating to 4.3.0. Symptom: instrument shows as Offline in
    the fleet view while operating normally. Remedy: Settings > Cloud >
    Re-enrol, then scan the QR code from VeriConnect.
  - The W-031 ambient temperature warning still uses the superseded 15-35 C
    thresholds. Revised thresholds land in 4.4.0. See VBB-0052.
  - Advisory flags increase result processing time by approximately 0.4 s per
    sample on Pro instruments. At sustained maximum throughput this reduces
    effective throughput from 80 to approximately 77 samples/hr. Disable
    advisory flags if maximum throughput is required.

-----------------------------------------------------
UPDATE PATH AND PREREQUISITES
-----------------------------------------------------
  From 4.2.3:  direct update supported.
  From 4.2.1 or 4.2.2:  update directly to 4.3.0. This also remedies the
                        timestamp defect described in VBB-0041.
  From 4.1.x:  update to 4.2.3 first, then to 4.3.0.
  From 4.0.x or earlier: contact support. A staged update is required and
                        calibration must be verified afterwards.

Full QC (both levels) must be run and accepted after any firmware update
before patient testing resumes. See SOP-VBS-001 section 9.
