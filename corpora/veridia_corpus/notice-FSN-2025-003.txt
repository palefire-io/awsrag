-----------------------------------------------------
DESCRIPTION OF ISSUE
-----------------------------------------------------
Veridia BioSystems has identified that RGT-L3 lyse reagent cartridges from the lot
range above may exhibit elevated baseline absorbance at 535 nm. The elevation
develops progressively after the cartridge is placed on board and becomes
measurable from approximately day 5 of on-board use.

On VeriScan 400 analysers the elevated baseline shifts the WBC differential
scatter/absorbance gating thresholds. The observed effect is:

  (a) spurious WBC abnormal-scatter flags (instrument flag "WBC ABN SCATTER");
  (b) intermittent W-042 "Background WBC count elevated" warnings;
  (c) in a minority of cases, E-208 "Baseline absorbance out of range" errors
      that halt the run;
  (d) a positive bias on the WBC count of up to +0.9 x10^9/L at the upper end
      of the observed distribution.

The bias is not accompanied by a corresponding change in the RBC or platelet
channels. Haemoglobin results are unaffected.

-----------------------------------------------------
SCOPE - WHY VERISCAN 400 PRO IS NOT AFFECTED
-----------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 400 Pro uses a laser-based optical bench with an independent
reference photodiode and applies a per-run baseline subtraction that compensates
for the absorbance shift described above. Testing of the affected lots on Pro
instruments (n = 240 samples across 4 instruments, see report VBI-0087 section 5)
showed no clinically significant deviation.

Customers operating a mixed fleet must nonetheless withdraw affected lots from
ALL instruments, because cartridges are physically interchangeable between
models and there is no on-instrument block preventing use of an affected lot on
a VeriScan 400.

-----------------------------------------------------
RISK ASSESSMENT
-----------------------------------------------------
A health hazard evaluation was completed on 2025-02-12. The identified risk is
that a spuriously elevated or flagged WBC result could prompt unnecessary
repeat testing, a blood film review, or - in the worst credible case - a
clinical decision based on an apparent leucocytosis that is not present.

Because the affected results carry instrument flags in the great majority of
cases, and because laboratory practice requires flagged differentials to be
reviewed before authorisation, the residual risk of an unreviewed erroneous
result reaching a clinician is assessed as LOW. No serious injury, no death and
no adverse patient outcome has been reported to Veridia in connection with this
issue.

Nineteen (19) customer complaints were received between 2025-01-28 and
2025-02-13 that are attributable to this issue.

-----------------------------------------------------
ACTION REQUIRED
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Immediately quarantine all RGT-L3 cartridges with lot numbers in the range
   L3-2025-0440 to L3-2025-0460 inclusive, whether on board an instrument or in
   stores. The lot number is printed on the cartridge label and on the outer
   carton, and is also displayed on the instrument under
   Reagents > Cartridge Detail.

2. If an affected cartridge is currently on board a VeriScan 400, remove it,
   install an unaffected cartridge, and run a Blank Cycle followed by full
   QC (both levels) before resuming patient testing.

3. Review WBC results and differentials reported from VeriScan 400 instruments
   during the period in which an affected lot was on board. Veridia recommends
   that laboratories review flagged results in particular. VeriConnect users on
   Professional tier and above can run the report
   Reports > Retrospective > Reagent Lot Exposure to generate this list
   automatically.

4. Complete and return the enclosed Customer Reply Form within 10 working days,
   even if you hold no affected stock. Return by email to
   fsn-response@veridiabio.com or by fax to +44 1223 496 118.

5. Arrange return of quarantined stock. Veridia will issue a returns
   authorisation number and prepaid shipping documentation on receipt of your
   reply form. Replacement stock from lot L3-2025-0471 onwards will be shipped
   at no charge, carriage paid, on a 1:1 basis.

6. Forward this notice to any other organisation to which you have transferred
   affected product.

-----------------------------------------------------
INVESTIGATION STATUS
-----------------------------------------------------
Root cause investigation is documented in report VBI-0087. At the time of
issue, the investigation had provisionally identified a raw material change in
the buffering agent supplied for the affected production campaign. The
investigation was closed on 2025-04-02; a summary is available on request.
A revised formulation (revision D) entered production with lot L3-2025-0471.
