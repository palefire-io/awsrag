Now the rest.

1. AUTOLOADER SKIPPING
Not a fault. If a tube fails the cap detect or the level check on first
presentation, the instrument moves on and re-presents it at the end of the rack
rather than stopping the batch. If it fails twice you get an error. Going
1, 2, 4, 5 then 3 means position 3 was retried successfully.

If you want to know why a position was deferred, Log > Events will show it. A
recurring deferral at the same position usually means an underfilled tube or a
label wrapped over the cap.

2. EXTENDED CLEAN FREQUENCY
"As needed" is not good enough for an SOP, you are right. What comparable sites
do at your volume (you said around 300 a week at install):

  - Automatic shutdown clean: daily, which happens on its own.
  - Extended Clean: weekly as a routine, plus whenever the background count
    creeps above about half the acceptance limit, plus after any known
    contamination event.

Marcus Chen is copied and can send you the applications note.

3. REAGENT LOT NUMBERS
Reagents > Cartridge Detail on the instrument. It shows product, lot, expiry
and days on board. Read them from there rather than the label, because if the
two disagree you want to know - a mismatch means an RFID or labelling problem
and should be raised with us.

4. LEVEY-JENNINGS HISTORY ON ESSENTIALS
The instrument holds 10,000 results and the on-screen chart shows 30 days.
Essentials tier in VeriConnect gives you charting but not the extended reporting
and export. Professional gives you configurable chart ranges, scheduled export
and the reagent lot exposure report.

I am not going to tell you to upgrade. For a two-instrument site doing 300 a
week, Essentials is a reasonable choice and plenty of labs your size stay on
it. If your accreditation assessor asks for longer QC history, you can export
from the instrument to USB, which is a manual job but it works. Peter Doyle can
give you the Professional numbers if you want to compare.

5. IQ/OQ REPORT
A scan is fine for your records, provided your quality system accepts scanned
documents generally. We hold the original signed copy at our end for the
lifetime of the instrument and can reissue a certified copy if you ever need
one. I would keep your paper original too if you have the filing space, purely
because accreditation assessors sometimes ask for wet signatures.

6. CATCH-UP TRAINING
Covered. Your install included a training day and two people missing it is
normal. I will do a half-day session for the two of them and anyone else who
wants a refresher. Let me know a date that suits. No charge.
