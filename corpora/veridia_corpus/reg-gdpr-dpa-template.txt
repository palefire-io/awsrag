1. ROLES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  The Controller determines the purposes and means of the processing of
     personal data through the VeriConnect service. The Processor processes
     personal data on the documented instructions of the Controller.

1.2  The parties agree that the Controller is the controller of patient
     personal data and of the personal data of its own personnel who use the
     Service. Nothing in this Agreement makes the Processor a controller of
     that data.

1.3  Where the Processor processes personal data for its own purposes -
     specifically, the account and billing data of the Controller's
     administrative contacts, and aggregated non-identifiable telemetry used
     for product improvement - it does so as controller and outside the scope
     of this Agreement. The Processor's privacy notice applies.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SUBJECT MATTER, DURATION, NATURE AND PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Subject matter   Provision of the VeriConnect cloud laboratory result
                 management, quality control and instrument fleet management
                 service, and where subscribed the VeriGuard compliance module.

Duration         For the term of the Services Agreement, plus the retention
                 periods at clause 10.

Nature           Collection, storage, structuring, retrieval, use,
                 transmission, restriction and erasure by automated means.

Purpose          To enable the Controller to manage, review, authorise and
                 quality-assure diagnostic results generated by Veridia
                 instruments, and to manage those instruments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECT AND PERSONAL DATA
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Data subjects
  - Patients whose specimens are analysed on the Controller's instruments
  - The Controller's personnel who use the Service

Personal data - patients
  - Identifiers transmitted with the order: NHS number or equivalent national
    identifier, local hospital number, specimen accession number
  - Name, date of birth, sex, where transmitted by the Controller
  - Encounter location, where transmitted
  - Health data: haematology analyte values, instrument flags, histogram and
    scattergram data, free-text comments entered by the Controller's personnel
  - Collection and reporting timestamps

Personal data - the Controller's personnel
  - Name, work email address, job role, assigned permissions
  - Authentication events, IP address, session identifiers
  - Actions recorded in the audit trail

SPECIAL CATEGORY DATA. Patient data processed under this Agreement is data
concerning health within the meaning of Article 9. The Controller is
responsible for identifying the Article 9(2) condition on which it relies.

PSEUDONYMISATION. The Controller may configure its instruments not to transmit
direct patient identifiers to the Service, in which case results are held
against the specimen accession number only. Where the Controller does so, the
data remains personal data in the Processor's hands to the extent it is
capable of re-identification by the Controller, but the risk profile changes
materially. The Processor recommends this configuration where the Controller's
use of the Service does not require patient identifiers, and will assist with
it at no charge.

4. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The Processor shall:

4.1  process personal data only on the documented instructions of the
     Controller, including as to international transfers, unless required by
     law, in which case it shall inform the Controller before processing unless
     the law prohibits it;

4.2  ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by
     confidentiality obligations;

4.3  implement the technical and organisational measures described in Schedule
     1;

4.4  not engage a sub-processor without the Controller's prior authorisation
     (see clause 6);

4.5  taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Controller by
     appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to
     requests to exercise data subject rights. The VeriGuard module provides
     subject access export and erasure workflows operable by the Controller
     within its own tenant; where VeriGuard is not subscribed the Processor
     will assist manually within 10 working days of a written request;

4.6  assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with Articles 32 to 36,
     taking into account the nature of the processing and the information
     available to the Processor;

4.7  at the choice of the Controller, delete or return all personal data at the
     end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless
     retention is required by law;

4.8  make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate
     compliance with Article 28 and allow for and contribute to audits (see
     clause 8);

4.9  immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction
     infringes applicable data protection law.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. PERSONAL DATA BREACH
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any
     event within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach
     affecting the Controller's personal data.

5.2  The notification shall describe, so far as known at the time: the nature
     of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and
     records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed,
     and the contact point for further information. Where information is not
     available at the time of notification it shall be provided in phases
     without undue delay.

5.3  The Processor shall not make any public statement or notify any supervisory
     authority in respect of a breach affecting the Controller's personal data
     without first consulting the Controller, save where the Processor is
     itself under a legal obligation to do so.

5.4  For the avoidance of doubt, an availability incident that does not involve
     unauthorised access to or loss of personal data is not a personal data
     breach. The Processor will nonetheless notify the Controller of any
     material service incident under the Services Agreement.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Personal data is hosted in the region configured for the Controller's
     tenant. The default is AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland). Enterprise tier with
     VeriGuard permits eu-central-1 (Germany), eu-west-2 (United Kingdom) or
     us-east-1 (United States).

7.2  The Processor is established in the United Kingdom. Transfers from the
     EEA to the United Kingdom are made in reliance on the European
     Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

7.3  Where a tenant is configured for us-east-1, transfers are made under the
     EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU)
     2021/914, Module Two) and, for UK-originating data, the UK International
     Data Transfer Addendum. A transfer impact assessment is available.

7.4  Support access from outside the configured region is possible and is
     recorded as an access event in the audit trail. The Controller may
     restrict it entirely, at the cost of longer support resolution times.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. AUDIT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  The Processor shall make available its ISO 27001:2022 certificate, its
     SOC 2 Type II report (under NDA), the executive summary of its annual
     penetration test (under NDA) and its NHS Data Security and Protection
     Toolkit submission where applicable.

8.2  The Controller may, on 30 days' notice and not more than once in any
     12-month period save following a personal data breach, audit the
     Processor's compliance. The parties shall agree the scope in advance. The
     Controller shall bear its own costs and the Processor's reasonable costs
     where the audit exceeds one working day.

8.3  The Processor may satisfy an audit request by providing the reports at
     clause 8.1 where they address the matters in scope.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  The Processor shall not respond directly to a data subject request
     relating to the Controller's personal data. It shall forward the request
     to the Controller within 3 working days.

9.2  Where a request is received directly from a data subject the Processor
     shall inform them that the request must be directed to the Controller and
     shall not disclose personal data to them.

                          Controller requests return or a legal hold applies

or accreditation retention obligations and that configuring a shorter retention
period than those obligations require is the Controller's decision and
responsibility.

SCHEDULE 1 - TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES (SUMMARY)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Encryption            TLS 1.2 or 1.3 in transit. AES-256 at rest. Customer
                      managed keys via AWS KMS on Enterprise tier.
Access control        Role-based, least privilege, named accounts, mandatory
                      MFA for local accounts, SAML/OIDC SSO on Enterprise.
                      Veridia staff access to customer tenants is
                      time-limited, approved, logged and notified.

                      another's data.
Logging               Immutable hash-chained audit trail (VeriGuard). Daily
                      tamper-check.
Resilience            Multi-AZ deployment. RPO 15 minutes, RTO 4 hours.
                      Backups encrypted and tested quarterly.
Personnel             Background checks, confidentiality undertakings, annual
                      data protection and security training.
Development           Secure development lifecycle, code review, dependency
                      scanning, annual third-party penetration test.
Incident response     Documented plan, tested annually, 24-hour breach
                      notification commitment.
