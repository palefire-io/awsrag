Hello,

I lead partnerships at Lumen Path, a UK company building deep learning models
for peripheral blood film morphology. We are Class B CE marked under IVDR for
our film review triage product and we work with three digital microscopy
vendors today.

I am writing because a mutual customer - a large NHS network in the North West
- suggested we talk. Their situation is that VeriScan analysers flag samples
for film review, a scientist decides whether to make a film, and our system
then reviews the film. The handoff between those steps is a person retyping an
accession number, and everyone involved thinks that is silly.

What we would like to explore:

1. An integration that takes the VeriScan flag set - including the advisory
   flags in firmware 4.3.0 - and uses it as an input to our triage model, so
   that our confidence estimate is informed by the analyser's scatter data
   rather than by the image alone.

2. A return path so that our morphology finding lands back in VeriConnect
   against the same accession, visible to the scientist reviewing the result.

3. Longer term, and more speculatively: the analyser's raw scattergram data is
   a richer input than the flag set. If there were an API for it we think there
   is a real research question about whether the combination outperforms either
   alone.

I am aware this raises questions on your side that I cannot answer:
- whether exposing advisory flags to a third party is compatible with their
  research use only status
- whether a return path writing into VeriConnect creates a regulatory
  entanglement between our device and yours
- whether your API v2 supports what we need, which from the documentation I
  think it partially does

I would rather have a short exploratory call than send a formal proposal into a
process. If there is no appetite, tell me and I will stop - I would rather a
quick no than a slow maybe.

For context on scale: we are 34 people, Series A, currently deployed at eleven
UK laboratories and two in Ireland. I am not pretending we are a large company.
But the customer who suggested this call is one of yours and is asking both of
us the same question.
