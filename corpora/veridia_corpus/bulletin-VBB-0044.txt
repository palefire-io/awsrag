=====================================================
VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - SECURITY ADVISORY
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0044
Date Issued: 2025-03-21
Type: Service Availability Advisory
Affected Product: VeriConnect (all tiers), VeriGuard add-on
Affected Region: eu-west-1 (Ireland)
Incident window: 2025-03-11 07:12 UTC to 2025-03-11 11:06 UTC
Related documents: report-VBI-0094 (post-incident review)

SUBJECT: SAML single sign-on outage of 11 March 2025 - customer advisory

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENED
-----------------------------------------------------
Between 07:12 and 11:06 UTC on 11 March 2025, users at customer tenants
configured for SAML single sign-on against NHS Identity (and, for a subset of
tenants, against customer-operated Microsoft Entra ID) were unable to
authenticate to VeriConnect. Affected users received the error
"SSO_ASSERTION_INVALID (VC-4412)".

The immediate trigger was a scheduled rotation of the identity provider's SAML
signing certificate. VeriConnect held a cached copy of the identity provider
metadata that had been fetched at the time of tenant onboarding and was not
being refreshed. When the identity provider began signing assertions with the
new certificate, VeriConnect rejected the signature as untrusted.

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS AND WAS NOT AFFECTED
-----------------------------------------------------
Affected:
  - Interactive login to the VeriConnect web application for SSO tenants.
  - The VeriConnect mobile fleet dashboard for the same users.

NOT affected:
  - Analyser operation. VeriScan and VeriPrep instruments run independently of
    VeriConnect and continued to process samples throughout.
  - HL7 result transmission from instruments to laboratory LIS. This path does
    not traverse VeriConnect authentication.
  - Result data integrity. No data was lost, altered or exposed.
  - Break-glass local accounts, where configured.
  - Tenants using local password authentication rather than SSO.

This was an availability incident. It was NOT a security breach. No
unauthorised access occurred. The failure mode was fail-closed: VeriConnect
refused to accept assertions it could not verify, which is the correct
behaviour.

-----------------------------------------------------
IMPACT
-----------------------------------------------------
17 customer tenants were affected, covering an estimated 1,340 named users.
Nine support tickets were raised during the incident window, the first at
07:26 UTC.

Laboratories reported the principal operational impact as loss of access to QC
charting and to the authorisation queue, requiring temporary reversion to
instrument-local result review and paper QC records.

-----------------------------------------------------
REMEDIATION COMPLETED
-----------------------------------------------------
  1. (11 March, 11:06 UTC) Identity provider metadata refreshed manually for
     all affected tenants; service restored.
  2. (14 March) Automatic metadata refresh implemented, running every 6 hours
     with signature validation against both current and next-signing
     certificates where the identity provider publishes them.
  3. (18 March) Monitoring added for metadata staleness (alert at 24 hours) and
     for SSO authentication failure rate (alert at 5 per cent over 5 minutes).
  4. (19 March) Break-glass account provisioning made part of standard tenant
     onboarding rather than an optional step.
  5. (21 March) This advisory issued.

-----------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED CUSTOMER ACTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------
  1. Confirm that at least two break-glass local administrator accounts exist
     for your tenant, that their credentials are held in your organisation's
     password vault, and that they are excluded from SSO enforcement.
     VeriConnect: Administration > Users > filter Authentication = Local.
     This requires the users.manage permission.

  2. Where your organisation controls the identity provider, notify Veridia of
     planned certificate rotations at least 5 working days in advance via
     support@veridiabio.com. This is no longer strictly necessary following
     remediation item 2, but remains good practice for major IdP migrations.

  3. VeriGuard customers: the audit trail for the incident window is complete.
     Failed authentication attempts are recorded with reason code VC-4412 and
     are available under Audit > Authentication. Note that because users could
     not log in, there is an expected gap in user-activity audit events during
     the window; this gap is explained by this advisory and should be
     referenced in any accreditation evidence pack.

  4. Laboratories holding ISO 15189 accreditation may wish to file this
     advisory with their business continuity records.
