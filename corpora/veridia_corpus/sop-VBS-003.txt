-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
To define the weekly decontamination cycle for the VeriPrep SP-1 and the
procedure for safely clearing a tube feed jam.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
All VeriPrep SP-1 stations, hardware revisions r1 and r2.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. SAFETY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  The SP-1 tube path handles uncapped primary blood tubes. Treat every
     surface within the enclosure as contaminated.

3.2  PPE: laboratory coat, disposable nitrile gloves, eye protection to
     EN 166.

3.3  Do not place hands inside the enclosure while the instrument is running.
     Opening the lid raises P-501 and stops motion, but the gripper may
     complete an in-progress movement.

3.4  DO NOT USE HYPOCHLORITE (bleach) IN OR ON THE SP-1 FLUID PATH. The
     aliquot path contains components that degrade on hypochlorite exposure,
     producing P-201 aliquot volume errors within weeks. Use RGT-CL only.
     External surfaces may be wiped with a 70 per cent alcohol wipe or with a
     chlorine-releasing agent at the manufacturer's stated dilution, avoiding
     the barcode window and the optical sensors.

3.5  Sharps: the aliquot needle is not user-accessible in normal operation. If
     a jam clearance exposes it, stop and call service.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. FREQUENCY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Daily     Empty cap waste bin; visual inspection of the tube path
  Weekly    Full decontamination cycle (this SOP, section 5)
  Weekly    Barcode window clean
  Monthly   Gripper jaw inspection
  Annual    Preventive maintenance visit by Veridia
  Immediately after any spill of sample within the enclosure

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WEEKLY DECONTAMINATION CYCLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Confirm no tubes remain in the input, output or exception racks. Remove
     all racks.

5.2  Confirm the RGT-CL reservoir contains at least 200 mL. The cycle
     consumes approximately 180 mL. An interrupted cycle raises P-505 and must
     be repeated in full - a partial cycle provides no assurance.

5.3  Empty the cap waste bin and fit a fresh liner (SP1-BIN-CAP).

5.4  Select Service > Maintenance > Decontamination Cycle. Confirm.

5.5  The cycle takes approximately 34 minutes and comprises:
       a) flush of the aliquot fluid path with RGT-CL;
       b) 12-minute static soak;
       c) purified water rinse x3;
       d) gripper and carrier wash sequence;
       e) dry cycle;
       f) self-test.

5.6  Do not open the lid during the cycle. P-501 will abort it and P-505 will
     be logged.

5.7  On completion, review the self-test report. Any failure must be resolved
     before the instrument is returned to service.

5.8  Wipe the enclosure interior surfaces, the carrier rails and the rack
     seats with a lint-free cloth dampened with 70 per cent alcohol. Do not
     spray directly onto the barcode window or the tube height sensor; dampen
     the cloth.

5.9  Record the cycle in the maintenance log.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. CLEARING A TUBE FEED JAM (P-112)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Background. P-112 indicates that a tube has failed to index through the
     feed mechanism and has wedged. On SP-1r1 units the most common cause by
     a wide margin is a 5 mL tube with a short-profile cap sitting low in the
     carrier. See VBB-0038 for the full explanation and mitigations. On r2
     units the mechanical cause is largely eliminated and a P-112 should be
     treated as a genuine fault.

6.2  Stop the run if it has not already stopped. Do not press Resume before
     clearing the jam; resuming drives the feed pawl against the wedged tube
     and can shear the pawl.

6.3  Open the lid. P-501 will be raised. This is expected.

6.4  Release the carrier lock (the grey lever at the left of the feed
     assembly). Pull it toward you until it clicks.

6.5  Withdraw the wedged tube VERTICALLY, without rotating it. Rotating a
     wedged tube is how caps get sheared off and how the guide rail gets
     scored.

6.6  If the tube will not move by hand, do not force it and do not use metal
     tools in the tube path. Call service. A scored guide rail is a
     chargeable repair; a stuck tube is not.

6.7  Inspect for:
       - shed cap material (rubber crumb or plastic flake) in the feed pawl
         and the guide rail;
       - a bent or worn feed pawl tip;
       - liquid in the carrier from a tube that has leaked;
       - damage to the gripper jaws.

6.8  Remove any debris with tweezers (plastic) or a lint-free swab. Wipe any
     spilled sample and disinfect the affected area.

6.9  Close the lid. Run Service > Diagnostics > Gripper Home. This re-datums
     the gripper, which is frequently left off-position by a jam and will
     otherwise raise P-115 on the next run.

6.10 Run a 10-tube verification batch with known-good tubes before resuming
     production work.

6.11 Record the jam: date, time, tube type and nominal volume, whether the cap
     was short-profile, the rack position, and the action taken. This record is
     what allows a pattern to be demonstrated when requesting an r2 retrofit
     or an adapter supply, and several sites lost time in 2025 because they
     had not kept it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. GRIPPER JAW INSPECTION AND REPLACEMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Inspect monthly for glazing, flattening of the textured contact face, or
     cracking.

7.2  Replacement interval:
       SP-1r1 (acetal jaws)        12 months, or on wear
       SP-1r2 (glass-filled PPS)   24 months, or on wear

7.3  Part number SP1-MEC-160. Replacement is a user-serviceable task but
     requires a torque driver; if one is not available, schedule the change
     with the annual PM visit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. ESCALATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Raise a support ticket if:
       - a jam recurs more than twice in one week on an r1 unit despite
         adapters or a raised label position;
       - any jam occurs on an r2 unit;
       - the decontamination self-test fails;
       - P-201 aliquot volume errors appear (consider whether hypochlorite has
         ever been used - see 3.4);
       - the guide rail is visibly scored.

8.2  Include: serial number, firmware version, the jam record from 6.11, tube
     supplier and nominal volume, and photographs of the feed area.
