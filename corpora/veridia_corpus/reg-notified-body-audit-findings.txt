2. OBSERVATIONS RAISED
-----------------------------------------------------------------

NB-2025-OBS-01
Area:       Post-market surveillance / complaint handling
Finding:    "The complaint handling procedure defines a target of 15 working
            days for completion of a health hazard evaluation. In the case
            reviewed (FSN-2025-003), the interval between the first complaint
            reasonably attributable to the issue and the completion of the
            health hazard evaluation was 15 days, during which affected product
            remained in distribution and in use. The organisation may wish to
            consider whether the target interval is consistent with its stated
            risk policy."

Veridia response and action:
            Accepted. SOP-VBS-014 revision 2 (2025-04-28) reduces the health
            hazard evaluation target from 15 to 5 working days and introduces
            a weekly complaint clustering review, which is the control that
            would have shortened the interval before the evaluation began.
            The clustering review is the more significant change: the
            evaluation was not slow, the recognition that one was needed was.
            Status: CLOSED, verified at surveillance audit 2026-04.

NB-2025-OBS-02
Area:       Design and development / software - access control
Finding:    "Evidence was seen of customer tenants operating shared user
            accounts within the VeriConnect platform. While the platform
            provides adequate controls, the organisation does not
            systematically detect or advise customers of this practice, which
            undermines the traceability of result authorisation."

Veridia response and action:
            Accepted. Three actions:
            (a) SOP-VBS-004 revision 5 (2025-09-15) states the prohibition on
                shared accounts explicitly and gives the reasons.
            (b) VeriConnect now surfaces likely shared-account patterns
                (concurrent sessions from disparate addresses, login frequency
                inconsistent with a single user) to tenant administrators.
            (c) Onboarding materials revised.
            Veridia notes for completeness that account practice within a
            customer tenant is the customer's responsibility as controller.
            The observation is nonetheless fair: we can see it and we were not
            telling anyone.
            Status: CLOSED, verified at surveillance audit 2026-04.

NB-2025-OBS-03
Area:       Design and development / software lifecycle
Finding:    "The abbreviated release path applied to firmware hotfixes omits
            the extended soak testing applied to major releases. Two defects
            reaching the field in Q1 2025 (firmware 4.2.1 and 4.2.2) were
            released through this path. The organisation's own investigation
            identifies the omission as contributory."

Veridia response and action:
            Accepted without qualification. This is the observation Veridia
            considers most serious of the four, notwithstanding its
            classification.
            Actions from CAPA-2025-022 through CAPA-2025-024:
            (a) A 72-hour continuous-uptime soak with an independent time
                reference added to the hotfix release path.
            (b) No hotfix ships before the investigation identifying the
                defect it addresses is complete. This is the rule that would
                have prevented 4.2.2.
            (c) Code review checklist: any change touching a time source, a
                clock read or a monotonic counter requires a second reviewer
                and an explicit statement of semantic intent.
            (d) Absolute time assertion added to the HL7 conformance suite.
            Status: CLOSED, verified at surveillance audit 2026-04. The
            auditor specifically reviewed the release records for firmware
            4.3.1 and 4.4.0 and confirmed the soak evidence was present.

NB-2025-OBS-04
Area:       Design and development / traceability
Finding:    "Instrument-side audit events are uploaded to the VeriConnect
            platform only where the instrument is running firmware 4.1.0 or
            later and where an optional setting is enabled. The setting
            defaults to disabled on upgrade. Customers purchasing the VeriGuard
            module on the basis of audit trail completeness may not be aware
            that the trail is incomplete."

Veridia response and action:
            Accepted. Actions:
            (a) DS-VG-002 revision 2 (2026-01-19) states the limitation
                explicitly under "Audit logging".
            (b) VeriConnect 6.1 surfaces a fleet-level warning where an
                enrolled instrument is not uploading audit events.
            (c) Firmware 4.4.0 (2026-04-21) changes the default so that cloud
                audit upload is enabled for any instrument in a VeriGuard
                tenant, opt-out rather than opt-in.
            Status: CLOSED, verified at surveillance audit 2026-04.

            Note: this observation was independently identified by a customer
            in June 2025 (ticket VBST-4070) before actions (b) and (c) had
            shipped. Veridia disclosed the audit observation to that customer
            at the time.

3. AREAS OF PARTICULAR STRENGTH NOTED BY THE AUDIT TEAM
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The audit report records three:

  - The root cause investigation for FSN-2025-003 (report VBI-0087) was
    described as "unusually thorough, including the identification of a
    supplier control weakness that the organisation was not obliged to
    disclose".

  - The Basel reagent QC laboratory's stability testing programme and the
    retention sample regime were commended.

  - The separation of the clinical result path (instrument to LIS) from the
    cloud platform was noted as "an architectural decision with a demonstrable
    safety benefit", with reference to the March 2025 SSO outage in which no
    result was delayed.

4. SURVEILLANCE AUDIT 2026
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Dates:        2026-04-20 to 2026-04-22
Sites:        Cambridge (remote for one day), Basel (on site)
Outcome:      Zero non-conformities. All four 2025 observations verified as
              closed. One new observation raised:

NB-2026-OBS-01
Area:       Design and development / software - beta features
Finding:    "The FHIR R4 export introduced in VeriConnect 6.2 is labelled as
            not for diagnostic use. The organisation should satisfy itself
            that the technical and procedural controls preventing diagnostic
            use are adequate, given that the output is clinically meaningful
            and is delivered through the same platform as validated outputs."

Status:     OPEN. Response due 2026-07-31. Under review by Regulatory Affairs
            and Product. Veridia's preliminary view is that a label is not by
            itself a control and that a technical measure is likely to be
            required.
