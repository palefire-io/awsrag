3. CLASSIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Under Part IV of the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (as amended), which
implements the requirements formerly derived from Directive 98/79/EC:

  VeriScan 400 and 400 Pro   General IVD (Annex III self-declaration route)
  VeriLyse reagents          General IVD (Annex III self-declaration route)

None of the devices is listed in Annex II List A or List B, and none is a
device for self-testing. Approved Body involvement is therefore not required
for this conformity assessment route.

NOTE FOR CUSTOMERS. Under the EU IVDR these devices are classified Class C and
DO require Notified Body involvement. The difference is not an inconsistency:
the UK framework currently in force derives from the earlier Directive and
uses a different classification scheme. See reg-ivdr-transition-summary.txt.
The UK is expected to introduce a revised framework aligned more closely with
international practice; Veridia monitors this and will reissue this declaration
when required.

7. UKCA MARKING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The UKCA mark is applied to the device label, the outer packaging and the
Instructions for Use. It is not accompanied by an Approved Body number, since
no Approved Body is involved in this conformity assessment route.

Devices bearing both the CE mark and the UKCA mark are placed on both markets.
The marks are applied under separate declarations; this document covers the
UKCA mark only.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. VERIPREP SP-1
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriPrep SP-1 is an accessory to an in-vitro diagnostic medical device. It
performs no measurement, produces no diagnostic result and does not itself fall
within the definition of an IVD.

It is UKCA marked under the Supply of Machinery (Safety) Regulations 2008, the
Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016 and the Electromagnetic
Compatibility Regulations 2016. A separate declaration (DOC-UKCA-SP1-002) is
available.
