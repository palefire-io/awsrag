[2025-03-11 09:24] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
1. No. This is an availability incident. The failure mode is that we are
   refusing to accept authentication assertions we cannot cryptographically
   verify. Nothing has been accessed by anyone unauthorised, no data has been
   exposed and no data has been altered or lost. If our assessment changes I
   will tell you immediately, but I do not expect it to.

2. The audit trail is complete. Every failed authentication is logged with
   reason code VC-4412 and is visible under Audit > Authentication. What you
   will see is an absence of user-activity events during the window, because
   there was no user activity - people could not log in to do anything. That
   is a gap in activity, not a gap in logging, and the two are different
   things. The advisory we issue will document the window so you can reference
   it in an evidence pack.
