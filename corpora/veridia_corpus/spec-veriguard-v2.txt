OVERVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
VeriGuard extends VeriConnect with the audit, access control, data residency
and data protection capabilities required by laboratories operating under
ISO 15189, ISO 27001, SOC 2, UK GDPR and Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

VeriGuard is not a separate application. It activates additional capability
within the customer's existing VeriConnect tenant and is licensed per tenant
per year.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CAPABILITIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. AUDIT LOGGING
   - Immutable, append-only audit trail covering authentication, authorisation
     decisions, result viewing, result authorisation and amendment, QC
     acceptance and rejection, configuration change, user lifecycle, firmware
     deployment and data export.
   - Each event records: event ID, UTC timestamp (server clock, NTP
     synchronised), actor user ID and display name, actor IP address, session
     ID, tenant ID, object type and identifier, action, outcome, and a
     before/after value pair for configuration changes.
   - Events are written to a write-once store and hash-chained. A tamper-check
     job verifies the chain daily; a break raises a P1 alert to Veridia
     operations and a banner in the customer tenant.
   - Retention 7 years. Export as a signed CSV or JSON Lines bundle with a
     detached signature and the issuing certificate chain.

   LIMITATION: audit events record what a user did in VeriConnect. Actions
   taken at the instrument keypad are recorded in the instrument's own audit
   log and are uploaded to VeriConnect only for instruments on firmware 4.1.0
   or later with cloud audit upload enabled. Sites running mixed firmware will
   have gaps. This was a finding in the 2025 Notified Body audit
   (observation NB-2025-OBS-04) and is addressed in firmware 4.4.0.

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL
   - Fixed roles: lab_technician, biomedical_scientist, lab_manager,
     qc_specialist, service_engineer, tenant_admin, auditor, dpo,
     integration_service. See lookup-vericonnect-permissions.csv.
   - Custom roles: compose any subset of permission keys. Custom role changes
     are themselves audited with before/after permission sets.
   - Separation of duties enforcement: a user holding results.authorise cannot
     simultaneously hold qc.chart.configure unless the tenant explicitly
     overrides, and the override is recorded.
   - Location scoping: roles may be scoped to a subset of sites or
     instruments within a tenant.
   - Periodic access review: scheduled report of users, roles and last login,
     with an attestation workflow for the tenant admin.

3. IDENTITY AND AUTHENTICATION
   - SAML 2.0 and OIDC single sign-on with automatic identity provider
     metadata refresh (default every 6 hours, configurable 1-24 hours since
     VeriConnect 6.2). The fixed-metadata behaviour that caused the 11 March
     2025 outage was removed in March 2025; see VBB-0044 and report-VBI-0094.
   - SCIM 2.0 user and group provisioning.
   - Break-glass local accounts, excluded from SSO enforcement, with mandatory
     TOTP and alerting on every use.
   - Session policy: idle timeout, absolute timeout, concurrent session limit,
     IP allow-listing per tenant.

4. DATA RESIDENCY
   - Tenant data may be pinned to eu-west-1 (Ireland), eu-central-1
     (Frankfurt), eu-west-2 (London) or us-east-1 (N. Virginia).
   - Pinning covers result data, QC data, audit trail and backups.
   - Support access from outside the pinned region is possible and is recorded
     as an access event; it can be blocked entirely at the cost of longer
     support resolution times.
   - Telemetry and product analytics are aggregated and do not leave the
     pinned region in identifiable form.

5. GDPR AND DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
   - Consent and lawful basis register per tenant.
   - Data subject access export: assembles all records relating to an
     identified data subject across results, audit and comments, in a portable
     format, within the tenant's configured SLA.
   - Erasure workflow honouring retention obligations: records subject to a
     statutory or accreditation retention period are marked rather than
     deleted, with the reason recorded.
   - Pseudonymisation of patient identifiers in exports and in the support
     view.
   - Records of processing activity (Article 30) template pre-populated with
     Veridia's processing.

WHAT VERIGUARD DOES NOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - It does not encrypt or protect data on the customer's own network or LIS.
  - It does not provide antivirus or endpoint protection for the analyser.
    VeriScan instruments run a locked-down embedded OS with no user-installable
    software; antivirus is neither required nor supported. See the
    manufacturer disclosure of security-related information (MDS2) available
    on request.
  - It does not make the customer compliant. It provides the technical
    controls and evidence; the laboratory remains the data controller.
