[2025-09-05 11:30] Aisha Rahman (Support Engineering L2):
8-hour DHCP lease is my prime suspect. When the analyser renews, if it gets a
different address the SP-1's cached endpoint is stale and you get P-405 until
discovery re-runs. That would give you drops clustered around multiples of 8
hours from whenever the leases were issued - which often looks random until
you plot it.

Recommendation regardless of what the log shows: put both on static IPs, or
DHCP reservations if the network team prefers. This is in the SP-1 datasheet
as a strong recommendation for exactly this reason.
