1. WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Automated sample handling equipment is routinely queried by freight forwarders
and by customer compliance functions because equipment of superficially similar
description appears in dual-use control lists. In particular, the SP-1 is a
robotic liquid handling instrument, and robotic liquid handling equipment
appears in the biological dual-use controls in some configurations.

The short answer is that the VeriPrep SP-1 is NOT controlled. This document
explains why, so that the question can be answered once rather than at every
shipment.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION FOR CLASSIFICATION PURPOSES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriPrep SP-1 is a benchtop pre-analytical sample preparation station for
clinical laboratory use. Its functions are:
  - removing caps from evacuated blood collection tubes
  - reading a barcode label on the tube
  - transferring a measured aliquot of the sample into a secondary tube
  - sorting the primary tube into an output rack
  - presenting the tube to a connected haematology analyser

It does not:
  - perform any measurement or analysis
  - contain, culture, propagate or process microorganisms
  - contain any biological agent, toxin or genetic element
  - provide containment (it is an open-bench instrument with a simple
    interlocked lid; it is not a biological safety cabinet and has no HEPA
    filtration, no directional airflow and no containment rating)
  - operate under vacuum or pressure beyond that generated by its own syringe
    pump
  - incorporate cryptographic functionality beyond standard TLS for network
    communication

3. CLASSIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
UNITED KINGDOM
  Assessed against the UK Strategic Export Control Lists, incorporating the
  retained EU dual-use list (Regulation (EU) 2021/821 as retained).

  Classification:  NOT LISTED. No rating.

  Specific entries considered and excluded:
    2B350   Chemical manufacturing equipment - not applicable; the SP-1 is not
            chemical manufacturing equipment and does not meet any of the
            sub-entries.
    2B352   Equipment capable of use in handling biological materials.
            Considered in detail because it is the entry most often raised.
            Excluded because:
            2B352.a  complete containment facilities - the SP-1 is not a
                     containment facility.
            2B352.b  fermenters - not applicable.
            2B352.c  centrifugal separators - the SP-1 contains no centrifuge.
            2B352.d  cross-flow filtration equipment - not applicable.
            2B352.e  freeze-drying equipment - not applicable.
            2B352.f  protective and containment equipment - the SP-1 provides
                     no containment as defined; the lid is an operator safety
                     interlock, not a containment barrier.
            2B352.g  aerosol inhalation chambers - not applicable.
            2B352.h  spray-drying equipment - not applicable.
            2B352.i  nucleic acid assembler/synthesiser - not applicable. The
                     SP-1 performs no synthesis of any kind.
            2B352.j  microencapsulation equipment - not applicable.
    5A002   Information security equipment - not applicable. The SP-1
            implements TLS using standard libraries for network communication
            and falls within the mass-market and ancillary cryptography
            provisions.

UNITED STATES (for re-export purposes)
  ECCN:            EAR99
  Basis:           Subject to the EAR but not listed on the Commerce Control
                   List. No specific ECCN applies.
  Note:            EAR99 items may still require a licence for embargoed
                   destinations, prohibited end-users or prohibited end-uses.

EUROPEAN UNION
  Classification:  Not listed under Regulation (EU) 2021/821 Annex I.

5. NON-EXPORT-CONTROL SHIPPING CONSIDERATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Matters that are not export control but that are commonly conflated with it
and that customers ask about in the same breath.

DANGEROUS GOODS
  None of the VeriLyse reagents is classified as dangerous goods for transport
  by road (ADR), sea (IMDG) or air (IATA). No UN number is assigned. See
  spec-verilyse-safety-data-summary-v2.txt section 7.

MATERIAL OF HUMAN ORIGIN
  VeriTrol control materials contain stabilised human erythrocytes. Each
  donation is tested and found non-reactive for HBsAg, anti-HCV, anti-HIV 1/2
  and HIV-1 RNA. Shipment as UN 3373 Biological Substance Category B is NOT
  required for stabilised, tested control material and Veridia ships it as
  non-regulated. Some importing countries nonetheless require a health
  certificate or an import permit for material of human origin; check locally.
  This has caused delays in three countries in the last two years, most
  recently Brazil and India.

TEMPERATURE-CONTROLLED SHIPMENT
  RGT-RT (reticulocyte stain) and VeriTrol control materials require 2-8 C
  storage. They are shipped with cool packs where transit exceeds 48 hours.
  This is a product quality requirement, not a dangerous goods requirement.

MEDICAL DEVICE IMPORT REQUIREMENTS
  Separate from export control. Importing countries generally require the
  device to be registered locally. Veridia holds registrations in the UK, EU
  (via the Irish authorised representative), Switzerland, and a number of other
  markets. Contact Regulatory Affairs before shipping to a market not on the
  standard list.

SANCTIONS AND RESTRICTED PARTIES
  Veridia screens all customers and shipping destinations against UK, EU, US
  and UN restricted party lists at order entry and at shipment. This applies
  irrespective of the product's non-controlled status. An EAR99 item still
  cannot be shipped to a sanctioned party.
