-----------------------------------------------------------------
INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
RGT-RT is a supravital fluorescent stain for the enumeration of reticulocytes
on the VeriScan 400 Pro. The stain binds residual ribosomal RNA in immature
erythrocytes, permitting their discrimination from mature erythrocytes by
fluorescence intensity and the derivation of the immature reticulocyte fraction
(IRF).

RGT-RT IS NOT COMPATIBLE WITH THE VERISCAN 400. The VeriScan 400 has no
reticulocyte channel and no fourth cartridge bay. An RGT-RT cartridge
presented to a VeriScan 400 will be rejected with E-302 (reagent cartridge not
recognised). Orders for RGT-RT against a site with no VeriScan 400 Pro on the
account are flagged by order processing; this is a recurring source of
confusion where a laboratory believes it operates a Pro and does not.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPOSITION (nominal, revision B)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Component                        | Concentration
---------------------------------|------------------
Polymethine fluorescent dye      | 0.09 g/L
Buffered saline carrier          | to 1 L
Stabiliser (revision B addition) | 0.15 g/L
Antimicrobial preservative       | < 0.1%
pH (25 C)                        | 7.0 +/- 0.1

Revision B adds a stabiliser and is supplied in an amber cartridge with a
revised storage statement. Revision A showed accelerated dye precipitation
above 25 C, producing a negative reticulocyte bias and E-601 calibration
failures. Revision A was withdrawn under FSN-2024-006 (2024-11-08).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
STORAGE AND STABILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Unopened                         | 2-8 C, 12 months from manufacture
                                 | DO NOT FREEZE
On board after first pierce      | 60 days
Light protection                 | Essential. Amber cartridge must not be
                                 | transferred to a clear container.
Equilibration before use         | 30 minutes at room temperature

Note the shorter unopened shelf life (12 months) and on-board life (60 days)
compared with the other VeriLyse reagents. Order accordingly; a laboratory
running fewer than approximately 15 reticulocyte counts per week will discard
stain on expiry rather than exhaust it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CALIBRATION AND QC
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The reticulocyte channel must be recalibrated after every stain lot change.
This is not optional and is enforced by the instrument from firmware 4.1.0;
the retic channel is locked out until calibration is accepted.

Run both levels of VeriTrol RET control (VT-RET-2) after calibration. Expected
recovery is within +/- 15 per cent of the assigned value at level 1 and +/- 10
per cent at level 2.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Classified under CLP:
  Skin Irrit. 2, H315
  Eye Irrit. 2, H319
Pictogram: GHS07. Signal word: Warning.

The dye stains skin and fabric. Stains on skin are harmless and fade within
several days.

Full safety data sheet: SDS-RGT-RT rev 2.
