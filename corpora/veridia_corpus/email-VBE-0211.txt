Ben,

I saw bulletin VBB-0067 this morning and I have a question that I suspect
nobody has asked you yet.

Firmware 4.4.0 puts IVDR labeling data - Basic UDI-DI and conformity route -
into the result header. We are a US site running the VeriScan 400 Pro under
Research Use Only. We do not have an IVDR conformity route. What does the
instrument put in that field for us, and does it go out on the wire to our
LIS?

I am not raising a problem. I am raising it because our LIS interface is
validated and if the message content changes we have to revalidate, and because
our compliance office will want to know why a research use only instrument is
emitting conformity assessment metadata.

Related questions while I have you:

1. Is 4.4.0 being pushed to US instruments, or is the US firmware track
   separate? Our two instruments are on 4.3.1.

2. The FHIR R4 export in VeriConnect 6.2 is flagged as beta and not for
   diagnostic use. For a research site, is "not for diagnostic use" a
   meaningful restriction or is it the same statement as the RUO labeling we
   already operate under? Our informatics group would like to use it for a
   study and I want to give them a clear answer.

3. Any update on FDA submission plans for the 400 Pro? I ask every year. I
   understand the answer may still be no. Two of my colleagues at other
   academic centers have asked me whether they should wait, and I would like to
   tell them something accurate.

For context on why this matters to us: we run the Pro for a marrow transplant
program where the reticulocyte and IRF data drive engraftment monitoring. The
instrument does what we need it to do and I have no complaints about it. But
running clinically useful measurements under RUO labeling is an
uncomfortable position and every year it continues it gets slightly more so.
