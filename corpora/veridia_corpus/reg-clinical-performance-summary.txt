Scope: Summary of the clinical performance evidence supporting the VeriScan
400 Pro. Prepared for customer evaluation, tender response and accreditation
purposes. This is a summary; the full study reports form part of the technical
documentation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. STUDY DESIGN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Design            Prospective and retrospective method comparison using
                  residual clinical specimens, conducted in accordance with
                  EN ISO 20916:2019 and CLSI EP09-A3.
Sites             5 (2 UK NHS teaching hospitals, 1 Swiss university
                  hospital, 1 German reference laboratory, 1 UK private
                  laboratory)
Period            2023-03 to 2024-09, with supplementary reticulocyte data
                  2025-02 to 2025-06 following the RGT-RT revision B change
Ethics            Residual anonymised specimens; approvals held at each site.
                  No specimen was collected for the purpose of the study.
Instruments       6 VeriScan 400 Pro, firmware 4.0.0 to 4.1.2 during the
                  study period
Reference         Differential: ICSH-standardised 400-cell manual
methods           differential by two independent morphologists, discordance
                  adjudicated by a third
                  Reticulocyte: ICSH reference method, manual new methylene
                  blue, 1000 erythrocytes counted
                  Body fluid: manual chamber count (Fuchs-Rosenthal),
                  cytospin differential
                  CBC parameters: predicate automated analyser with
                  established traceability to reference materials

3. DIFFERENTIAL - METHOD COMPARISON
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Passing-Bablok regression against the ICSH manual differential, percentages.

  Analyte   n     Slope   95% CI          Intercept  95% CI         r
  --------  ----  ------  --------------  ---------  -------------  -----
  NEUT%     2140  0.994   0.988 - 1.001   +0.31      -0.08 - +0.70  0.978
  LYMPH%    2140  1.008   1.000 - 1.016   -0.24      -0.56 - +0.09  0.981
  MONO%     2140  0.971   0.951 - 0.992   +0.44      +0.22 - +0.66  0.902
  EOS%      2140  1.012   0.995 - 1.030   -0.02      -0.09 - +0.05  0.964
  BASO%     2140  0.918   0.871 - 0.968   +0.09      +0.04 - +0.14  0.741

The basophil correlation is the weakest, which is expected and is common to
every automated platform. Basophils are present at low absolute numbers and the
manual reference method itself has poor precision at those counts. The
practical consequence is that a basophil percentage from any automated
haematology analyser, including this one, should not be relied upon in
isolation. This is stated in the Instructions for Use.

Monocyte correlation is also lower than neutrophil and lymphocyte, again
consistent with the platform class and with the known difficulty of the
monocyte/large-lymphocyte boundary.

4. FLAGGING PERFORMANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Against the manual differential as reference, for the validated flag set.

  Flag                   Sensitivity  Specificity  PPV    NPV
  ---------------------  -----------  -----------  -----  -----
  Blast/abnormal cells   93.0%        88.4%        21.6%  99.7%
  Immature granulocytes  89.8%        91.2%        39.8%  99.3%
  Atypical lymphocytes   81.4%        93.1%        28.2%  99.4%
  Nucleated RBC          96.1%        97.8%        61.9%  99.9%
  Platelet clumps        84.4%        98.1%        59.3%  99.5%

  Overall flag rate in the study population: 14.2%
  Overall flag rate observed in routine use across the installed base
  (VeriConnect telemetry, 2025): median 5.8%, interquartile range 3.5% to 9.1%

The difference between the study flag rate and the routine flag rate is a
consequence of the study population being deliberately enriched for abnormality.
Laboratories should expect a flag rate in the routine range above, varying
principally with case mix.

The low positive predictive values are inherent to screening for rare events
and are not a defect. A blast flag PPV of 21.6% means roughly four films
reviewed for each true finding. The negative predictive value of 99.7% is the
number that matters clinically.

5. RETICULOCYTE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Method comparison against the ICSH manual reference method, n = 220.

  Parameter  Slope   95% CI          Intercept  r      Range covered
  ---------  ------  --------------  ---------  -----  ----------------
  RET%       1.021   1.004 - 1.039   -0.04      0.981  0.2 - 18.4%
  RET#       1.014   0.996 - 1.033   -0.001     0.979  0.011 - 0.740
                                                        x10^12/L
  IRF        0.988   0.951 - 1.026   +0.008     0.931  0.02 - 0.61

Precision, within-run CV, n = 31 replicates:
  RET% at 0.8%     CV 8.4%
  RET% at 2.1%     CV 5.2%
  RET% at 6.7%     CV 3.1%
  RET% at 14.2%    CV 2.4%

The CV at low reticulocyte percentages is the limiting performance
characteristic. At 0.8% the manual reference method's own CV is approximately
20%, so the automated method is substantially more precise than the reference
it is compared against - a well-recognised difficulty in evaluating
reticulocyte methods.

Subgroup: post-haematopoietic stem cell transplant specimens (n = 47).
Slope 1.033, r 0.974. No systematic difference from the main population. This
subgroup was analysed separately at the request of two study sites because
engraftment monitoring is a principal use of the measurement.

6. BODY FLUID MODE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Against manual chamber count and cytospin differential, n = 410.

  Fluid type              n     WBC-BF slope  r      Notes
  ----------------------  ---   ------------  -----  ---------------------
  Cerebrospinal fluid     186   1.008         0.972  See low-count note
  Pleural                  94   0.994         0.988
  Peritoneal/ascitic       81   1.002         0.991
  Synovial                 49   0.978         0.964  Viscosity effects

CSF low-count performance, which is the clinically critical region:

  True count range   n    Sensitivity for   Mean bias
  (manual, /uL)           count >5 /uL
  -----------------  ---  ----------------  ----------
  0 - 5               94  n/a               +0.4 /uL
  6 - 20              41  95.1%             -0.9 /uL
  21 - 100            31  100%              -2.1 /uL
  >100                20  100%              -1.4%

Two specimens in the 6-20 /uL band were reported by the instrument as below 5
/uL. Both were reviewed: one had a delay to analysis of 3 hours 40 minutes
(recommended maximum 1 hour), the other showed evidence of cellular
degeneration on the cytospin. Neither is attributable to the instrument, but
both are recorded because they illustrate the dominant preanalytical risk in
body fluid counting.

MN/PMN differentiation was assessed against cytospin (n = 268 specimens with
adequate cellularity): MN% slope 0.981, r 0.943.

The body fluid mode is not validated for fluids containing visible particulate
matter, lavage specimens, or fluids with haematocrit above 5 per cent. These
exclusions are stated in the Instructions for Use.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Stated plainly because evaluators ask and because omitting them invites the
assumption that there are more.

  1. The study population, while large, contained 71 specimens with blasts.
     Flag performance for blasts rests on that number. It is adequate for the
     claim made but it is not a large number.

  2. Paediatric representation (118 specimens under 16, of which 21 under 1
     year) is limited. Neonatal specimens in particular are under-represented
     relative to their analytical difficulty.

  3. Body fluid specimens came predominantly from two of the five sites,
     reflecting where those specimens are analysed.

  4. The reticulocyte data was generated substantially on RGT-RT formulation
     revision A. The supplementary 2025 dataset (n = 96) on revision B showed
     no significant difference (slope ratio 1.004, p = 0.61), but the primary
     dataset predates the reformulation.

  5. Firmware during the study period was 4.0.0 to 4.1.2. The extended
     flagging algorithms introduced in 4.3.0 are NOT covered by this study and
     are labelled research use only for that reason.

  6. Method comparison against a manual reference method inherits the manual
     method's imprecision. For low-frequency cell types and low reticulocyte
     counts this dominates the apparent disagreement.
