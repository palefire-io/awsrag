We updated VS4P-0447 to firmware 4.3.0 over the weekend. This morning the SP-1

Rolling back the analyser is not attractive; we've already run QC on 4.3.0 and

[2025-09-29 08:00] Danny Whitcombe (Support Engineer):
This is expected behaviour and easily fixed - you need to update the SP-1 too.

VeriScan 4.3.0 uses host protocol version 2. SP-1 firmware 2.2.0 speaks
version 1. The SP-1 refuses the handoff rather than falling back, deliberately,
because a silent protocol downgrade in a sample handling path is worse than a
stop.

Update the SP-1 to 2.3.0 and you're done. It's a 20-minute update from
Service > Firmware.

This is in bulletin VBB-0055 and in the 4.3.0 bulletin VBB-0061 under
prerequisites - I'm not saying that to score a point, just so you know where
to look next time.

[2025-09-29 08:20] Jason Okonkwo (Pathology Informatics Manager):
Fair. I read VBB-0061 and I read the "requires SP-1 firmware 2.3.0" line and
did not connect it to anything because I didn't know the SP-1 had its own
firmware track. I'd assumed it was part of the analyser.

Updating now.

[2025-09-29 09:45] Jason Okonkwo (Pathology Informatics Manager):

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: VeriScan firmware 4.3.0 uses host protocol v2; SP-1 on firmware
2.2.0 speaks v1. P-407 is correct protective behaviour.
Actions: SP-1 updated to 2.3.0.
Product feedback: firmware deployment wizard should pre-flight check enrolled
SP-1 firmware compatibility.
See also: VBB-0055, VBB-0061, lookup-error-codes-veriprep-sp1.csv
