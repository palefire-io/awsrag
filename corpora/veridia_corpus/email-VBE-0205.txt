Dear Camille,

Following our conversation at Medlab, I would like a formal quotation for the
expansion of our Hamburg reference laboratory.

Requirement:
  6 x VeriScan 400 Pro with 50-position autoloader
  2 x VeriPrep SP-1 (r2)
  VeriConnect Enterprise, existing tenant, additional instrument licences
  VeriGuard - we do not have this today and would like it costed
  Installation, IQ/OQ and training for 14 staff
  5-year Platinum service contract

Volumes for consumable projection:
  CBC + 5-part differential      approximately 340,000 per annum
  Reticulocyte                   approximately 22,000 per annum
  Body fluid                     approximately 3,500 per annum

Several specific questions, some of which are commercial and some technical.

1. Throughput. Your datasheet states 80 samples/hr for CBC + DIFF and 62 for
   CBC + DIFF + RET. Our workload is approximately 6 per cent reticulocyte. Can
   your applications team model the sustained mixed throughput for us rather
   than leaving us to interpolate? We are sizing the instrument count from it
   and if we are wrong we are wrong by an instrument.

2. Advisory flags. VBB-0061 describes the extended flagging in firmware 4.3.0
   as research use only and notes a throughput penalty. Two questions: is there
   a timeline for these flags being brought into the validated set, and does
   the throughput penalty apply per sample or only to samples where a flag
   fires?

3. IVDR. What is the current status of the Class C assessment with Notified
   Body 0086? I ask each supplier this at each quotation and I record the
   answers. Your answer in September 2025 was "submitted January 2025,
   assessment in progress". If that is still the answer in February 2026 I
   would like to understand what the holdup is, and I would rather hear it
   from you than infer it.

4. Data residency. Our group policy requires German or EU hosting. eu-central-1
   Frankfurt is listed as available on Enterprise with VeriGuard. Please
   confirm this includes backups and the audit trail, and confirm the process
   and downtime for migrating our existing eu-west-1 tenant.

5. SP-1r2. We have three r1 units at Basel that have given us reliable service
   because our tube stock happens to be within the r1 band. For the new Hamburg
   units, is there any reason to prefer r1 if we could source it more cheaply,
   or is r2 now the only option? I ask because our procurement will ask.

6. Trade-in. We have two VeriScan 200 units at Hamburg approaching end of
   support. What allowance is available and until when?

Finally, a commercial point I will make directly. Synlab Nordwest has been a
Veridia customer since 2019 and we have 12 instruments. The RGT-L3 recall in
February 2025 affected us at two sites and the handling was, on the whole,
good. The firmware timestamp problem in the same quarter affected us less
because our instruments are power cycled nightly, but we noticed.

Two significant product problems in one quarter is the kind of thing a
procurement committee remembers when a competitor is in the room. I am telling
you this because I would rather Veridia won this expansion on its merits and
knew what it was up against, than lost it and wondered why.
