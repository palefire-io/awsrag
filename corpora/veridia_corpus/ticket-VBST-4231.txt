[2025-09-26 11:20] Marcus Chen (QC Applications Specialist):
Agreed, not the stain. That leaves the retic flow cell or the optics.

A retic flow cell accumulates stain deposit over time in a way the whole blood
cell doesn't, because the dye binds to surfaces as well as to cells. Rising CV
with stable mean is the classic presentation.

Try a Retic Clean cycle (Service > Maintenance > Retic Clean, 18 minutes)
followed by n=10 both levels. If that improves it materially, the cell is
fouled and may recover with a longer cleaning regime. If it improves it a
little and it drifts back, the cell needs replacing.

[2025-09-29 14:00] Priti Shah (Senior BMS):

[2025-10-02 11:30] Marcus Chen (QC Applications Specialist):
Honest answer: not automatically, no. The instrument doesn't trend QC CV and
VeriConnect charts the values but not the CV.

What would have caught it is exactly what you did, just sooner: someone
looking at the trend rather than at whether each point passed. A point can

I'm raising a product request for CV trending with an alert on a rising trend.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: reticulocyte flow cell fouled with dye deposit, producing a rising
QC CV with a stable mean. Stain ruled out by cartridge swap.
Actions: VS4-OPT-224 replaced (FSV-2025-0384); retic channel recalibrated;
Retic Clean frequency increased from monthly to fortnightly for this site's
volume.
Product feedback: QC CV trending with rising-trend alert.
