FIELD SAFETY NOTICE
=====================================================
Reference: FSN-2024-006
Date Issued: 2024-11-08
Urgency: Standard
Issued by: Veridia BioSystems Ltd., Cambridge, United Kingdom
Signatory: Sarah Lindgren, Regulatory Affairs Manager

Affected Product(s):
  VeriLyse RGT-RT Reticulocyte Stain (formulation revision A)
  Catalogue number: VL-RT-250

Affected Lot(s):
  All revision A lots (RT-2023-0001 through RT-2024-0028 inclusive)

Affected Instruments:
  VeriScan 400 Pro only (reticulocyte channel)

-----------------------------------------------------
DESCRIPTION OF ISSUE
-----------------------------------------------------
Revision A of the RGT-RT stain shows accelerated dye precipitation when stored
above 25 C for extended periods. Precipitate carried into the reticulocyte flow
cell produces a negative bias on the reticulocyte percentage and, in severe
cases, an E-601 reticulocyte channel calibration failure.

The effect was first observed at customer sites in the DACH region during the
summer of 2024 and reproduced internally under accelerated storage conditions.

-----------------------------------------------------
RISK ASSESSMENT
-----------------------------------------------------
Reticulocyte counts are used to assess erythropoietic response, most commonly in
the monitoring of anaemia treatment and post-transplant recovery. An
underestimated reticulocyte count could delay recognition of marrow recovery.
Because reticulocyte results are typically interpreted in a trend rather than
in isolation, and because affected instruments frequently fail calibration
outright rather than producing plausible-but-wrong results, the residual risk
is assessed as LOW.

Four complaints were received. No adverse patient outcome reported.

-----------------------------------------------------
ACTION REQUIRED
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Discontinue use of revision A RGT-RT stain. Revision A is identified by the
   suffix "/A" following the lot number on the vial label.
2. Order replacement revision B stock (catalogue number unchanged).
3. Recalibrate the reticulocyte channel after changeover and verify with both
   levels of retic QC material.
4. Return the enclosed reply form within 15 working days.

Revision B has been available since 2024-10-14 and is supplied in an amber vial
with a revised storage statement (2-8 C, do not freeze).
