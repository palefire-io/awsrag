INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 400 is an automated haematology analyser intended for the in-vitro
quantitative determination of haematological parameters, including a five-part
leucocyte differential, in whole blood samples collected into K2 or K3 EDTA.
The device is intended for use by trained laboratory professionals in clinical
laboratory settings. Results are intended to be used in conjunction with other
clinical and laboratory findings and are not intended to be used as the sole
basis for diagnosis.

The VeriScan 400 is not intended for use in point-of-care settings, for home
use, or for the analysis of body fluids. Body fluid analysis is available on
the VeriScan 400 Pro only.

ANALYTICAL PERFORMANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Linearity (measuring interval)
Analyte | Interval                       | r
--------|--------------------------------|-------
WBC     | 0.10 - 120.0 x10^9/L           | 0.999
RBC     | 0.05 - 8.50 x10^12/L           | 0.999
HGB     | 5 - 260 g/L                    | 0.999
PLT     | 5 - 3000 x10^9/L               | 0.998
HCT     | 0.5 - 75.0 %                   | 0.999

Repeatability (within-run CV, normal-range material, n=31)
WBC <= 2.0%   RBC <= 1.5%   HGB <= 1.0%   PLT <= 4.0%   MCV <= 1.0%

Reproducibility (between-day, 20 days, 2 runs/day, 2 replicates)
WBC <= 3.0%   RBC <= 2.0%   HGB <= 1.5%   PLT <= 5.5%

Limit of blank / limit of detection
WBC: LoB 0.02, LoD 0.05 x10^9/L
PLT: LoB 2, LoD 4 x10^9/L

Known interferences: lipaemia above 1000 mg/dL triglycerides affects HGB;
cold agglutinins affect RBC/MCV; nucleated RBC above 5 per 100 WBC produce a
positive WBC bias. Flagging is provided for each. See IFU section 12.

REAGENT COMPATIBILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Required:
  RGT-D5  VeriLyse Standard Diluent      500 mL cartridge   VL-D5-500
  RGT-L3  VeriLyse Lyse Reagent          500 mL cartridge   VL-L3-500
  RGT-CL  VeriLyse Cleaning Solution     250 mL cartridge   VL-CL-250

RGT-L3 formulation revisions
  Revision C: supplied to 2025-04. Compatible. Note that lots L3-2025-0440
    to L3-2025-0460 were recalled under FSN-2025-003 and must not be used.
  Revision D: supplied from lot L3-2025-0471 (2025-03-24). Compatible.
    No instrument setting, calibration or QC target change is required at
    changeover. See VBB-0047.
  Revisions C and D may be used on the same instrument at different times but
  must not be mixed within a single cartridge bay or decanted between
  cartridges.

Not compatible:
  RGT-RT  VeriLyse Reticulocyte Stain - VeriScan 400 Pro only. The cartridge
          bay will reject an RGT-RT cartridge with E-302.
  RGT-D5X VeriLyse Extended Diluent - evaluation product, VeriScan 400 Pro
          on firmware 4.3.0 or later only.

Cartridge shelf life: 18 months unopened. 90 days on board after first pierce.
Third-party reagents are not supported and will not be recognised (E-302).
Full matrix: lookup-reagent-compatibility.csv

-----------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CONTROL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Three levels of VeriTrol control material (low, normal, high) are supported.
Westgard rules 1-3s, 2-2s and R-4s are applied by default; the rule set is
configurable from VeriConnect Professional tier and above. Levey-Jennings
charting is available on the instrument and in VeriConnect.

Recommended QC frequency: both normal and abnormal levels once per 24 hours of
operation and after each calibration, reagent lot change or firmware update.
See SOP-VBS-001.
