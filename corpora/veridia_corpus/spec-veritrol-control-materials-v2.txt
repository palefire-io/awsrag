COMPOSITION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Stabilised human erythrocytes, stabilised avian erythrocytes (as a leucocyte
surrogate for the differential channels), platelet-sized particles and a
haemoglobin-containing matrix, in a buffered preservative medium.

CONTAINS MATERIAL OF HUMAN ORIGIN. Each donation has been tested and found
non-reactive for HBsAg, anti-HCV, anti-HIV 1/2 and HIV-1 RNA. No test method
can offer complete assurance that products derived from human blood will not
transmit infection. Handle as potentially infectious using universal
precautions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
ASSIGNED VALUES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Assigned values and acceptable ranges are lot specific and are supplied on the
assay sheet in the carton and as a downloadable file from the customer portal
(format: .vqc, importable from VeriConnect or from a USB stick at the
instrument).

Values are assigned on VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400 Pro instruments. The two
models have separate value columns on the assay sheet. USING THE WRONG COLUMN
IS A COMMON CAUSE OF APPARENT QC FAILURE AT MIXED-FLEET SITES - the
differential channel values differ materially between the halogen/absorbance
optics of the 400 and the laser/fluorescence optics of the Pro.

Ranges are expressed as target +/- 2SD or as a range; the instrument applies
Westgard rules 1-3s, 2-2s and R-4s by default.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
STORAGE AND STABILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Unopened, 2-8 C            | To the expiry date on the vial (typically 90-120
                           | days from manufacture)
Open vial, 2-8 C           | 14 days for VT-CTRL; 7 days for VT-RET; 24 hours
                           | for VT-BF
Do not freeze              | Yes
Equilibration before use   | 15 minutes at room temperature, then mix by
                           | gentle inversion for 30 seconds. Do not vortex.
                           | Do not mix mechanically.

Inadequate mixing is by a large margin the most common cause of an out-of-range
QC result on this platform. A vial that has stood overnight requires the full
30 seconds of inversion; a rushed 5-second mix typically produces a low RBC and
a low HCT with a normal HGB, which is a recognisable signature.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
FREQUENCY AND WHEN TO RUN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum: two levels every 24 hours of operation.
Additionally, run QC after:
  - any calibration
  - any reagent lot change (see SOP-VBS-002)
  - any firmware update (see SOP-VBS-001 section 9)
  - replacement of any fluidic or optical component
  - relocation of the instrument
  - any period of more than 7 days without use

-----------------------------------------------------------------
LOT CROSSOVER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
When moving to a new control lot, run the new lot alongside the current lot for
at least 5 consecutive days (10 runs) before switching. Establish the
laboratory's own mean and SD from a minimum of 20 data points rather than
relying on the assay sheet SD, which is derived from a multi-instrument
consensus and is generally wider than a single instrument achieves.

VeriConnect Professional tier and above supports parallel lot running and will
compute the new lot statistics automatically (Quality > Control Lots >
Crossover).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
ORDERING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Control material is supplied on a scheduled standing order by default, timed
to arrive 3 weeks before the current lot expires. Contact your account manager
to adjust the schedule. Ad hoc orders carry a 5 working day lead time and are
shipped at 2-8 C.
