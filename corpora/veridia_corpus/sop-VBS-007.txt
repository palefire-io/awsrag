1. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Shipping preparation, delivery acceptance, installation qualification (IQ) and
operational qualification (OQ) for VeriScan 400, VeriScan 400 Pro and
VeriPrep SP-1.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. PRE-DELIVERY SITE SURVEY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Complete the site survey before the instrument ships. A survey completed
     after delivery is a survey completed too late; the commonest consequence
     is an instrument sitting in a corridor for a fortnight.

2.2  Confirm:
     [ ] Bench dimensions and load rating. VeriScan 480 x 520 mm, 42-46 kg.
         SP-1 860 x 640 mm, 118 kg - a surprising number of laboratory
         benches are not rated for the SP-1.
     [ ] Clearance: 150 mm rear, 300 mm above for the SP-1 lid.
     [ ] Mains socket within 2 m, correctly earthed.
     [ ] Ambient temperature achievable within 18-32 C (analysers) or
         18-30 C (SP-1) throughout the working day, including in summer.
         Ask specifically about west-facing windows and about whether the air
         handling is switched off overnight and at weekends.
     [ ] Network drop available; static IP or DHCP reservation agreed with
         the trust's IT.
     [ ] Firewall rules: TCP 443 outbound to *.vericonnect.veridiabio.com,
         TCP 6661 to the LIS host, UDP 123 to an NTP server.
     [ ] Access route: lift dimensions, door widths, thresholds.
     [ ] Waste and water arrangements.
     [ ] Reagent storage space at 2-25 C, and 2-8 C for RGT-RT.

2.3  Record the survey and attach it to the installation job.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. SHIPPING PREPARATION (FROM VERIDIA OR BETWEEN SITES)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Remove all reagent cartridges. Cap the fluidic ports with the transit
     caps supplied.

3.2  Flush the fluid path with purified water and then drain. An instrument
     shipped with reagent in the lines will arrive with crystalline deposit at
     the fittings.

3.3  Fit the transit brackets to the rotor and the probe arm. These are in the
     accessory box; if they have been lost, do not improvise, order
     replacements.

3.4  Photograph the instrument before packing.

3.5  Use the original packaging where available. Where it is not, use a
     purpose-built flight case; general-purpose pallet wrap over a bare
     instrument is not acceptable and voids the transit warranty.

3.6  Fit a shock indicator to the outer packaging.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. DELIVERY ACCEPTANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Inspect the shock indicator before signing. If triggered, note it on the
     carrier's paperwork before signing and photograph it.

4.2  Inspect the packaging for crush, puncture and water damage.

4.3  Do not unpack the instrument until an engineer is present unless the
     laboratory has been trained to do so.

4.4  A recurring cause of E-311 (cartridge seal breach) reports shortly after
     an install is reagent shipped separately and roughly handled in transit.
     Inspect reagent cartons on arrival and reject any that are crushed.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. INSTALLATION QUALIFICATION (IQ)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Serial number matches the delivery note and the sales order.
[ ] Model matches the order. Check VS4-100 against VS4-200P explicitly -
    the two are visually similar and a mis-shipped model has reached a
    customer bench more than once. Confirm the platform string under
    Settings > About, not just the badge.
[ ] Accessory box contents checked against the packing list.
[ ] Instrument sited per the survey; ambient measured at the instrument and
    recorded.
[ ] Mains supply verified.
[ ] Network connectivity verified; IP address recorded.
[ ] NTP reachable and time synchronised.
[ ] Firmware version recorded; updated to current if agreed with the site.
[ ] VeriConnect enrolment completed and instrument visible in the fleet view.
[ ] LIS interface configured; test message sent and acknowledged.
[ ] Documentation handed over: IFU, spec sheet at current revision, safety
    data summary, quick reference card.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. OPERATIONAL QUALIFICATION (OQ)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Reagents fitted, primed, lot numbers recorded and checked against active
    field safety notices.
[ ] Startup sequence completed without error.
[ ] Background counts within SOP-VBS-001 Appendix A limits.
[ ] Optical calibration passed.
[ ] Calibration with VeriCal material completed and accepted.
[ ] QC: all levels in use, both within range.
[ ] Precision: 10 replicates of a normal specimen, CV within specification.
[ ] Carryover: high specimen followed by three blanks, < 0.5%.
[ ] Linearity verification with VT-LIN-6 where the laboratory requires it.
[ ] Reticulocyte channel calibration and QC (Pro, where retic is in use).
[ ] Body fluid mode background and QC (Pro, where body fluid is in use).
[ ] SP-1 integration verified where applicable: 20-tube run, correct barcode
    read, correct handoff, correct sorting. Confirm host protocol versions are
    compatible (P-407).
[ ] Aliquot volume verification per SOP-VBS-009 where the SP-1 aliquots.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. TRAINING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  One day for the VeriScan 400; two days for the 400 Pro; one day for the
     SP-1. Record attendee names and roles.

7.2  Cover as a minimum: daily QC (SOP-VBS-001), reagent changeover
     (SOP-VBS-002), error code lookup and first-line response, how to raise a
     support ticket and what information to include, and where to find field
     safety notices.

7.3  Leave the site with at least two staff able to perform a reagent
     changeover unsupervised.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. HANDBACK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  IQ/OQ report signed by the engineer and by the laboratory.
8.2  Copy filed with Veridia and with the customer.
8.3  Warranty start date recorded.
8.4  Account record updated: instruments, serial numbers, firmware,
    VeriConnect tier.
