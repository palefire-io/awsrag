-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
To verify that the VeriPrep SP-1 delivers aliquot volumes within the specified
tolerance of +/- 3 per cent or +/- 10 uL, whichever is greater, with a
precision (CV) of 2 per cent or better at 500 uL.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHEN TO PERFORM
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - At installation, as part of OQ.
  - At each annual preventive maintenance visit.
  - After replacement of the syringe, syringe seal or aliquot needle.
  - On investigation of P-201 (aliquot volume out of tolerance).
  - Where downstream analysers report systematically low or high results on
    aliquoted samples but correct results on primary tubes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. EQUIPMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Analytical balance, readability 0.1 mg, calibrated within 12 months,
    with a valid calibration certificate.
  - Draught shield.
  - Purified water at ambient temperature, equilibrated in the room for at
    least 1 hour.
  - Calibrated thermometer and barometer (or the room's environmental
    monitoring record).
  - Secondary tubes of the type in routine use.
  - Disposable tips of the type in routine use (SP1-TIP-1000 or SP1-TIP-200).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROCEDURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Record ambient temperature, relative humidity and barometric pressure.

4.2  Determine the density of water at the recorded temperature (Z factor).
     Use the table at Appendix A.

4.3  Run a Syringe Prime (Service > Maintenance > Syringe Prime) and confirm
     no air is visible in the syringe or the line. Air in the syringe is by
     far the most common cause of a failed verification and is not a fault in
     itself; prime again.

4.4  Configure a run of 10 aliquots at the target volume into pre-weighed
     secondary tubes.

4.5  Weigh each tube before and after. Record to 0.1 mg.

4.6  Compute delivered volume: V = (m_after - m_before) x Z.

4.7  Compute mean, standard deviation and CV.

4.8  Repeat at 200 uL, 500 uL and 1000 uL, or at the volumes in routine use if
     different.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. ACCEPTANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Target      Mean must be within        CV must be
  ----------  -------------------------  ----------
  200 uL      194.0 - 206.0 uL           <= 3.0%
  500 uL      485.0 - 515.0 uL           <= 2.0%
  1000 uL     970.0 - 1030.0 uL          <= 2.0%

If the mean is out of tolerance but the CV is acceptable, the fault is
calibration or a syringe seal. If the CV is out of tolerance, the fault is
usually air, a worn seal, or tip seating.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. ACTION ON FAILURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Repeat the prime and the verification once.

6.2  Inspect the tip cone O-ring and the syringe seal. Replace if worn.

6.3  Check that hypochlorite has never been used in the fluid path. Degraded
     seals from hypochlorite exposure present exactly as a slowly worsening
     aliquot CV. See SOP-VBS-003 section 3.4.

6.4  If failure persists, adjust the volume calibration factor
     (Service > Calibration > Aliquot) and repeat the full verification. Do
     not adjust the factor by more than 5 per cent without investigating the
     mechanical cause.

6.5  Escalate to Veridia if the required adjustment exceeds 5 per cent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
APPENDIX A - Z FACTORS (uL/mg) FOR PURIFIED WATER AT 101.3 kPa
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  18 C  1.0025      22 C  1.0033      26 C  1.0043
  19 C  1.0027      23 C  1.0035      27 C  1.0046
  20 C  1.0029      24 C  1.0038      28 C  1.0048
  21 C  1.0031      25 C  1.0040      29 C  1.0051
