--- Resolution ---
Root cause: RGT-L3 lot L3-2025-0444 (formulation revision C) developing
elevated baseline absorbance at 535 nm during on-board use. Not an instrument
fault.

charge; FSN-2025-003 issued 2025-02-14; investigation VBI-0087.
