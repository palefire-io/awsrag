Dear Sir or Madam,

North Cumbria and Ayrshire Unified Health Partnership is undertaking a
replacement programme for haematology analysers across four laboratory sites.
We expect to go to formal tender in Q1 2026 and are currently gathering
information to inform the specification.

I would be grateful for the following:

1. Current product specification sheets for the VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400
   Pro, at the latest revision. Please confirm the revision number and date, as
   we have been sent superseded documents by other suppliers and it has caused
   confusion.

2. Indicative pricing for:
   - 4 x VeriScan 400 Pro with 50-position autoloader
   - 2 x VeriScan 400
   - 2 x VeriPrep SP-1
   - VeriConnect at Professional tier, 8 instruments
   - Five-year consumable projection at 180,000 CBC and 9,000 reticulocyte
     counts per annum across the group
   - Five-year service contract at Gold and at Platinum for comparison

3. Your current position on IVDR conformity for both analyser models. We need
   to know whether the devices will be CE marked under IVDR at the point of
   contract award, and if not, what the transitional position is and what
   assurance you can give.

4. UKCA marking status.

5. Confirmation of throughput figures under realistic conditions. I will be
   direct: we have been burned before by throughput figures quoted from
   open-tube bench testing. Please quote sustained closed-tube throughput with
   the autoloader running, and say so explicitly.

6. Details of any known issues, field safety notices or field safety corrective
   actions affecting either model in the last 36 months. We ask every supplier
   this and we do check. A supplier who discloses openly scores better with our
   evaluation panel than one who does not and is later found out.

7. Reference sites in the UK, ideally an NHS trust of comparable size running a
   mixed 400 and 400 Pro fleet with VeriPrep integration.

8. Your data protection position for VeriConnect, including hosting location
   and whether an NHS-standard DPA is available.

Our timetable is:
  Information gathering        to 30 November 2025
  Specification drafting       December 2025
  Tender issue                 February 2026
  Award                        June 2026
  Installation                 September to December 2026

I should say that we are not currently a Veridia customer and have no
incumbent advantage in play here.
