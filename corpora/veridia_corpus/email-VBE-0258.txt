Tom,

I have been collecting these since January and there are now enough that I want
to put them in front of you as one thing rather than as a trickle.

Thirty-one customers have asked, in some form, for VeriConnect to annotate
Levey-Jennings charts with the events that change the process being charted.
Specifically:

  reagent lot change        27 requests
  firmware update           24 requests
  calibration               22 requests
  control lot change        19 requests
  instrument service visit  11 requests
  operator                   4 requests

The firmware number jumped from 6 to 24 between February and April, for obvious
reasons.

The argument customers make, and I think it is right, is this. A QC chart is a
picture of a process. When the chart shows a discontinuity, the first question
is always "what changed" and the answer is always somewhere else - in a paper
lot changeover record, in a service report, in a bulletin. The chart shows you
that something happened and gives you no way to find out what.

Representative quotes, anonymised:

  "We spent two days establishing that a shift in our platelet mean coincided
  with a reagent lot change, which the system already knew about." - Enterprise
  customer, 12 instruments

  "During the firmware timestamp business I had three charts with points in the
  wrong order and no way to show my assessor why. A vertical line saying
  'firmware 4.2.1 applied' would have saved me a fortnight." - Professional
  customer

  "Every other QC package I have used in twenty years does this." - Enterprise
  customer, and I have checked, and they are right

What I would ask for, in priority order:

1. Vertical annotation lines on the L-J chart for reagent lot change, firmware
   update, calibration and control lot change, with a hover or click showing
   the detail. All four events are already in the data.

2. The ability to add a manual annotation with free text. Sites want to record
   "moved instrument", "new operator started", "air conditioning failed".

3. Annotation visible in exported reports and PDFs, not just on screen. This is
   the one that matters for accreditation and it is the one that will get
   missed if we only do the interactive chart.

I am aware you have a roadmap and I am not asking you to reorder it on my say
so. But I would note that this is the single most requested VeriConnect feature
I have seen in four years in this role, that the data already exists, and that
the two product problems we have had this year would both have been easier for
customers to live with if it had existed.

Elena is copied because she asked me to keep her sighted on what customers are
asking for after the recall.
