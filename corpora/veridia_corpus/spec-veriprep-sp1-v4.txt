INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriPrep SP-1 is an automated pre-analytical sample preparation station
intended to decap, identify by barcode, aliquot and sort evacuated blood
collection tubes prior to analysis. It is intended for use with the VeriScan
400 and VeriScan 400 Pro haematology analysers and with third-party analysers
supporting the documented host protocols.

The SP-1 does not measure any analyte and does not produce a diagnostic result.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
FUNCTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Decapping     Automated cap removal and disposal for screw and push caps
  Identification Barcode read of primary tube label (1D and 2D symbologies)
  Aliquoting    Volumetric transfer into secondary tubes or plates
  Sorting       Routing of primary tubes to destination racks by rule
  Handoff       Direct presentation to a connected VeriScan analyser

-----------------------------------------------------------------
HARDWARE REVISIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
SP-1r1  Serial prefix SP1-R1-. Shipped 2021 to Q2 2025.
SP-1r2  Serial prefix SP1-R2-. Shipping from Q3 2025.

The r2 revision introduces a redesigned tube feed mechanism (SP1-MEC-141), a
repositioned and widened barcode scanner window, revised gripper jaw material
and a relocated lid interlock. The functional specification is otherwise
identical. See VBB-0055 for validation data and VBB-0038 for the background to
the change.

KNOWN LIMITATION (r1 only): 5 mL tubes with a short-profile cap, giving an
overall capped height below approximately 75.5 mm, sit low in the carrier.
This produces barcode read failures (P-101/P-103), tube height errors (P-105)
and, in a minority of presentations, a feed jam (P-112). Mitigations are the
SP1-ADP-05 tube adapter, a raised label position, or retrofit to the r2 feed
mechanism (SP1-KIT-R2UP). This limitation does not apply to r2 units.

INTEGRATION WITH VERISCAN ANALYSERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Two physical options:

  Serial (RS-232)
    Cable: SP1-CBL-232, 3 m shielded, DB9 female to DB9 female
    Settings: 115200 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit, no flow control
    Maximum cable length 15 m
    Suitable for a single analyser directly adjacent to the SP-1

  LAN (Ethernet)
    100BASE-TX, static IP strongly recommended
    Analyser and SP-1 must be on the same subnet or have a route with UDP
    discovery relayed
    Supports up to 4 analysers from one SP-1
    DHCP is supported but a lease change on the analyser will produce P-405.
    Use a DHCP reservation if static addressing is not permitted locally.

Host protocol versions
    v1  SP-1 firmware 2.0.0 - 2.2.x with VeriScan firmware up to 4.2.3
    v2  SP-1 firmware 2.3.0+ with VeriScan firmware 4.3.0+
    Mixing v1 and v2 across the link raises P-407 and the SP-1 will not hand
    off samples. Plan analyser and SP-1 firmware updates together.

Handoff sequence (v2, summarised)
    1. SP-1 reads primary tube barcode
    2. SP-1 queries analyser for pending order matching the accession
    3. Analyser responds with test profile or NO_ORDER
    4. On NO_ORDER the SP-1 routes the tube to the exception rack
    5. On order match the SP-1 decaps and presents the tube to the analyser
       aspiration position, or aliquots to a secondary tube as configured
    6. Analyser acknowledges aspiration complete; SP-1 sorts the primary tube

Full protocol documentation: DS-SP1-PROTO-002, available under NDA.
