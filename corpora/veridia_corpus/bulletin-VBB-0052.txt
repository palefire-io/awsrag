VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - TECHNICAL BULLETIN
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0052
Date Issued: 2025-04-11
Type: Specification Revision
Affected Product: VeriScan 400, VeriScan 400 Pro (all serial numbers)
Supersedes: operating temperature statement in DS-VS400-002 (spec sheet v2)
            and DS-VS400P-002
Superseded by: nothing. This bulletin is current.
Incorporated into: DS-VS400-003 (spec sheet v3), IFU revision 7

SUBJECT: Revised ambient operating temperature range - 18 C to 32 C

-----------------------------------------------------
THE CHANGE
-----------------------------------------------------
  Previous published range:  15 C to 35 C
  Revised published range:   18 C to 32 C
  Relative humidity:         unchanged, 20% to 80% non-condensing
  Storage/transport:         unchanged, -10 C to 50 C

This bulletin narrows the published operating range. It does not describe a
defect and no instrument is being modified or recalled.

-----------------------------------------------------
WHY
-----------------------------------------------------
The original 15-35 C range was established from environmental chamber testing
on pre-production units at steady state. Field data gathered from VeriConnect
fleet telemetry between 2024-06 and 2025-02, covering 411 instruments, shows
that performance at the extremes of the original range is less robust than the
chamber data indicated, principally because real laboratories do not hold
steady state - they cycle.

Two effects were quantified:

  Below 18 C:  reagent viscosity increases, aspiration volumes trend low, and
               the platelet channel shows a small negative bias (mean -2.8 per
               cent at 16 C against the 22 C reference). Below approximately
               16 C, E-101 aspiration pressure errors become more frequent.

  Above 32 C:  the reticulocyte flow cell heater on VeriScan 400 Pro loses
               headroom to regulate, producing E-607 errors. On both models
               the optical bench baseline drifts, and lyse reagent on-board
               degradation accelerates, shortening effective on-board life
               below the labelled 90 days.

Neither effect produces a result that is silently wrong across the whole
measuring range. The affected channels flag or error in the majority of cases.
However, the narrowed range better reflects the conditions under which Veridia
can support the full performance claims.

-----------------------------------------------------
RELATIONSHIP TO THE W-031 WARNING
-----------------------------------------------------
The W-031 ambient temperature warning was introduced in firmware 4.1.2 with
thresholds set to the then-current 15-35 C range. Firmware 4.1.2 through 4.3.1
therefore warn later than the revised specification requires.

  Firmware 4.1.2 - 4.3.1:  W-031 raised outside 15-35 C
  Firmware 4.4.0 onwards:  W-031 raised outside 18-32 C

Until an instrument is updated to 4.4.0, laboratories operating near the
boundary should monitor ambient temperature independently. VeriConnect
Professional tier and above records ambient temperature with each run and can
chart it under Reports > Environment.

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT CUSTOMERS SHOULD DO
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Check the ambient temperature at the instrument, not at the room
   thermostat. Instruments sited against a south-facing window, above a
   centrifuge, or in a small side room with poor circulation commonly run
   3-6 C above the room reading.

2. Where the environment cannot be brought within 18-32 C, contact support
   before assuming results are affected. In many cases the excursion is brief
   and outside testing hours.

3. Update your local IQ/OQ and environmental monitoring records to reference
   18-32 C. Document sheet DS-VS400-003 and IFU revision 7 carry the revised
   figure. If your quality system holds a controlled copy of DS-VS400-002,
   supersede it.

4. Accreditation bodies have in some cases queried the change. Veridia can
   supply a supporting statement for ISO 15189 assessors on request.

-----------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON EXISTING DOCUMENTATION
-----------------------------------------------------
Spec sheet DS-VS400-002 (v2), which remains in circulation and is still present
on some customer intranets, states 15-35 C. That figure is superseded by this
bulletin. Where a customer contract, tender response or validation report cites
15-35 C, Veridia will honour the original figure for the purposes of that
document but recommends operating to the revised range.
