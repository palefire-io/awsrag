[2025-10-08 13:15] Dr Stefan Ruettimann (Leitender Arzt):
During our internal access review we found that eleven users hold the
lab_manager role who should hold biomedical_scientist. They can therefore edit
our Westgard rule configuration.

Nobody has done so - I have checked the audit trail. But it should not have
been possible.

I would like to understand how this happened, because we did not do it
manually.

[2025-10-08 16:40] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Investigating. Your tenant uses SCIM provisioning with group-to-role mapping
from your identity provider, so my first suspicion is the mapping rather than
anything in VeriConnect.

Can you tell me the IdP group membership of one of the eleven?

[2025-10-09 08:30] Dr Stefan Ruettimann (Leitender Arzt):
Example user is in groups: HAEM-LAB-STAFF, HAEM-SENIOR, USB-ALL-STAFF.

[2025-10-09 11:15] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Found it, and it is a configuration problem rather than a defect, though the
configuration was easy to get wrong.

Your role mapping has:
  HAEM-LAB-STAFF   -> lab_technician
  HAEM-SENIOR      -> lab_manager
  USB-ALL-STAFF    -> (no mapping)

When a user matches more than one mapping, VeriConnect grants the union of the
mapped roles and effectively the highest privilege wins. Your eleven users are
in HAEM-SENIOR, which was created by your IT team in June for a mailing list
purpose and happens to contain your senior BMS staff - who are senior, but are
not laboratory managers.

So the mapping is doing what it was told. What it was told does not match what
you meant.

[2025-10-09 13:20] Dr Stefan Ruettimann (Leitender Arzt):
That is our error then, at least in part. Though I would say that "highest
privilege wins" on multiple matches is a surprising default and is not stated
anywhere I looked.

How do we fix it, and how do we stop it happening again?

[2025-10-09 15:00] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Agreed on the default - it is documented in the API reference and nowhere a
human would look. I'm raising that.

To fix:
1. Change the HAEM-SENIOR mapping to biomedical_scientist. Or better, ask your
   IT team for a purpose-built group (HAEM-LAB-MANAGERS) and map that to
   lab_manager, leaving HAEM-SENIOR unmapped. Mapping a group that exists for
   another purpose will bite you again.
2. Re-run SCIM sync. The eleven users will drop to the correct role at their
   next sync, within an hour.
3. Run an access review afterwards to confirm.

To prevent recurrence:
- Enable the VeriGuard periodic access review with attestation, six-monthly.
  You found this manually, which is good, but it should not depend on someone
  deciding to look.
- Consider separation-of-duties enforcement, which is on by default in
  VeriGuard but can be overridden. Let me check whether yours is overridden.

[2025-10-09 16:10] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Checked - separation of duties is NOT overridden on your tenant, which is why
none of the eleven could both authorise results and configure QC charts
simultaneously. That control did its job even though the role assignment was
wrong.

[2025-10-13 09:45] Dr Stefan Ruettimann (Leitender Arzt):
IT created HAEM-LAB-MANAGERS with four members. Mapping updated. SCIM synced.
All eleven now show biomedical_scientist. Access review scheduled six-monthly
with attestation enabled.

I am satisfied. Please do pursue the documentation point - the union-of-roles
behaviour should be in the administrator documentation, not the API reference.

[2025-10-14 10:00] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Raised. SOP-VBS-004 will carry a worked example of group mapping in its next
revision, including the multiple-match behaviour.

Closing. Thank you for finding this yourselves - the access review that caught
it is exactly the control we recommend and most sites don't run.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: SCIM group-to-role mapping pointed at an IdP group created for an
unrelated purpose; multiple-match behaviour grants the union of mapped roles,
which is documented only in the API reference.
Actions: purpose-built IdP group created and mapped; SCIM re-synced; periodic
access review with attestation enabled.
Product feedback: document multiple-match role resolution in administrator
documentation and SOP-VBS-004.
