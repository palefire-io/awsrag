[2025-08-12 15:00] Owen Trask (Support Engineer L1):
W-031 is a warning rather than an error, so nothing is being blocked, but it's
worth taking seriously because it means the instrument is measuring an ambient
outside the range it's specified for.

Your firmware is 4.3.0, which raises W-031 outside 15-35 C. Note that the
published operating range was narrowed to 18-32 C in April (bulletin VBB-0052)
but the firmware warning thresholds don't catch up until 4.4.0. So if you're
seeing W-031 on 4.3.0, you are outside 15-35, which is well outside the range
you should actually be operating in.

Can you check the temperature at the instrument - on top of the chassis, not
the room thermostat - at a few points through the day?

[2025-08-13 16:20] Karen Dalgleish (Laboratory Manager):
09:00 - 27 C
12:00 - 33 C
15:00 - 36.5 C
17:00 - 34 C

Room thermostat reads 26 C at 15:00.

It's been a hot fortnight. Is this just the weather?

[2025-08-14 09:15] Owen Trask (Support Engineer L1):
Partly the weather, but 36.5 C at the instrument against 26 C in the room is a
10 degree difference, and that's not weather - that's siting or airflow.

I'd like Rob Ellery to have a look. He's in the North East on the 19th.

In the meantime: the practical risks at that temperature are accelerated
reagent degradation on board (your L3 cartridge will not reach its labelled 90
days), optical baseline drift, and increased E-101 aspiration errors. Your QC
is passing, which is reassuring, but I'd watch the L-J charts closely for the
rest of the hot spell.

[2025-08-19 15:30] Rob Ellery (Field Service Engineer):
Attended (FSV-2025-0341).

Measurements at 14:40: 35.9 C top of chassis, 34.2 C rear vent, 25.8 C room
thermostat.

Cause: the instrument sits in an alcove approximately 700 mm deep with walls
on three sides, rear clearance 50 mm against a required 150 mm. The
instrument's own exhaust is recirculating. There is no local extraction and
the alcove is on the opposite side of the room from the air handling outlet.

Instrument itself: no fault. Optics, fluidics, calibration all normal. QC in
range.

This is a building services problem, not an instrument problem. I have written
it up for the trust's estates department with the measurements and a
recommendation for either local extraction or relocation, and left a copy of
VBB-0052 with Karen.

Told Karen plainly that Veridia cannot support the full performance
specification at 36 C and that this needs fixing rather than monitoring.

Time on site 1.5 hours.

[2025-09-30 11:00] Karen Dalgleish (Laboratory Manager):
Update - estates have moved us. Instrument is now on an open bench with proper
clearance. Temperature at the instrument 22-25 C through the day. No W-031
since the move.

Rob's written report was what got estates to act. Thank you for putting it in
writing.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: instrument sited in a three-sided alcove with 50 mm rear clearance
causing exhaust recirculation; ambient at the instrument reaching 36.5 C
against 25.8 C room. No instrument fault (FSV-2025-0341).
Actions: written report to the trust's estates department; instrument
relocated to an open bench; ambient now 22-25 C.
