--- Resolution ---
Root cause: two contributing causes - NTP server decommissioned in a trust
network change, and RTC battery at 2.41 V (nominal 3.0 V) causing fast clock
drift.
Actions: NTP reconfigured to ntp.lothian.scot.nhs.uk; RTC battery VS4-ELE-090
replaced (FSV-2025-0401).
NOT related to the firmware 4.2.1 timestamp defect (VBI-0091) - different
mechanism, instrument-side warning only.
