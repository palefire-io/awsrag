[2025-08-06 09:20] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):
The VeriConnect fleet dashboard has become very slow. Loading takes 25-40
seconds where it used to be 2-3. The results search is also slow but less so.

This has been getting worse over perhaps two months. All users, all our sites,
different networks, different browsers. So it is not local to us.

[2025-08-06 11:45] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
Bernd - thank you, and the detail that it's across networks and browsers saves
me from asking.

Looking at your tenant now. Initial observation: your fleet dashboard is
querying 12 instruments with full scattergram upload enabled and a 400-day
default date range. That's a large query and it's got larger as your data has
accumulated, which fits the gradual worsening.

Give me a day to look properly rather than guess.

[2025-08-07 14:30] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
Confirmed. Two things are happening.

1. Your fleet dashboard default date range is 400 days. Most tenants run 30 or
   90. At 400 days across 12 instruments with scattergrams, the query returns
   about 340 MB before aggregation. That is the 25-40 seconds.

2. Separately, there is a genuine inefficiency on our side: the dashboard
   aggregation query doesn't use the instrument index when the date range
   exceeds 180 days, so it falls back to a scan. That's our bug and it's why
   the degradation is non-linear - under 180 days you'd not have noticed.

What I can do today: change your dashboard default to 90 days. You can still
select longer ranges when you need them; they'll just be slow until we fix (2).

What we'll do: the query fix is going into the next VeriConnect release. I'll
give you a version number when I have one rather than a date.

[2025-08-07 15:10] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):
Please change the default to 90 days. We rarely need more and when we do we
can wait.

I would like to know why the default was 400 days in the first place.

[2025-08-07 16:00] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
Changed, and it should be immediate.

On your question: 400 days was set on your tenant during a migration in 2023
so that a historical data verification could be done, and it was never changed
back. That's a housekeeping failure on our side. I've checked the other
tenants that went through the same migration - four others have the same
setting, and I'm contacting them.

[2025-08-07 16:30] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):

[2025-09-04 10:00] Yusuf Demir (Cloud Operations):
Follow-up: the aggregation query fix shipped in VeriConnect 6.1.2 last week.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: (a) tenant fleet dashboard default date range left at 400 days
after a 2023 migration; (b) dashboard aggregation query not using the
instrument index above a 180-day range, falling back to a table scan.
Actions: tenant default reset to 90 days; four other tenants with the same
migration artefact identified and contacted; query fix shipped in VeriConnect
6.1.2.
