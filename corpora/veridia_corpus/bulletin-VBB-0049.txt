=====================================================
VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - END OF LIFE NOTIFICATION
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0049
Date Issued: 2025-04-08
Type: End of Life / End of Support Notification
Affected Product: VeriScan 200 Benchtop Haematology Analyser
Model numbers: VS2-100, VS2-100T
Serial ranges: VS2-0001 to VS2-1876 (all units)

SUBJECT: End of life for VeriScan 200 - key dates and migration options

-----------------------------------------------------
KEY DATES
-----------------------------------------------------
  2025-06-30   Last order date for new VeriScan 200 instruments.
  2025-12-31   End of sale for VeriScan 200 consumable configurations
               specific to the platform. RGT-D5 revision B production ends.
  2026-12-31   End of software and firmware support. No further firmware
               releases for the 3.7.x line after this date.
  2027-12-31   End of service. Last date on which Veridia will accept a
               service contract renewal or perform a chargeable repair.
  2028-12-31   End of spare parts availability, subject to stock.

VeriScan 200 has been in the field since 2017. Approximately 340 units remain
in active service.

-----------------------------------------------------
REASON
-----------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 200 optical bench uses a photodiode array that the component
manufacturer discontinued in 2023. Veridia holds a last-time-buy inventory
sufficient to support the service commitments above but cannot extend them.

Separately, the VeriScan 200 is a legacy device under the IVD Directive. It will
not be transitioned to IVDR conformity. From the applicable IVDR transition
deadline the device may no longer be placed on the EU market, although devices
already placed on the market and put into service may continue in use subject
to the conditions in Article 110. Customers in the EU should discuss this with
their own regulatory function; Veridia's regulatory affairs team can provide
supporting documentation. Refer to reg-ivdr-transition-summary.txt.

-----------------------------------------------------
CONSUMABLE SUPPORT
-----------------------------------------------------
RGT-D5 revision B is the diluent qualified for VeriScan 200. Revision C, which
is shipped for VeriScan 400 and 400 Pro, is backward compatible with VeriScan
200 running firmware 3.4.0 or later, and will be supplied against VeriScan 200
orders after revision B production ends.

RGT-L3 revision D is NOT validated on VeriScan 200 and must not be used on that
platform. Remaining VeriScan 200 sites requiring lyse reagent after 2025-12-31
should contact their account manager; a platform-specific fill will be
maintained to 2027-12-31.

Refer to lookup-reagent-compatibility.csv for the current compatibility matrix.

-----------------------------------------------------
MIGRATION OPTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------
1. VeriScan 400. Direct functional successor. Same 5-part differential, higher
   throughput (60 vs 40 samples/hr), IVDR Class C conformity in progress.
   Trade-in allowance available until 2026-12-31.

2. VeriScan 400 Pro. Adds reticulocyte counting, body fluid mode and extended
   flagging. Recommended where the VeriScan 200 is currently supplemented by a
   manual reticulocyte method.

3. Where a VeriScan 200 is used as a backup or overflow analyser, sites should
   note that VeriConnect will continue to accept results from VeriScan 200
   instruments until end of software support, but the instrument will not
   appear in the fleet firmware management view.

Contact your account manager for trade-in pricing and migration planning.
