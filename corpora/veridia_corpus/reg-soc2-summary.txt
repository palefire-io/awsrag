3. EXCEPTIONS NOTED
-----------------------------------------------------------------

EXCEPTION 1 (CC6.1 - Logical access)
Description:  For 3 of 40 sampled privileged access grants to production, the
              documented approval was recorded after the access was granted
              rather than before. In all 3 cases the access was subsequently
              approved by an appropriate authority, the activity performed
              under the access was reviewed and found appropriate, and the
              access was revoked within the expected window.
Management
response:     Accepted. All 3 occurred during the incident of 11 March 2025,
              when engineers were granted production access to remediate the
              SSO outage and the approval workflow was completed
              retrospectively. The break-glass access procedure permitted
              this but did not require the approval to be recorded within a
              defined window.
              Remediation: break-glass production access now generates an
              approval request automatically at the point of grant, requiring
              completion within 4 hours, with escalation to the CSO
              thereafter. Implemented 2025-06-30.

EXCEPTION 2 (CC8.1 - Change management)
Description:  For 1 of 25 sampled production changes, the change record did
              not evidence completion of the required pre-deployment testing.
Management
response:     Accepted. The change was a configuration change to a tenant's
              dashboard default date range performed during the investigation
              of a customer performance issue. The change was low risk,
              correct and beneficial, but it was made as an operational action
              and was recorded as a change afterwards without the testing
              evidence the change process requires.
              Remediation: tenant configuration changes performed by
              operations are now made through a distinct workflow with its own
              (lighter) evidence requirements, rather than being retrofitted
              into the software change process where they do not fit.
              Implemented 2025-11-14.

Neither exception resulted in unauthorised access to, loss of, or alteration of
customer data.

5. WHAT IS NOT IN SCOPE, AND WHY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Customers frequently ask why Processing Integrity and Privacy are excluded.

PROCESSING INTEGRITY is not in scope because the processing that matters
clinically - the computation of analyte values from optical and impedance
measurements - happens on the instrument, not in VeriConnect. The instrument's
processing integrity is assured through the medical device conformity
assessment route (IVDR / UK MDR), which is a substantially more rigorous
regime than the Trust Services Criteria for this purpose. Including Processing
Integrity in the SOC 2 scope would imply that the SOC 2 report speaks to result
accuracy, which it would not.

PRIVACY is not in scope because Veridia acts as processor rather than
controller for patient data, and the Privacy criteria are framed around a
controller's obligations. Customers requiring assurance on data protection
should refer to the data processing agreement (reg-gdpr-dpa-template.txt), the
ISO 27001 certification, and where applicable the NHS Data Security and
Protection Toolkit submission.

We would rather explain this than expand the scope to something that looks
more comprehensive and means less.
