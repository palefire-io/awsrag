Dear Mr Okafor,

I am escalating to you because I have run out of patience with the process
rather than with any individual.

Since December 2024 our VeriPrep SP-1 (SP1-R1-0219) has jammed 87 times. I have
the log, it is attached, and I have kept it because your Danny Whitcombe asked
me to in January and told me it would be the evidence I needed. I have done
what was asked.

In that time we have:
- bought a set of tube adapters, which helped for about three months
- had an engineer visit in January who replaced the gripper jaws
- been told repeatedly that the underlying problem is a tolerance interaction
  between the r1 feed mechanism and the tubes our trust procurement has us
  contracted to until December
- been told a hardware revision is coming "in Q3"

What we have not had is a resolution. We are now back to three or four jams a
day. Each one takes a scientist away from the bench for ten minutes and puts a
sample at risk of being mishandled during recovery.

I want to be fair to your people. Danny has been responsive and honest. Rob
Ellery has been out and did good work. Nobody has fobbed me off. But the
cumulative effect of a series of reasonable individual responses is that six
months have passed and my laboratory is still fighting a machine every day.

What I would like:

1. A date for the r2 retrofit being available, not a quarter.

2. A serious conversation about who pays for it. Veridia's position, as I
   understand it, is that our tubes are outside the specification. My position
   is that a specification that excludes a large fraction of the 5mL EDTA tubes
   on the European market is a specification that was drawn too narrowly, and
   that the redesign you are shipping is an admission of exactly that. If the
   r1 tolerance band were adequate you would not be widening it.

3. In the meantime, some acknowledgement that this is costing us. I have not
   costed it and I am not going to, because I would rather have the machine
   working than have an argument about money.

I would rather resolve this with you than through procurement, but I should
tell you that our contract manager has started asking questions and I am
running out of ways to answer them.
