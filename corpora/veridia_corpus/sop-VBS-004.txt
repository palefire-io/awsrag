-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
To define how users are created, assigned roles, reviewed and removed in a
VeriConnect tenant, and how role-based access control is configured.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Applies to all VeriConnect tenants on all tiers. Sections 5 (SCIM), 7
     (custom roles) and 10 (residency) apply to Enterprise tier with
     VeriGuard only.

2.2  This SOP is written for the customer's tenant administrator. Veridia
     staff accounts within a customer tenant are governed by Veridia's own
     internal access policy (IT-POL-004), not by this document.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRINCIPLES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Least privilege. Assign the narrowest role that permits the person to do
     their job.

3.2  Named accounts only. Shared or generic logins (for example a single
     "nightshift" account) are prohibited. They defeat the audit trail, they
     are a finding under SOC 2 and ISO 15189, and they were specifically
     raised as observation NB-2025-OBS-02 in the 2025 Notified Body audit.

3.3  Separation of duties. A user who authorises results should not also
     configure the QC rules against which those results are judged. VeriGuard
     enforces this by default and records any override.

3.4  Access follows employment. A leaver's access is removed on their last
     working day, not at the next review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. ROLES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  The fixed roles and their permission keys are listed in
     lookup-vericonnect-permissions.csv. Summary:

     lab_technician         View results, add comments, enter QC.
     biomedical_scientist   Authorise and amend results, review QC.
     lab_manager            Edit Westgard rules, invite users, export reports,
                            view the fleet dashboard.
     qc_specialist          Configure QC charts and targets, run lot
                            crossover studies.
     service_engineer       Remote diagnostics; firmware deployment on
                            Enterprise. Intended for Veridia FSE accounts.
     tenant_admin           User management, SSO configuration, API keys,
                            residency.
     auditor                Read-only access to the full audit trail.
                            VeriGuard required.
     dpo                    Data subject access and erasure workflows.
                            VeriGuard required.
     integration_service    Service account for HL7/FHIR. No interactive
                            login.

4.2  Do not assign tenant_admin as a convenience. A tenant should have two to
     four tenant administrators. More than four is a finding at most audits.

4.3  service_engineer should normally be held only by Veridia field service
     accounts. Where a customer's own engineering staff require it, scope it
     to specific instruments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROVISIONING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Local accounts (all tiers)
     a) Administration > Users > Invite.
     b) Enter the work email address. Personal email addresses must not be
        used.
     c) Select the role and, where applicable, the site or instrument scope.
     d) The invitee receives an email valid for 72 hours.
     e) TOTP multi-factor authentication is mandatory for local accounts and
        is set up at first login. It cannot be disabled per user.

5.2  SSO (Enterprise tier)
     a) Users authenticate against the customer's identity provider.
     b) Role assignment is either mapped from an IdP group claim or set
        locally in VeriConnect. Group mapping is preferred; it keeps joiners
        and leavers in one system.
     c) Configure group-to-role mapping under Administration > SSO > Role
        Mapping.

5.3  SCIM provisioning (Enterprise tier)
     a) Enables automatic creation, update and deactivation of accounts from
        the identity provider.
     b) Endpoint and bearer token are generated under Administration > SSO >
        SCIM. Rotate the token annually.
     c) With SCIM enabled, do not create users manually; manually created
        accounts will not be deactivated by the leaver process and are the
        commonest source of orphaned access.

5.4  Service accounts
     a) Created under Administration > API > Service Accounts.
     b) Assign integration_service and nothing else.
     c) Credentials are shown once. Store them in the organisation's secret
        management system, not in a spreadsheet or a middleware config file
        in plain text.
     d) Rotate at least annually and on any suspected exposure.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. SSO CONFIGURATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Supported: SAML 2.0 and OIDC.

6.2  VeriConnect fetches and refreshes the identity provider metadata
     automatically. The default refresh interval is 6 hours; it is
     configurable from 1 to 24 hours in VeriConnect 6.2 and later
     (Administration > SSO > Metadata Refresh).

     Historical note: before March 2025 the metadata was fetched once at
     tenant onboarding and cached indefinitely. A scheduled certificate
     rotation at the identity provider on 11 March 2025 therefore broke
     authentication for 17 tenants for just under four hours. See
     report-VBI-0094 and VBB-0044. Automatic refresh removes this failure
     mode but does not remove the need for break-glass accounts (section 8).

6.3  Where the customer controls the identity provider, notify Veridia of any
     major IdP migration at least 5 working days in advance. Routine
     certificate rotation no longer requires notification.

6.4  Test SSO with a non-privileged account before enforcing it tenant-wide.

6.5  Do not enable "SSO only" enforcement until break-glass accounts have been
     created, tested and stored (section 8).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. CUSTOM ROLES (ENTERPRISE + VERIGUARD)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Compose custom roles from individual permission keys under
     Administration > Roles > New.

7.2  Every custom role change is audited with a before and after permission
     set. Do not edit a role in place to grant a temporary elevation; create a
     time-limited assignment instead.

7.3  Keep the number of custom roles small. Tenants with more than about a
     dozen custom roles consistently end up unable to explain who can do what,
     which is the outcome RBAC exists to prevent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. BREAK-GLASS ACCOUNTS (REQUIRED FOR SSO TENANTS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Every tenant using SSO MUST maintain at least two local administrator
     accounts excluded from SSO enforcement.

8.2  Requirements:
     a) Named to individuals, not generic. "breakglass1" is not acceptable;
        the account must be attributable.
     b) TOTP enrolled.
     c) Credentials held in the organisation's password vault, accessible to
        the on-call laboratory manager out of hours.
     d) Tested at least every 6 months. Record the test.
     e) Every use raises an alert to the tenant's security contact and is
        recorded in the audit trail.

8.3  Break-glass accounts are for loss of the identity provider. They are not
     for day-to-day administration and must not be used for routine work.

8.4  During the 11 March 2025 outage, tenants with tested break-glass accounts
     restored administrative access within minutes. Tenants without them were
     dependent on Veridia's own remediation timeline.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. PERIODIC ACCESS REVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Review all accounts and role assignments at least every 6 months, and
     within 5 working days of any organisational change affecting the
     laboratory.

9.2  VeriGuard tenants: Administration > Access Review generates the report
     (user, role, scope, last login, created date, created by) and provides an
     attestation workflow.

9.3  Non-VeriGuard tenants: export the user list and review manually.

9.4  Act on:
     - accounts with no login in 90 days;
     - accounts created outside the SCIM flow where SCIM is enabled;
     - users holding tenant_admin who are not on the approved administrator
       list;
     - service accounts whose credentials are more than 12 months old.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. DEPROVISIONING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 On a leaver's last working day, deactivate the account. Do not delete it.
     Deletion breaks the attribution of historical audit and result
     authorisation records.

10.2 With SCIM, deactivation flows from the identity provider automatically.
     Verify it has occurred.

10.3 Where the leaver held a service account credential, rotate that
     credential.

10.4 Where the leaver was one of the two break-glass account holders, create
     and test a replacement before the account is deactivated.
