VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - PRODUCT CHANGE NOTIFICATION
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0047
Date Issued: 2025-03-31
Type: Product Change Notification (no safety impact)
Affected Product: VeriLyse RGT-L3 Lyse Reagent (VL-L3-500)
Effective from lot: L3-2025-0471
Classification of change: Minor - no change to intended use, no change to
                          performance claims, no revalidation required at
                          customer sites.

SUBJECT: RGT-L3 formulation revision C to revision D

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS CHANGING
-----------------------------------------------------
The buffering system in RGT-L3 has been reformulated. Revision D replaces the
previous organic buffer with a dual-buffer system that is substantially less
sensitive to trace metal ion contamination in the incoming raw material stream.

The change follows the investigation into FSN-2025-003 (see VBI-0087), in which
a trace contaminant in a single raw material campaign was found to catalyse a
slow oxidation of the buffering agent, producing a chromophore absorbing near
535 nm.

Revision D is identified by:
  - Lot numbers from L3-2025-0471 onwards
  - The suffix "/D" following the lot number on the cartridge label
  - A revised cartridge label with a blue rather than grey identification band

-----------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS NOT CHANGING
-----------------------------------------------------
  - Catalogue number (VL-L3-500)
  - Fill volume (500 mL)
  - Storage conditions (2-25 C, do not freeze)
  - Unopened shelf life (18 months from manufacture)
  - On-board stability after opening (90 days)
  - Intended use and instrument compatibility for VeriScan 400 and 400 Pro
  - Instrument settings, calibration factors or QC target values

No customer action is required at changeover beyond the normal lot changeover
procedure in SOP-VBS-002.

-----------------------------------------------------
PERFORMANCE COMPARISON
-----------------------------------------------------
Method comparison of revision C (unaffected lot L3-2025-0433) against revision D
(lot L3-2025-0471) on a VeriScan 400, n = 180 residual clinical specimens:

  Analyte   Slope   Intercept    r        Bias at medical decision point
  --------  ------  -----------  -------  ------------------------------
  WBC       0.998   +0.02        0.9991   +0.4% at 4.0 x10^9/L
  RBC       1.001   -0.01        0.9994   +0.1% at 4.5 x10^12/L
  HGB       0.999   +0.3 g/L     0.9989   +0.2% at 100 g/L
  PLT       1.003   -1.4         0.9976   -0.6% at 150 x10^9/L
  NEUT%     0.997   +0.18        0.9962   within total allowable error
  LYMPH%    1.004   -0.21        0.9958   within total allowable error

Accelerated stability at 35 C for 12 weeks showed a baseline absorbance shift of
0.004 AU for revision D against 0.061 AU for the affected revision C lots.

-----------------------------------------------------
TRANSITION ARRANGEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------
Unaffected revision C stock (lots L3-2025-0421, L3-2025-0433 and earlier) may
continue to be used until its labelled expiry. Revision C and revision D
cartridges may be used on the same instrument, though not concurrently, and no
crossover study is required.

Distribution of revision D began 2025-04-07. All shipments from 2025-05-01 are
revision D.
