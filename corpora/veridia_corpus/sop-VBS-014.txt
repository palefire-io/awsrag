1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
To define how Veridia receives, records, investigates and closes customer
complaints, and how a complaint is assessed for reportability to competent
authorities.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEFINITIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Complaint       Any written, electronic or oral communication alleging
                deficiency in the identity, quality, durability, reliability,
                usability, safety or performance of a device after release for
                distribution. A support ticket is not automatically a
                complaint; a support ticket alleging that the device did not
                perform as intended is.

Incident        An event that has led, might have led, or might lead to the
                death of a patient or user, or to a serious deterioration in
                their state of health.

Field Safety    Corrective action taken by a manufacturer for technical or
Corrective      medical reasons to prevent or reduce the risk of an incident.
Action (FSCA)

Field Safety    The communication sent to customers about an FSCA.
Notice (FSN)

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IS AND IS NOT A COMPLAINT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
IS a complaint:
  - "The WBC count is reading high on this instrument."
  - "The reagent cartridge leaked."
  - "Results reached the LIS with the wrong time on them."
  - "The instrument jammed and crushed a sample tube."
  - "The IFU says 15 to 35 degrees but the bulletin says 18 to 32; which is
    right?" - a documentation discrepancy affecting use IS a complaint.

Is NOT a complaint:
  - A request for training.
  - A request for a quotation.
  - A feature request.
  - A question answered by the IFU where the device performed correctly.
  - A network fault at the customer site with no device deficiency.

If in doubt, record it as a complaint. Under-recording is a finding; over-
recording is merely noise.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. INTAKE AND RECORDING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  All complaints are recorded in the complaint register within 1 working
     day of receipt, whatever channel they arrive on - ticket, email, phone,
     an engineer's observation on site, or a comment at a user meeting.

4.2  Minimum record: date received, channel, customer and account ID, product,
     model, serial or lot number, firmware version, complainant name and role,
     description in the complainant's own words, whether patient results were
     affected, and whether any harm is alleged.

4.3  An engineer who observes a device deficiency on site must record it even
     if the customer has not complained. Several of the earliest signals in
     the FSN-2025-003 sequence were engineer observations that were written in
     visit reports and not carried into the complaint register.

4.4  Link the complaint to the originating ticket number and to any related
     complaints.

4.5  COMPLAINT CLUSTERING REVIEW. Support reviews the register weekly for
     clusters: the same symptom across more than two accounts, or the same lot
     or firmware version appearing in more than two complaints, within a
     rolling 30 days. A cluster is escalated to QA and Regulatory the same
     week regardless of the severity of the individual complaints.

     This control exists because the individual RGT-L3 complaints in January
     2025 each looked minor and locally explicable. The pattern was only
     visible in aggregate.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRIAGE AND TIMELINE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Initial assessment within 1 working day: is harm alleged, and are patient
     results potentially affected?

5.2  If harm is alleged or a death or serious deterioration is reported:
     escalate IMMEDIATELY to the Regulatory Affairs Manager. Do not wait for
     the technical investigation. Reporting clocks under IVDR Article 82 and
     the UK MDR run from the date Veridia becomes aware, which is today, not
     the date the investigation concludes.

5.3  Technical investigation opened within 3 working days.

5.4  Health hazard evaluation, where a potential safety issue is identified:
     within 5 working days of the trigger. (Revision 1 stated 15 working days.
     The interval actually achieved during FSN-2025-003 was 15 days, and that
     was 15 days during which affected product remained in use.)

5.5  Complaint closure target: 30 calendar days. Complaints open beyond 60
     days are reported to the management review.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. INVESTIGATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Investigations are conducted by the function best placed to do so:
     support for use and configuration issues, field service for hardware,
     R&D reagents for reagent performance, firmware for software.

6.2  Where product is returned, follow the returned goods procedure. Preserve
     the sample as received; do not run it, clean it or disassemble it before
     photographing and logging.

6.3  Where a root cause is established, the investigation report is issued
     under a VBI number and the complaint record references it.

6.4  Where no root cause is established, say so explicitly. "Cannot reproduce"
     is a legitimate outcome and must be recorded as such, with a statement of
     what was tried. It must not be recorded as "no fault found" if the
     customer's observation was credible and simply could not be reproduced -
     the two are different and the distinction matters when a pattern emerges
     later.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. REPORTABILITY ASSESSMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Regulatory Affairs assesses every complaint involving a potential
     incident against the reporting criteria of IVDR Article 82 (EU), the UK
     MDR and Swissmedic requirements.

7.2  Decision and rationale are recorded whether or not the complaint is
     reportable. A decision not to report requires the same rigour as a
     decision to report.

7.3  Reporting timelines (indicative, confirm current requirements before
     relying on these):
       Serious public health threat        immediately, not later than 2 days
       Death or unanticipated serious      not later than 10 days
       deterioration
       Other reportable incidents          not later than 15 days
       Trend reports                       as specified in the technical
                                           documentation

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. FIELD SAFETY CORRECTIVE ACTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Where an FSCA is decided, Regulatory Affairs drafts the FSN and notifies
     the competent authorities of every member state and territory in which
     affected product was distributed.

8.2  The FSN template is at QA-TMP-011. It must state clearly which products
     and lots are affected AND which are not - the FSN-2025-003 experience
     showed that customers with mixed fleets could not readily tell whether
     their VeriScan 400 Pro instruments were in scope, and the support team
     fielded a large volume of avoidable questions as a result.

8.3  Distribution list is generated from the shipping records, not from the
     account list. Product transferred between customer sites must be traced;
     the FSN instructs recipients to forward it onward.

8.4  Reply form returns are tracked. Non-responders are chased at 10 working
     days and at 20 working days, and escalated to the account manager
     thereafter. Effectiveness of the FSCA is assessed on the response rate
     and on the proportion of affected stock recovered.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. RECORDS AND REVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Complaint records are retained for the lifetime of the device plus 10
     years.

9.2  The complaint register is an input to the post-market surveillance plan,
     the periodic safety update report and management review.

9.3  Trends are reviewed quarterly by the CSO, the RA Manager and the VP
     Customer Support.
