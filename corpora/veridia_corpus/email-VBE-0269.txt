Hi,

Two feature requests, both arising from real situations we have had this year.
Feel free to pass to whoever owns the roadmap.

1. QC CV TRENDING WITH ALERTING

VeriConnect charts QC values. It does not chart QC precision.

We have eight analysers. Twice this year we have had a channel degrade slowly -
the mean stayed put, every individual point passed, and the CV crept up over
weeks. Both times we caught it because somebody happened to look at a run of
numbers and thought "these are more scattered than they used to be". That is
not a control, it is luck.

What I would like: a rolling CV per analyte per level per instrument, charted,
with an alert when it rises by more than a configurable amount over a
configurable window. Something like "20-point rolling CV has increased by more
than 50 per cent over 60 days".

This would have caught our retic flow cell fouling months earlier and it would
have caught a platelet channel issue at our Northern General site in March.

2. REAGENT EXPOSURE BY PATIENT EPISODE

I know the reagent lot exposure report is coming and I know it lists samples by
lot. What I would also like is the inverse: given a patient identifier, tell me
every reagent lot and instrument that has touched their samples.

The use case is a query from a clinician. "You reported a white cell count of
14 on my patient in January and I do not believe it." Today, answering that
means finding the sample, finding the instrument, finding what was on board
that day, and checking it against the notices. It takes half an hour and it is
the kind of question that arrives on a Friday afternoon.

Both of these are things the system already knows. Neither needs new data
collection.

I would also say, since Marcus is copied and has been good to us this year,
that Veridia has been noticeably better than our other diagnostics suppliers at
actually responding to this kind of thing. The QC chart annotation that turned
up in 6.2 is something I asked for in 2024 and I did not expect it to happen.
