OVERVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
VeriConnect is a cloud platform for the management of results, quality control
and instrument fleets generated by Veridia analysers. It is licensed per
instrument per year in three tiers, with VeriGuard available as an add-on to
the Enterprise tier (and, from release 6.1, to Professional tier by exception).

VeriConnect is not a laboratory information system and does not replace one. It
sits between the analyser and the customer's LIS, or alongside it.

VeriConnect is a component of the device's data path but is not itself a
measuring function. Result values are computed on the instrument.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - FHIR R4 export is a beta feature and is NOT validated for diagnostic use.
  - The QC chart x-axis uses OBR-22 (result reported time) from the instrument.
    Instruments running firmware 4.2.1 or 4.2.2 supplied drifting timestamps
    and their historical charts may show misordered points. See VBB-0041.
  - Scattergram upload is disabled by default on Essentials tier.
  - VeriScan 200 instruments report results to VeriConnect but do not appear
    in the fleet firmware management view (see VBB-0049).
  - API v1 is deprecated and will be withdrawn 2027-04-30.
