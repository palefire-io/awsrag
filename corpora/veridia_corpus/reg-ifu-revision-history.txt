Scope: Revision history of the Instructions for Use for the VeriScan 400 and
VeriScan 400 Pro (IFU-VS400, common document covering both models), and for
the VeriLyse reagent range.

Purpose: laboratories are required to hold the current IFU and, in many quality
systems, to record when a revision was received and what changed. This document
supports that. It is not a substitute for the IFU itself.

IFU-VS400 - VERISCAN 400 AND 400 PRO
-----------------------------------------------------------------

REVISION 8   Issued 2026-04-21   Current
  Reason:    Firmware 4.4.0 release; IVDR labelling data
  Sections changed:
    s.3 (Symbols)        Basic UDI-DI symbol added
    s.7 (Installation)   Ambient range enforcement note updated - W-031 now
                         raised outside 18-32 C from firmware 4.4.0
    s.11 (Results)       New subsection on IVDR labelling data in the result
                         header
    s.13 (Maintenance)   Reagent lot exposure export described
    s.18 (Error codes)   No change to codes; applies_to column corrected for
                         E-215 (400 Pro only, previously shown as Both)
  Note: the E-215 correction was a documentation error present since revision
  4. E-215 has never been raisable on a VeriScan 400, which has no laser. No
  customer was affected but the error had propagated into two customer SOPs
  that we know of.

REVISION 7   Issued 2025-05-22   Superseded
  Reason:    Operating temperature range revision (VBB-0052); firmware 4.2.3
  Sections changed:
    s.4 (Specifications) Ambient operating range 15-35 C changed to 18-32 C
    s.7 (Installation)   Siting guidance expanded - measure at the instrument,
                         not the room thermostat; examples of siting that
                         causes local heating
    s.11 (Results)       Note added on HL7 timestamp fields and the firmware
                         4.2.1 defect, with reference to VBB-0041
    s.12 (Quality control) Requirement for full QC after a firmware update
                         made explicit
    s.14 (Reagents)      RGT-L3 formulation revision C/D note added
  This is the revision that carries the temperature change. Laboratories
  holding revision 6 or earlier hold the superseded 15-35 C figure.

DISTRIBUTION AND CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. The current IFU is supplied with each instrument and each reagent shipment,
   and is available from the customer portal at www.veridiabio.com/ifu.

2. Revised IFUs are distributed to registered customer contacts by email and
   are announced in VeriConnect under Notifications > Documents.

3. Where a revision changes a specification or an instruction with operational
   effect - revisions 7 and 8 of IFU-VS400, revisions 5 and 6 of IFU-RGT -
   Veridia also issues a bulletin. A bulletin without an IFU revision is
   advisory; an IFU revision is a change to the controlled instructions.

4. Laboratories should:
   - hold the current revision;
   - withdraw superseded revisions from circulation, including copies on
     intranets and in local SOPs;
   - record receipt and review of each revision;
   - assess whether local SOPs referencing the IFU require amendment.

5. KNOWN ISSUE WITH SUPERSEDED DOCUMENTS. Veridia is aware that superseded
   revisions remain in circulation at a number of sites, most significantly
   IFU-VS400 revision 6 and datasheet DS-VS400-002, both of which carry the
   15-35 C ambient range. Several support tickets in 2025 originated from
   customers working to the superseded figure. Where a customer contract,
   tender response or validation report cites the superseded figure, Veridia
   will honour it for the purposes of that document but recommends operating
   to the current range.
