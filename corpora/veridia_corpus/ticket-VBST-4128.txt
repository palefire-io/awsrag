[2025-07-08 15:40] Sean McAllister (Senior BMS):
Quick question rather than a fault. We've got an L3 cartridge that's been on
board 88 days and the instrument is warning us. If we take it off and put it
back on next week does the 90 days restart?

Also we have two unopened cartridges that expire in September, can we get an
extension if they're stored properly?

[2025-07-08 16:20] Owen Trask (Support Engineer L1):
Hi Sean, both good questions and both answers are no, but here's why.

1. The 90-day on-board clock starts at first pierce and does not stop. Taking
   the cartridge off doesn't pause it - the reagent has been exposed to air
   and to the septum puncture and continues to degrade whether it's on the
   instrument or on a shelf. The instrument tracks it from the pierce
   timestamp on the RFID tag, so it will still show 88 days when you put it
   back and it will hit E-304 on day 90 regardless.

2. Unopened shelf life extensions aren't something we grant. The 18-month
   figure comes from real-time stability data and there's no mechanism for
   case-by-case extension.

Practical advice: 88 days is close enough that I'd change it now rather than
run into an E-304 mid-batch. And for the September stock, if you can't use it
by then, tell your account manager - sometimes we can swap short-dated stock
for longer-dated at another site that will get through it.

[2025-07-08 16:35] Sean McAllister (Senior BMS):
Grand, that's clear. Didn't know about the swap option, I'll ring Callum.

One more - the instrument shows a countdown now which it didn't used to. Is
that new?

[2025-07-09 08:50] Owen Trask (Support Engineer L1):
It came in with firmware 4.3.0 - reagent on-board countdown, and the low
shelf-life warning W-035 moved from 3 days to 7. You must have been updated
recently.

Full list of what's in 4.3.0 is in bulletin VBB-0061 if you want it.
