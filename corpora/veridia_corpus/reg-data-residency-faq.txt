Q1. WHERE IS MY DATA HELD?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
By default, in Amazon Web Services region eu-west-1, which is Ireland.

Customers on Enterprise tier with the VeriGuard add-on may pin their tenant to:
  eu-west-1     Ireland (default)
  eu-central-1  Frankfurt, Germany
  eu-west-2     London, United Kingdom
  us-east-1     Northern Virginia, United States

Pinning covers result data, quality control data, the audit trail, and backups.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q2. WE ARE AN NHS TRUST. DOES OUR DATA LEAVE THE UK?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
On the default configuration, yes - it is held in Ireland.

Transfers from the UK to Ireland are transfers to an EEA country covered by
the UK's adequacy regulations for the EEA. They are lawful and require no
additional safeguard.

If your information governance position requires UK hosting, eu-west-2 (London)
is available on Enterprise tier with VeriGuard. A number of NHS trusts use the
default Irish region and a smaller number have moved to London. Both positions
are defensible; which is right for you is a question for your IG function, not
for us.

We would note that Veridia BioSystems Ltd is itself a UK company, so support
access to your tenant is from the UK regardless of where the data is stored.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q3. WHAT ABOUT SWITZERLAND?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Swiss customers' data is held in eu-west-1 by default, or in one of the pinned
regions above. There is no Swiss region.

Transfers from Switzerland to the EEA and to the UK are covered by the Swiss
Federal Council's adequacy decisions. Transfers to the United States require
additional safeguards and we do not recommend a US-pinned tenant for a Swiss
customer.

Veridia BioSystems Schweiz GmbH in Basel is our Swiss establishment and our
Swiss Authorised Representative for regulatory purposes. It is not a data
hosting location.

Q4. WE ARE IN FRANCE AND OUR REGULATOR REQUIRES HDS-CERTIFIED HOSTING.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Veridia does not hold Hebergeur de Donnees de Sante (HDS) certification, and
the VeriConnect deployment is not within the scope of an HDS certification.

AWS holds HDS certification for certain services in eu-west-3 (Paris).
VeriConnect is not deployed there.

Whether HDS applies to your use of VeriConnect depends on your configuration
and on a legal question we are not qualified to answer. If your instruments
report directly to your own LIS and VeriConnect holds a parallel copy for
quality control purposes, the analysis may differ from a configuration in which
VeriConnect is in the primary result path.

Two things that may help:
  - The pseudonymisation option (Q7) materially changes what VeriConnect holds
    and may change the analysis.
  - We will speak directly to your DPO and legal counsel.

We would rather tell you plainly that we cannot meet an HDS requirement today
than let you discover it during an inspection. Three French customers have
raised this since 2024 and the accumulated weight of that is on our internal
agenda.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q5. WE ARE IN GERMANY. IS FRANKFURT HOSTING ENOUGH?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) hosting is available on Enterprise tier with
VeriGuard and covers result data, QC data, audit trail and backups.

German customers frequently ask about the additional requirements arising from
sectoral rules and from the position of the data protection authorities on US
cloud providers. Points to note:

  - The infrastructure provider is Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, a Luxembourg
    entity, contracted under EU law.
  - AWS's ultimate parent is a US company. Several German authorities have
    expressed views on the implications. This is a live question across the
    industry and is not specific to Veridia.
  - We can provide our transfer impact assessment, the AWS data processing
    addendum, and details of the technical measures (encryption at rest with
    customer-managed keys on Enterprise tier) that bear on it.

Your DPO will need to form a view. We will support the assessment with whatever
documentation you need.

Q6. CAN VERIDIA STAFF SEE OUR PATIENT DATA?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
In principle yes, and in practice rarely, under controls.

  - Support access to a customer tenant requires a request, an approval, and a
    stated reason. It is time-limited, defaulting to 8 hours.
  - Every access is recorded in your audit trail as an access event
    attributable to a named Veridia individual. You can see it. VeriGuard
    customers can alert on it.
  - Support staff see a pseudonymised view by default. Direct patient
    identifiers are masked unless the support case requires them and the
    unmasking is separately approved and logged.
  - You may block support access from outside your pinned region entirely, or
    block it altogether. Both increase support resolution times, and blocking
    it altogether means some classes of problem cannot be diagnosed remotely.

Q7. CAN WE AVOID SENDING PATIENT IDENTIFIERS TO VERICONNECT AT ALL?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, and more customers should consider it than do.

Instruments can be configured not to transmit direct patient identifiers to
VeriConnect. Results are then held against the specimen accession number only.
Your LIS continues to receive the full identified result over the direct HL7
path, unaffected.

What you keep: QC charting, fleet management, turnaround reporting, reagent lot
exposure reporting, audit trail, firmware management. Everything that is about
managing the laboratory rather than about individual patients.

What you lose: the ability to search VeriConnect by patient, and the ability to
run a subject access export from VeriConnect - though if VeriConnect does not
hold identifiable patient data, you do not need to.

This is a configuration change, not a licensing change, and we will assist with
it at no charge. It is the single most effective thing most customers can do to
simplify their information governance position, and we mention it here because
it comes up too late in most conversations.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q8. WHAT HAPPENS TO OUR DATA IF WE LEAVE?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
On termination, you may request return of your data in a portable format
(JSON plus CSV) or deletion. Data is purged within 30 days of termination
unless you request return or a legal hold applies.

Backups roll off within 35 days.

We will provide written confirmation of deletion.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q9. HOW LONG IS OUR DATA KEPT WHILE WE ARE A CUSTOMER?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Result records          Your choice, 1-10 years, default 7
  Quality control         7 years default, not reducible below 2
  Audit trail             90 days (Essentials), 1 year (Professional),
                          7 years (Enterprise with VeriGuard)
  Instrument telemetry    13 months
  Backups                 35 days rolling

A note on the telemetry retention: 13 months has proved short in at least one
case where a customer needed to reconstruct events from 14 months earlier and
we could not (ticket VBST-4345). We record that here because a customer
planning around accreditation evidence should know it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q10. CAN WE MOVE REGIONS LATER?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Yes. Migration between regions is a service we perform, typically over a
weekend, with a read-only window of 2 to 6 hours depending on data volume.
Instruments continue to operate and to report to your LIS throughout; only
VeriConnect is affected.

There is a one-off charge (VC-VG-RES). Contact your account manager.
