[2025-06-17 10:44] Jason Okonkwo (Pathology Informatics Manager):
We're preparing our ISO 15189 evidence pack and I've been reconciling the
VeriGuard audit trail against what actually happened in the laboratory.

There are gaps. Specifically, actions taken at the instrument keypad -
calibrations, reagent changeovers, QC acceptance done at the analyser rather
than in VeriConnect - appear for some instruments and not for others.

Six of our eleven instruments show instrument-side audit events. Five show
nothing at all. All eleven are in the same tenant.

I'd assumed VeriGuard captured everything. Is that wrong?

[2025-06-17 14:20] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
It's partly wrong, and I'd rather say so plainly than manage the message.

VeriGuard's audit trail is complete for everything done in VeriConnect. For
actions taken at the instrument itself, the instrument writes to its own local
audit log and uploads it to VeriConnect only if:
(a) it is on firmware 4.1.0 or later, and
(b) cloud audit upload is enabled in its settings.

Let me check your fleet. My expectation is that the five silent instruments
are on older firmware, or have the setting off, or both.

[2025-06-17 16:05] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Checked. Of your eleven:
- Six on firmware 4.2.3 with cloud audit upload enabled - these are the ones
  you see.
- Three on 4.1.2 with the setting available but disabled. It defaults to off
  on upgrade, which is a poor default and I'm not going to defend it.
- Two on 4.0.x, which predates the capability entirely.

Actions:
1. Enable cloud audit upload on the three 4.1.2 instruments now, with your
   permission. Takes effect immediately, no restart.
2. Update the two 4.0.x instruments to 4.2.3 or later. That needs planning on
   your side because of the QC requirement afterwards.

What I cannot do is backfill. The local logs on those five instruments hold
their own history - retrievable at the instrument or by an engineer - but they
have not been uploaded and the upload is not retrospective. For your evidence
pack you would need to export the local logs and attach them separately.

[2025-06-18 10:30] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):

[2025-06-18 11:00] Dr Priyanka Vaidya (Consultant Haematologist):

[2025-06-18 15:40] Tom Reeves (Product Manager, VeriConnect):
Acknowledged without qualification. The limitation is real, it is not
documented prominently enough, and the default on upgrade is wrong.

What changes:
1. The VeriGuard datasheet is being revised to state the limitation explicitly
   under "Audit logging". That's DS-VG-002, out in due course.
2. VeriConnect will show a fleet-level warning where an enrolled instrument is
   not uploading audit events, so this is visible without anyone going looking.
3. The default is being changed so that cloud audit upload is on for any
   instrument in a VeriGuard tenant, opt-out rather than opt-in.

I'll be straight about timing: (1) is quick, (2) and (3) are product work and I
don't have dates yet.

I should also tell you that this was raised as observation NB-2025-OBS-04 in
our own Notified Body audit in April, so you are not the first to spot it and
we were already on notice.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: instrument-side audit events upload to VeriConnect only on
firmware 4.1.0+ with cloud audit upload enabled; setting defaulted to off on
upgrade. Five of eleven instruments silent.
Actions: setting enabled on three instruments; two upgraded from 4.0.x; local
logs exported for the evidence pack; datasheet, fleet warning and default
behaviour all changed.
Related: Notified Body observation NB-2025-OBS-04.
