[2025-01-20 11:03] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):
We are seeing P-201 aliquot volume out of tolerance on SP1-R1-0173,
increasing in frequency over the last six weeks. Initially perhaps twice a
week, now several times a day. Aliquots that pass appear low - our downstream
chemistry analyser is flagging short samples.

We have replaced the tips, we have re-primed, we have checked the tip rack
seating. No improvement.

[2025-01-20 12:40] Aisha Rahman (Support Engineering L2):
Thank you, that is a helpful history. A gradually worsening aliquot problem
that survives a prime and a tip change usually points at the syringe seal.

Before we send an engineer, please can you run the gravimetric verification in
SOP-VBS-009 at 200, 500 and 1000 uL, 10 replicates each. What I want to see is
whether the mean is off, the CV is off, or both:

- mean off, CV fine  -> calibration or seal, recoverable
- CV off             -> air, seal wear, or tip seating
- both off           -> seal, almost certainly

Also, a direct question, and I ask everyone this so please don't take it
personally: has anything other than RGT-CL ever gone through the fluid path?
Bleach in particular.

[2025-01-21 09:15] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):
Gravimetric results attached. Summary:

  200 uL   mean 189.4   CV 4.1%
  500 uL   mean 478.2   CV 3.4%
  1000 uL  mean 962.0   CV 2.9%

All three fail on both criteria.

On your question - I have asked. In November a member of staff used a
chlorine-based disinfectant for a decontamination cycle because the RGT-CL had
run out and there was pressure to get the instrument back. This was not
recorded anywhere. I only know because I asked the question you told me to
ask.

[2025-01-21 10:30] Aisha Rahman (Support Engineering L2):
Thank you for asking and for telling me. That's the cause and it explains the
six-week onset - the seals degrade progressively rather than failing at once.

What happens next: the syringe seal and the tip cone O-ring need replacing, and
the fluid path needs a full flush. An engineer visit is required. I'm going to
be straight with you that this is likely to be chargeable, because it's damage
rather than a fault, but I'll ask about goodwill given that the incompatibility
was not as prominent in the documentation as it should have been. It said "use
RGT-CL" but it did not say "and here is what happens if you don't".

I'm also raising this internally as a documentation issue.

[2025-01-21 11:02] Bernd Kaltenbrunner (Laborleiter):
That is fair. Please arrange the visit. And yes, the documentation point is

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: hypochlorite-based disinfectant used in the SP-1 fluid path in
place of RGT-CL, degrading the syringe seal and aliquot needle over ~6 weeks.
Actions: seal, O-ring and needle replaced; fluid path flushed; gravimetric
verification passed. Documentation CAPA raised (SOP-VBS-003 rev 3, RGT-CL
spec sheet).
Chargeable at 50% under goodwill.
