1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
This document states Veridia BioSystems' current position with respect to
Regulation (EU) 2017/746 on in-vitro diagnostic medical devices (IVDR). It is
maintained so that customers can answer questions from their own accreditation
assessors and regulatory functions without having to ask us each time.

It is a summary. It is not a declaration of conformity and it does not create
any contractual commitment.

4. TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400 Pro are currently placed on the EU market
under the transitional provisions of Article 110 IVDR, as amended by Regulation
(EU) 2022/112 and subsequently by Regulation (EU) 2024/1860.

Conditions we meet:
  - The devices were lawfully placed on the market under Directive 98/79/EC
    before 26 May 2022 and continue to be placed on the market.
  - There has been no significant change in the design or intended purpose.
  - The devices continue to comply with the Directive.
  - IVDR requirements relating to post-market surveillance, market
    surveillance, vigilance and registration of economic operators and devices
    apply in place of the corresponding Directive requirements. Veridia
    complies with these; our post-market surveillance system, PMS plan and
    vigilance reporting are operated to IVDR requirements.
  - A quality management system compliant with Article 10(8) is in place
    (audited 2025-04).
  - A formal application to a Notified Body was lodged before the applicable
    deadline (2024-06-17) and a written agreement is in place.

Applicable transitional deadline for Class C devices of this type:
31 December 2027, subject to the conditions above continuing to be met.

Devices already placed on the market and put into service before the deadline
may continue to be made available or put into service until 31 December 2028.

CUSTOMERS SHOULD NOTE: these dates are our reading of the current legislation
as at the date of this document. Transitional deadlines under IVDR have been
amended twice. Customers with a regulatory function should form their own view
and should not rely solely on this summary.

5. WHAT CHANGES FOR CUSTOMERS ON CERTIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing operational. The devices, reagents, software and performance are
unchanged; IVDR conformity assessment is an assessment of evidence Veridia
already holds, not a modification of the product.

What customers will receive:
  - An updated EU Declaration of Conformity referencing IVDR.
  - Updated labelling and Instructions for Use carrying the Notified Body
    number alongside the CE mark.
  - Updated Basic UDI-DI registration in EUDAMED.
  - A summary of safety and performance (SSP), which is an IVDR requirement for
    Class C devices and which will be publicly available through EUDAMED.

Firmware 4.4.0 and later already carry the Basic UDI-DI and conformity route in
the result header (see VBB-0067), in anticipation.

6. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The IVDR performance evaluation for both analysers comprises:

  Scientific validity        Established from published literature and from
                             the accepted clinical role of the full blood
                             count and differential. Not contentious.
  Analytical performance     Precision, trueness, linearity, measuring
                             interval, limits of detection and quantitation,
                             analytical specificity and interference,
                             carryover, sample stability. Studies conducted
                             2019-2024, supplemented 2025.
  Clinical performance       Method comparison against reference methods
                             (ICSH manual differential; flow cytometry) at
                             five sites across three countries.
                             n = 2,140 specimens for the differential;
                             n = 220 for reticulocyte; n = 410 for body
                             fluid. Summary at
                             reg-clinical-performance-summary.txt.

The advisory flags introduced in firmware 4.3.0 (BLAST?, NRBC?, PLT-CLUMP?,
FRAGMENTS?) are NOT within the scope of the performance evaluation and are
labelled research use only. They are excluded from the intended purpose. A
laboratory must not rely on them as the basis of a clinical decision.

7. VERISCAN 200
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 200 will not be transitioned to IVDR conformity. See VBB-0049.

Devices already placed on the market and put into service may continue in use
subject to the Article 110 conditions and to the transitional deadlines above.
From the applicable deadline the device may no longer be placed on the EU
market.

Veridia will continue to supply consumables and service for the VeriScan 200
according to the dates in VBB-0049 (end of software support 2026-12-31, end of
service 2027-12-31, end of spare parts 2028-12-31), subject to the transitional
provisions permitting it.

Customers operating a VeriScan 200 in the EU should plan migration. Customers
in Great Britain are not affected by IVDR and should refer to
reg-ukca-self-declaration.txt.

8. GREAT BRITAIN, NORTHERN IRELAND AND SWITZERLAND
-----------------------------------------------------------------
GREAT BRITAIN. IVDR does not apply. The devices are UKCA marked under the UK
Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (as amended). The UK's future regulatory
framework for medical devices is subject to ongoing legislative change; Veridia
monitors it and will notify customers of any change affecting them.

NORTHERN IRELAND. IVDR applies under the Windsor Framework. The position is as
sections 3 and 4 above. Devices placed on the NI market carry the CE mark.

SWITZERLAND. Following the lapse of the EU-Swiss Mutual Recognition Agreement
for medical devices, IVDs placed on the Swiss market require a Swiss
Authorised Representative and registration with Swissmedic.
