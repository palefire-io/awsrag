INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 400 Pro is an automated haematology analyser intended for the
in-vitro quantitative determination of haematological parameters, including a
five-part leucocyte differential and reticulocyte enumeration, in whole blood
samples collected into K2 or K3 EDTA, and for the quantitative determination of
total nucleated cell and red cell counts in body fluids (cerebrospinal fluid,
serous fluids and synovial fluid).

Intended for use by trained laboratory professionals in clinical laboratory
settings. Results are intended to be used in conjunction with other clinical
and laboratory findings and are not intended to be used as the sole basis for
diagnosis.

Body fluid mode is not validated for fluids containing visible particulate
matter, for lavage specimens, or for fluids with a haematocrit above 5 per
cent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
RELATIONSHIP TO THE VERISCAN 400
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The VeriScan 400 Pro shares the chassis, fluidics architecture, user interface
and firmware platform of the VeriScan 400. The two models are visually similar
and can be distinguished by:

  - the model badge to the right of the display (VS4-200P vs VS4-100);
  - the serial number prefix, VS4P- on the Pro and VS4- on the 400;
  - the presence of a fourth cartridge bay (RGT-RT) on the Pro;
  - the Reticulocyte and Body Fluid entries on the main run menu;
  - Settings > About reports "VeriScan 400 Pro" as the platform string.

Sites operating a mixed fleet are advised to label instruments clearly. A
recurring source of support tickets is a laboratory attempting a reticulocyte
count or citing a Pro-only error code on a non-Pro instrument, or ordering
RGT-RT stain against a VeriScan 400. See lookup-error-codes-veriscan-400.csv
column "applies_to".

Substantive differences are in the table below. They are not all obvious from a
casual comparison of the two datasheets.

  Attribute                    VeriScan 400        VeriScan 400 Pro
  ---------------------------  ------------------  ---------------------
  Throughput (sustained)       60 samples/hr       80 samples/hr
  WBC/DIFF light source        Halogen, 535 nm     Diode laser, 638 nm
  Reference photodiode         No                  Yes (baseline subtract)
  Reticulocyte channel         No                  Yes
  Body fluid mode              No                  Yes
  Extended flagging            No                  Yes (FW 4.3.0+)
  Cartridge bays               3                   4
  On-board result storage      10,000              20,000
  Mass                         42 kg               46 kg
  Sample volume (closed tube)  150 uL              130 uL
  Pro-only error codes         n/a                 E-215, E-601, E-604,
                                                   E-607, E-711, E-715
  Spare lamp assembly          VS4-OPT-104         not applicable
  Spare laser module           not applicable      VS4-OPT-118

The reference photodiode and per-run baseline subtraction on the Pro are the
reason the Pro was outside the scope of FSN-2025-003, although the affected
reagent lots were withdrawn from all instruments regardless of model.

MEASURED AND DERIVED PARAMETERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Whole blood, measured:  WBC, RBC, HGB, PLT, MCV, RET%
Whole blood, derived:   HCT, MCH, MCHC, RDW-SD, RDW-CV, MPV, PDW, P-LCR,
                        PCT, RET#, IRF (immature reticulocyte fraction)
Differential (5-part):  NEUT, LYMPH, MONO, EOS, BASO (absolute and %)
Body fluid mode:        WBC-BF, RBC-BF, MN# / MN%, PMN# / PMN%
Advisory flags (RUO, firmware 4.3.0 and later, disable-able):
                        BLAST?, NRBC?, PLT-CLUMP?, FRAGMENTS?

Advisory flags are research use only and must not be used as the sole basis
for a clinical decision.

OPERATING CONDITIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Ambient temperature       | 18 C to 32 C  (revised - see VBB-0052)
Relative humidity         | 20% to 80% non-condensing
Altitude                  | up to 2000 m
Storage/transport temp    | -10 C to 50 C

The reticulocyte flow cell is temperature-regulated to 37.0 +/- 0.5 C. Above
32 C ambient the heater has insufficient headroom to regulate reliably and
E-607 may be raised. This is the most common environmental failure mode
reported on the Pro.
