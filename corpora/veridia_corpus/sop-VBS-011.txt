SOP Number:      SOP-VBS-011
Title:           Annual Preventive Maintenance Checklist - VeriScan 400 /
                 400 Pro
Revision:        4
Effective Date:  2025-10-06
Author:          Priya Anand, Head of Field Service Engineering
Approved by:     James Okafor, VP Customer Support
Review due:      2027-10-06
Audience:        Veridia field service engineers

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Annual preventive maintenance for VeriScan 400 (VS4-100) and VeriScan 400 Pro
(VS4-200P). Approximately 2.5 hours per instrument for a 400, 3.5 hours for a
Pro including optical verification.

Parts: VS4-KIT-PM1 (annual PM kit). Additional parts as found.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. BEFORE ATTENDING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Review the instrument's event log in VeriConnect for the preceding 12
    months. Note recurring error codes.
[ ] Review open and recently closed tickets for the account.
[ ] Check the installed firmware against log-firmware-releases.csv. Plan an
    update if the instrument is more than two minor versions behind, and
    confirm with the laboratory before doing so - a firmware update requires
    full QC afterwards (SOP-VBS-001 section 9) and the lab must have time.
[ ] Confirm the site has control material in date for post-PM QC.
[ ] Confirm parts availability and lead time.
[ ] Where the site also runs a VeriPrep SP-1, check whether an SP-1 PM is due
    at the same visit, and whether the analyser firmware update would take the
    pair out of protocol compatibility (P-407). See VBB-0055.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. ON ARRIVAL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Report to the laboratory manager. Agree the window.
[ ] Confirm the instrument is not mid-batch.
[ ] Record: serial number, firmware version, run count, ambient temperature,
    relative humidity.
[ ] Photograph the installation if the siting looks marginal - against a
    window, above a centrifuge, in an unventilated alcove. Ambient siting
    problems are the root cause of a material share of E-607 and W-031
    tickets and are far easier to raise with evidence.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. FLUIDICS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Replace sample probe seal kit (VS4-SP-021).
[ ] Inspect the sample probe for burrs, bends and deposit. Replace
    (VS4-MEC-011) if in doubt.
[ ] Replace pump tubing from the PM kit.
[ ] Replace inline filters from the PM kit.
[ ] Inspect all fluidic connections for weeping. Retighten to spec.
[ ] Inspect the waste line for kinks and the waste float sensor for deposit.
[ ] Run Fluidics Prime x3. Confirm no air.
[ ] Check aspiration pressure in Service > Diagnostics > Fluidics. Record.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. MECHANICS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Clean the cap-pierce station. Remove shed septum material.
[ ] Inspect the piercing needle. Replace (VS4-MEC-011) if bent or blunt.
[ ] Check rotor home sensor operation. Clean the sensor window.
[ ] Check mixer operation. Listen for bearing noise - a mixer that will fail
    within the year is usually audible now (E-412 follows).
[ ] Exercise the autoloader through a full cycle with empty racks.
[ ] Inspect and clean the barcode reader window.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. OPTICS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
VeriScan 400 (halogen)
[ ] Record lamp hours. Replace (VS4-OPT-104) if above 1800 h; the specified
    life is 2000 h and an in-service failure costs the laboratory more than
    the lamp does.
[ ] Clean the collector lens.
[ ] Run optical calibration with fresh VeriCal beads. Record pass/fail and the
    gain values.

VeriScan 400 Pro (laser)
[ ] Record laser hours and drive current. A drive current trending upward
    toward the E-215 threshold predicts a laser module failure; note it in the
    visit report and flag to the account manager if within 20 per cent of the
    limit.
[ ] Do not open the laser enclosure. Class 1 rating depends on it remaining
    closed.
[ ] Run optical calibration. Record.
[ ] Inspect the retic flow cell. Check heater regulation holds 37.0 +/- 0.5 C.

Both models
[ ] Run an Extended Clean. Confirm background counts within the limits at
    SOP-VBS-001 Appendix A.
[ ] Inspect the flow cell for deposit. Replace (VS4-OPT-220, or VS4-OPT-224
    for the retic cell) if background will not come down.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. ELECTRICAL AND SOFTWARE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Visual inspection of the mains lead and the IEC inlet.
[ ] Portable appliance test if required by the site.
[ ] Check the RTC. If drift exceeds 2 minutes per week, replace the battery
    (VS4-ELE-090). Confirm NTP is configured and reachable; an instrument with
    no NTP source will drift and the drift is easily mistaken for the firmware
    4.2.1 defect (VBB-0041), which it is not.
[ ] Confirm the instrument appears online in VeriConnect. Re-enrol if it shows
    offline while operating normally - a known issue after updating to 4.3.0
    on instruments enrolled before 4.1.0 (VBB-0061).
[ ] Check the display and touchscreen. A flickering display is usually the
    ribbon cable (VS4-DIS-142) rather than the panel.
[ ] Download the event log for the visit report.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. REAGENTS AND CONSUMABLES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Record all cartridge lot numbers and on-board days.
[ ] Check each lot against active field safety notices.
[ ] Note any cartridge approaching the 90-day on-board limit and tell the
    laboratory before you leave.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. VERIFICATION AND HANDBACK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Background count within limits.
[ ] Both QC levels run and within range. Retic and body fluid QC on the Pro
    where those modes are in use.
[ ] Carryover check: high sample followed by three blanks, carryover < 0.5%.
[ ] Precision check: 10 replicates of a normal sample, CV within the
    specification in the datasheet.
[ ] Complete the visit report. Have the laboratory sign it.
[ ] Enter the visit in the field service log with visit ID, parts used, time
    on site and any related ticket number.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. NOTES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 If the instrument fails verification at section 9 and cannot be brought
     into specification during the visit, do not hand it back as serviceable.
     Record the failure, tell the laboratory manager in person, and raise a
     P2 ticket before leaving site.

10.2 Where a site's instruments repeatedly need the same part, say so in the
     visit report. Two mixer motors in eighteen months at the same site is a
     siting or supply signal, not bad luck.
