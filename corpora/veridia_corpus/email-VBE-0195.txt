6. FIELD SAFETY NOTICES AND KNOWN ISSUES, LAST 36 MONTHS
Full disclosure as requested.

  FSN-2025-003 (14 Feb 2025). Recall of VeriLyse RGT-L3 lyse reagent lots
  L3-2025-0440 to L3-2025-0460. Elevated baseline absorbance developing during
  on-board use, causing spurious WBC flags and a positive WBC bias of up to
  +0.9 x10^9/L on the VeriScan 400. The 400 Pro was not affected. Root cause

  oxidation of the buffer. Reformulated (revision D) from March 2025.

  FSN-2024-006 (8 Nov 2024). Withdrawal of RGT-RT reticulocyte stain

  adverse patient outcome.
