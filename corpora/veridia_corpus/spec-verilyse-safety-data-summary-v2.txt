2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
All products: laboratory coat, disposable nitrile gloves.
RGT-L3, RGT-CL, RGT-RT: additionally eye protection to EN 166.
RGT-CL: do not aerosolise. Handle in a well ventilated area. Personnel with a
        known proteolytic enzyme sensitivity must not handle this product.

Once a reagent has contacted a patient sample, treat the mixture and all waste
as potentially infectious regardless of the reagent's own classification.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. FIRST AID
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Eye contact     Rinse cautiously with water for at least 15 minutes, holding
                the eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if present and easy to
                do. For RGT-CL seek immediate medical attention (H318).
Skin contact    Remove contaminated clothing. Wash with soap and water. Seek
                medical advice if irritation persists.
Inhalation      Move to fresh air. For RGT-CL, if breathing is difficult seek
                immediate medical attention.
Ingestion       Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Seek medical advice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. SPILLAGE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Small spill (< 100 mL)
  Absorb with inert absorbent. Place in a chemical waste container. Wash the
  area with water. For RGT-CL, neutralise the residue with a dilute weak acid
  (e.g. 1% citric acid) before washing, then rinse thoroughly.

Large spill (> 100 mL) or spill of a reagent that has contacted samples
  Evacuate the immediate area. Wear eye protection and gloves. Contain with
  absorbent. Where the spill includes sample material, disinfect the area
  after absorption using the laboratory's standard blood spill procedure. Do
  NOT apply hypochlorite directly to RGT-L3 or RGT-CL spills before
  absorption; the combination of hypochlorite with the surfactant load
  generates an unpleasant and irritant aerosol.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. STORAGE AND INCOMPATIBILITIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Product  | Storage        | Do not store with / mix with
---------|----------------|--------------------------------------------
RGT-D5   | 2-25 C         | Nothing specific. Do not freeze.
RGT-L3   | 2-25 C         | Strong oxidisers. Hypochlorite.
RGT-CL   | 5-25 C         | Acids (generates heat). Hypochlorite.
RGT-RT   | 2-8 C, dark    | Do not freeze. Protect from light.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. DISPOSAL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Waste from the instrument fluid path contains lysed patient sample and must be
handled as clinical waste in accordance with local regulations (in the UK,
Hazardous Waste Regulations and HTM 07-01).

Unused RGT-D5 may generally be disposed of to drain with copious water where
permitted by the local water authority. Unused RGT-L3 and RGT-CL should be
consigned as chemical waste; RGT-L3 carries H412 and should not be discharged
to surface water. Unused RGT-RT should be consigned as chemical waste owing to
the dye content.

Empty cartridges: rinse and dispose of as laboratory plastic waste. The RFID
tag contains no battery and requires no special handling.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. TRANSPORT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
None of the VeriLyse products is classified as dangerous goods for transport
by road (ADR), sea (IMDG) or air (IATA). No UN number is assigned. Ship at
ambient; RGT-RT should be shipped with a cool pack where transit exceeds 48
hours, but is not a temperature-controlled dangerous good.
