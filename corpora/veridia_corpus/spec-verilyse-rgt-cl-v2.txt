INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
RGT-CL is an enzymatic and alkaline cleaning solution for the fluid paths of
VeriScan 400, VeriScan 400 Pro and VeriPrep SP-1 instruments. It removes
protein deposits, lysed cell debris and stain residue from flow cells, tubing
and aspiration probes.

RGT-CL is not a disinfectant and is not validated as a decontaminant for
transmissible agents. Where surface disinfection is required, use the agents
listed in the instrument IFU section 14.

STORAGE AND STABILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Unopened, 5-25 C                 | 24 months from manufacture
On board after first pierce      | 180 days
Do not freeze                    | Yes
Protect from direct sunlight     | Yes

The enzyme loses activity above 30 C. Cartridges stored above 30 C for more
than 30 days should be discarded even if within labelled shelf life.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
VeriScan 400 / 400 Pro
  Automatic shutdown clean       daily, 12 mL per cycle
  Extended Clean routine         on demand, 45 mL per cycle, 22 minutes
  Probe soak                     manual, per SOP-VBS-001 appendix B

An Extended Clean is the first-line response to E-207 (flow cell background
high) and to W-042 (elevated background WBC count). If two consecutive Extended
Clean cycles do not clear the condition, the flow cell (VS4-OPT-220) or, on the
Pro, the retic flow cell (VS4-OPT-224) should be considered for replacement.

VeriPrep SP-1
  Weekly decontamination cycle   180 mL per cycle, per SOP-VBS-003
  Do NOT substitute a hypochlorite-based disinfectant. The SP-1 aliquot fluid
  path includes components that are not compatible with hypochlorite and will
  degrade, producing P-201 aliquot volume errors within weeks.

SAFETY SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Classified under CLP:
  Eye Dam. 1, H318 - Causes serious eye damage
  Resp. Sens. 1, H334 - May cause allergy or asthma symptoms or breathing
    difficulties if inhaled (enzyme content)
Pictograms: GHS05 (corrosion), GHS08 (health hazard)
Signal word: Danger

Do not aerosolise. Wear eye protection. Persons with a known enzyme sensitivity
should not handle this product.
