[2025-02-21 10:15] Aisha Rahman (Support Engineering L2):
They do. And it explains why your middleware is rejecting rather than
accepting bad data - it's validating OBR-7 against its own clock and refusing
anything in the future, which is the correct behaviour and has protected you
from the worst of it.

This is a firmware defect in 4.2.1, now under investigation as VBI-0091. The
HL7 timestamp is derived from a counter that runs fast on the Pro. The
instrument display reads the clock directly, which is why it's right.

Result values are not affected. No number you have received is wrong; the
times attached to them are.

Immediate options:
a) power cycle both instruments daily - holds the error to a few minutes and
   should stop the rejections;
b) roll back to firmware 4.2.0 - removes the defect entirely.

I'd suggest (a) today and (b) as soon as we can get an engineer to you.

[2025-02-21 11:35] Aisha Rahman (Support Engineering L2):
Straight answers.

Magnitude: on a Pro at 22 days uptime, we would expect on the order of 60-75
minutes of drift. Our reference instrument measured 47 minutes at 14 days.
It's larger than I'd like.

Period: firmware 4.2.1 was released on 3 February. Your instruments will have
taken it around then, so about three weeks.

Direction: always forward. The displayed collection time is later than the
true collection time, never earlier. So a result appears more recent than it
is.

Important caveat on OBR-7 specifically: where your LIS sends a collection time
with the order, the instrument passes that value through unaltered and it is
NOT affected. It's only where the instrument has to supply the time itself
that the drift appears. Jason will know which applies at your site - it may
be that your ward-collected samples with a booked-in collection time are
completely unaffected and only your analyser-timed samples are involved.

OBR-22, the result time, is affected regardless.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: VeriScan firmware 4.2.1 HL7 timestamp accumulator defect
(VBI-0091). Middleware correctly rejected future-dated OBR-7.
Actions: daily power cycle as interim; rollback to 4.2.0 (FSV-2025-0180);
update to 4.2.3; VBB-0041 supplied.
Secondary outcome: tenant firmware update policy changed to manual approval;
product feedback raised on firmware deployment notifications.
