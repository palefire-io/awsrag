INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
RGT-L3 is a lytic reagent for use with VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400 Pro
haematology analysers. It lyses erythrocytes, converts haemoglobin to a stable
cyanide-free chromogen for colorimetric measurement, and differentially
modifies leucocyte membranes to permit five-part differentiation by scatter and
absorbance or fluorescence. For in-vitro diagnostic use by trained laboratory
personnel.

COMPOSITION (nominal, revision D)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Component                          | Concentration
-----------------------------------|------------------
Quaternary ammonium surfactant     | 12 g/L
Sodium lauryl sulfate              | 1.4 g/L
Dual buffer system (rev D)         | 8.5 g/L total
  - organic zwitterionic buffer    |
  - phosphate co-buffer            |
Chelating agent                    | 0.6 g/L
Stabiliser                         | 0.3 g/L
Purified water                     | to 1 L
pH (25 C)                          | 6.9 +/- 0.1
Absorbance at 535 nm (blank)       | <= 0.015 AU at release
                                   | <= 0.025 AU at end of on-board life

CYANIDE-FREE. Contains no reagents of human or animal origin.

Revision C used a single organic buffer. Revision D replaces it with the dual
buffer system above, which is substantially less sensitive to trace metal ion
contamination in the incoming raw material stream. See VBB-0047 for the change
notification and method comparison data.

IMPORTANT - RECALLED LOTS
  Lots L3-2025-0440 through L3-2025-0460 inclusive (revision C) were recalled
  under Field Safety Notice FSN-2025-003 issued 2025-02-14. These lots may show
  elevated baseline absorbance at 535 nm developing from approximately day 5 of
  on-board use, producing spurious WBC flags and a positive WBC bias on
  VeriScan 400 instruments. They must not be used on any instrument. If you
  hold stock from this range, refer to FSN-2025-003 for the return process.

  Root cause is documented in investigation report VBI-0087.

INSTRUMENT COMPATIBILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  VeriScan 400        Yes. Firmware 4.0.0 and later. Revisions C and D.
  VeriScan 400 Pro    Yes. Firmware 4.0.0 and later. Revisions C and D.
  VeriScan 200        NO. Revision D is not validated on the VeriScan 200
                      platform and must not be used on it. A platform-specific
                      lyse fill is maintained for VeriScan 200 sites until
                      2027-12-31. See VBB-0049.

Revisions C and D may be used on the same instrument at different times. Do not
mix within a cartridge bay and do not decant between cartridges. No
recalibration is required at revision changeover; run both QC levels per
SOP-VBS-002.
