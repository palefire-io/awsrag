1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  This procedure defines the minimum daily quality control activities to be
     performed on VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400 Pro haematology analysers
     before patient samples are analysed and results released.

1.2  It is written for customer laboratories. Veridia field service engineers
     performing QC as part of a service visit follow SOP-VBS-011 in addition
     to this procedure.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Applies to all VeriScan 400 (VS4-100) and VeriScan 400 Pro (VS4-200P)
     instruments.

2.2  Sections 7 and 8 (reticulocyte and body fluid QC) apply to the VeriScan
     400 Pro only. If your instrument does not display a Reticulocyte entry on
     the run menu, it is a VeriScan 400 and those sections do not apply. See
     spec-veriscan-400-pro-v3.txt section "Relationship to the VeriScan 400"
     if you are unsure which model you operate.

2.3  This SOP does not cover calibration. Calibration is described in the
     Instructions for Use, section 9.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. RESPONSIBILITIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  The operator performing the QC run is responsible for executing this
     procedure and for recording the result.

3.2  A biomedical scientist holding the results.authorise permission (or local
     equivalent) is responsible for reviewing and accepting or rejecting the
     QC run. The operator who ran the QC may perform this review provided
     they hold the permission.

3.3  The laboratory manager is responsible for the QC policy, the control
     material ordering schedule, and for investigating repeated failures.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. PRE-CHECKS (BEFORE ANY QC RUN)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Confirm ambient temperature at the instrument is between 18 C and 32 C.
     Measure at the instrument, not at the room thermostat. Instruments sited
     near a window, above a centrifuge or in a poorly ventilated side room
     commonly run 3-6 C above the room reading. Reference: VBB-0052.

     NOTE: firmware up to and including 4.3.1 raises the W-031 ambient warning
     only outside 15-35 C, so the absence of W-031 does not confirm compliance
     with 4.1. Firmware 4.4.0 and later align the warning with 18-32 C.

4.2  Confirm relative humidity is between 20 and 80 per cent, non-condensing.

4.3  Check the waste container. Empty it if more than three quarters full.
     E-107 will halt a run mid-batch, which is avoidable.

4.4  Check reagent cartridge levels and remaining on-board days. From firmware
     4.3.0 the instrument displays a countdown against the 90-day on-board
     limit. Replace any cartridge with fewer than 2 days remaining before
     starting a batch.

4.5  Review any errors or warnings logged since the previous QC run
     (Log > Events). Investigate and clear before proceeding.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. STARTUP AND BACKGROUND CHECK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Power on the instrument or wake it from standby. Allow the startup
     sequence to complete. Startup includes an automatic prime and background
     count and takes approximately 8 minutes from cold.

5.2  Review the background count. Acceptance limits:

       WBC   <= 0.20 x10^9/L
       RBC   <= 0.02 x10^12/L
       HGB   <= 1 g/L
       PLT   <= 10 x10^9/L

5.3  If any background result exceeds its limit, run a second background
     cycle. If it fails again:
       a) run an Extended Clean (Service > Maintenance > Extended Clean,
          approximately 22 minutes, consumes 45 mL RGT-CL);
       b) repeat the background count;
       c) if it still fails, do NOT proceed to patient testing. Raise a
          support ticket. Persistent high background is most often flow cell
          fouling (E-207) or a reagent problem.

5.4  If the elevated background is confined to the WBC channel and is
     accompanied by W-042, check the RGT-L3 lot number against current field
     safety notices before assuming an instrument fault. See section 6.4.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. REAGENT AND LOT VERIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Record the lot number of each cartridge on board: RGT-D5, RGT-L3, RGT-CL
     and, on the Pro, RGT-RT. The lot number is shown under
     Reagents > Cartridge Detail and printed on the cartridge label.

6.2  Confirm the lot number displayed by the instrument matches the label. A
     mismatch indicates an RFID or labelling problem; quarantine the cartridge
     and raise a ticket with photographs of both.

6.3  Confirm each cartridge is within its unopened shelf life and within its
     on-board limit (90 days for D5, L3 and CL; 60 days for RT).

6.4  Check each lot number against the field safety notices in force. The
     current list is published on the customer portal and in VeriConnect under
     Notifications > Safety. As at the effective date of this revision, lots
     L3-2025-0440 to L3-2025-0460 inclusive are recalled under FSN-2025-003
     and must not be used.

     This check was added in revision 6. During the FSN-2025-003 episode
     several laboratories continued to use affected stock for some days after
     the notice was issued because there was no defined point in the daily
     workflow at which lot numbers were compared against notices.

6.5  VeriConnect Professional tier and above records lot numbers automatically
     and will display a banner if a cartridge matching an active notice is
     detected. This does not remove the requirement at 6.4; the banner depends
     on the instrument being enrolled and online.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. CBC AND DIFFERENTIAL QC (ALL MODELS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Remove the control vials from refrigeration and allow 15 minutes at room
     temperature. Do not shortcut this.

7.2  Mix by gentle inversion for a full 30 seconds. Do not vortex. Do not use
     a mechanical mixer. Inadequate mixing is the single most common cause of
     an out-of-range QC result on this platform; the characteristic signature
     is a low RBC and low HCT with a normal HGB.

7.3  Run a minimum of two levels (normal and one abnormal) in QC mode:
     Run > Quality Control > select control lot > present vial.

7.4  Do not run controls in patient mode. Results run in patient mode are not
     charted and will not satisfy accreditation requirements.

7.5  Review each result against the assigned range for the lot.

     CRITICAL: on a mixed fleet, use the value column for the correct model.
     The assay sheet carries separate columns for the VeriScan 400 and the
     VeriScan 400 Pro. The differential channel values differ materially
     between the two because the optics differ. Using the wrong column
     produces an apparent systematic differential failure on an instrument
     that is performing correctly.

7.6  Westgard rules applied by default: 1-3s (reject), 2-2s (reject), R-4s
     (reject). 1-2s is a warning only and does not require rejection of the
     run. The rule set is configurable from VeriConnect Professional tier.

7.7  Acceptance:
       - All levels within range, no rejection rule triggered: accept the run
         and proceed to patient testing.
       - A 1-2s warning on a single analyte at a single level: accept, but
         note it. Two consecutive 1-2s warnings in the same direction warrant
         investigation even though no rule has been broken.
       - Any rejection rule triggered: go to section 10.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. RETICULOCYTE AND BODY FLUID QC (VERISCAN 400 PRO ONLY)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Reticulocyte QC (VT-RET-2, two levels) must be run:
       - daily, on any day on which reticulocyte counts will be reported;
       - after every RGT-RT stain lot change (mandatory, and the retic channel
         is locked by the instrument until calibration and QC are accepted);
       - after replacement of the retic flow cell (VS4-OPT-224).

8.2  Reticulocyte control has a 7-day open-vial stability. Date the vial when
     opened.

8.3  Body fluid QC (VT-BF-2) must be run on any day on which body fluid
     samples will be analysed. Open-vial stability is 24 hours.

8.4  Before the first body fluid sample of a batch, run three background
     cycles in body fluid mode to clear whole blood carryover. Failure to do
     this produces E-711 and, worse, may produce a plausible but falsely
     elevated cell count on a genuinely acellular fluid.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. QC AFTER A FIRMWARE UPDATE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  A full QC run (both levels, plus retic and body fluid levels where those
     modes are in use) must be run and accepted after any firmware update
     before patient testing resumes.

9.2  Record the firmware version before and after the update in the QC record.
     VeriConnect 6.2 and later annotate the Levey-Jennings chart with firmware
     changes automatically.

9.3  If the update was applied to correct a known defect, confirm that the
     symptom has cleared before releasing the instrument. For example, after
     updating from firmware 4.2.1 or 4.2.2 to 4.2.3 or later, confirm that
     result timestamps in the LIS match the instrument display (see VBB-0041).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. ACTION ON QC FAILURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Do not report patient results from a run in which QC has been rejected.

10.2 First-line actions, in order:
       a) Confirm the correct control lot and the correct model column were
          selected.
       b) Re-mix the control vial thoroughly and repeat. A single repeat is
          permitted. Repeated repeating until a pass is obtained is not
          acceptable practice and will be visible in the audit trail.
       c) Check the vial's open date and expiry.
       d) Open a fresh vial of the same lot and repeat.
       e) Run a background count (section 5).

10.3 If the failure persists with fresh material:
       a) Review recent reagent lot changes. Correlate the onset of the
          failure with the date of any changeover (SOP-VBS-002 records this).
       b) Check the reagent lot against current field safety notices.
       c) Run an Extended Clean and repeat.
       d) Consider recalibration. Recalibration should not be performed
          casually; an instrument recalibrated onto a drifting reagent will
          appear to pass and will then fail when correct reagent is restored.

10.4 If the failure is not resolved by 10.3, raise a support ticket with:
       - instrument serial number and firmware version;
       - control lot number and level(s) affected;
       - which analytes failed and in which direction;
       - all reagent lot numbers on board;
       - the last 20 QC data points, exported from the instrument or from
         VeriConnect (Quality > Export);
       - background count results;
       - ambient temperature.

     Tickets that arrive without the above take materially longer to resolve.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
11. RECORDS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
11.1 QC results are retained on the instrument (10,000 results on the 400,
     20,000 on the Pro) and, where enrolled, in VeriConnect for the tenant's
     configured retention period (minimum 2 years for QC records, default 7).

11.2 The laboratory must retain evidence of QC review and acceptance. In
     VeriConnect this is captured automatically in the audit trail. On a
     standalone instrument the laboratory must maintain its own record.

11.3 Where QC has been rejected, the record must show the investigation and
     the outcome, not only the rejection.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
APPENDIX A - BACKGROUND COUNT ACCEPTANCE LIMITS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Analyte  | Limit
  ---------|-------------------------
  WBC      | <= 0.20 x10^9/L
  RBC      | <= 0.02 x10^12/L
  HGB      | <= 1 g/L
  PLT      | <= 10 x10^9/L
  RET#     | <= 0.005 x10^12/L  (Pro)
  WBC-BF   | <= 2 /uL           (Pro, body fluid mode)

-----------------------------------------------------------------
APPENDIX B - PROBE SOAK PROCEDURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
B.1  Use where E-104 (sample probe blockage) recurs after a Probe Unblock
     cycle has been run twice.
B.2  Power down the instrument.
B.3  Wearing gloves and eye protection, remove the probe guard.
B.4  Apply approximately 2 mL of RGT-CL to a clean vessel and immerse the
     probe tip for 15 minutes. Do not immerse above the probe collar.
B.5  Rinse with purified water. Do not wipe the probe tip with tissue; fibres
     shed into the aperture.
B.6  Refit the guard, power on, and run two background counts.
B.7  If E-104 recurs within 24 hours, the probe or the seal kit (VS4-SP-021)
     requires replacement. Raise a ticket.
