DECLARATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
We, Veridia BioSystems Ltd., declare under our sole responsibility that the
devices identified above are in conformity with the provisions of Council
Directive 98/79/EC of 27 October 1998 on in-vitro diagnostic medical devices,
and with the essential requirements of Annex I thereof.

Conformity assessment route: Annex III (EC Declaration of Conformity),
self-declaration. The devices are not listed in Annex II List A or List B and
are not devices for self-testing. Notified Body involvement is not required
under this Directive for these devices.

The devices also conform to:
  Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS), as amended
  Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (REACH), in respect of substance content

TRANSITIONAL STATEMENT - REGULATION (EU) 2017/746
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Directive 98/79/EC has been repealed and replaced by Regulation (EU) 2017/746
(IVDR). The devices identified above are placed on the EU market under the
transitional provisions of Article 110 IVDR as amended.

Veridia BioSystems Ltd confirms:
  (a) the devices were lawfully placed on the market under Directive 98/79/EC
      before 26 May 2022;
  (b) there has been no significant change in design or intended purpose;
  (c) a quality management system compliant with Article 10(8) IVDR is in
      place, audited by Notified Body 0086 in April 2025;
  (d) a formal application for conformity assessment was lodged with Notified
      Body 0086 on 17 June 2024 and a written agreement is in place;
  (e) the IVDR requirements relating to post-market surveillance, market
      surveillance, vigilance and registration of economic operators and
      devices are applied in place of the corresponding Directive
      requirements.

Current status of the IVDR conformity assessment is maintained at
reg-ivdr-transition-summary.txt and is available on request.
