1. SAMPLE TUBE REQUIREMENTS - ANALYSERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Anticoagulant     K2 EDTA or K3 EDTA. Fill to the manufacturer's mark.
                  Lithium heparin, citrate and fluoride oxalate are NOT
                  acceptable for haematology on this platform.
Minimum fill      1.0 mL in a nominal 4 mL tube. Underfilled tubes give a
                  dilutional error and raise W-050.
Maximum fill      To the tube mark. Overfilled tubes give inadequate
                  anticoagulation and may clot (E-104).
Mixing            Invert 8-10 times immediately after collection.
Stability         Whole blood at 18-25 C: CBC 8 hours, differential 6 hours.
                  At 2-8 C: CBC 24 hours, differential 8 hours.
                  Reticulocyte (Pro): 24 hours at 2-8 C.
                  Body fluid (Pro): analyse within 1 hour; refrigerate and
                  analyse within 4 hours if delay is unavoidable.

Closed-tube (cap-pierce) mode dimensional envelope
  Diameter        12.0 to 16.5 mm
  Height, capped  73.5 to 100.0 mm
  Cap material    Rubber-lined polypropylene (Hemogard-style) or rubber
                  stopper. Metal-crimped caps are not supported.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. TUBE REQUIREMENTS - VERIPREP SP-1
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The SP-1 envelope is narrower than the analyser envelope. A tube that runs
without issue on the analyser in closed-tube mode may not be handled reliably
by an SP-1r1.

  Attribute            SP-1r1              SP-1r2
  -------------------  ------------------  ------------------
  Diameter             12.0 - 16.5 mm      12.0 - 16.5 mm
  Height, capped       73.5 - 80.0 mm      68.0 - 82.5 mm
  Label top position   within 1 mm of      within 4 mm of
                       scanner window      scanner window
  Short-cap 5 mL       NOT RELIABLE        Supported
  tubes (< 75.5 mm)    see VBB-0038

If your 5 mL tube measures less than 75.5 mm overall with the cap on, and you
are running an SP-1r1, expect P-101, P-105 and eventually P-112. Mitigations:
SP1-ADP-05 adapters, raised label position, or the SP1-KIT-R2UP retrofit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. VERIFIED TUBE TYPES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The following generic descriptions have been verified by Veridia. Supplier
catalogue numbers are omitted deliberately: the same catalogue number may be
filled at different manufacturing sites with different cap profiles, and
Veridia cannot warrant a supplier's dimensional consistency.

  Nominal    Diameter x Height    Analyser   SP-1r1   SP-1r2
  ---------  -------------------  ---------  -------  -------
  2 mL       13 x 75 mm           Yes        Yes      Yes
  3 mL       13 x 75 mm           Yes        Yes      Yes
  4 mL       13 x 75 mm           Yes        Yes      Yes
  4 mL       13 x 100 mm          Yes        Yes      Yes
  5 mL       13 x 75 mm std cap   Yes        Yes      Yes
  5 mL       13 x 75 mm short cap Yes        NO (1)   Yes
  6 mL       13 x 100 mm          Yes        Yes      Yes
  9 mL       16 x 100 mm          Yes        Yes      Yes
  Paediatric 10.25 x 47 mm        Open tube  No       No
             (500 uL microtube)   mode only

  (1) See section 2 and VBB-0038.

Paediatric microtubes must be presented in open-tube mode. They cannot be
cap-pierced and cannot be handled by the SP-1 in either revision. Minimum
volume in open-tube mode is 130 uL (VeriScan 400) or 110 uL (400 Pro), which
in practice means a microtube filled to at least 250 uL to allow for dead
volume.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. LABELLING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Symbologies       Code 128 (recommended), Code 39, Interleaved 2 of 5,
                  Codabar, Data Matrix, QR
Print quality     ANSI grade C minimum; 300 dpi direct thermal recommended
Quiet zone        2.5 mm minimum either side
Orientation       Vertical bars, label applied straight; skew above 5 degrees
                  reduces read rate materially
Position          Label top edge no more than 10 mm below the base of the cap
                  for SP-1r1; no more than 14 mm for r2
Overlabelling     One label only. A second label over the first is a common
                  cause of P-103.
Wrap              Leave a 20 mm vertical window unlabelled so the operator and
                  the instrument clot detector can see the sample.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. RACKS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  VS4-RACK-10    10-position analyser autoloader rack
  VS4-RACK-50    50-position extended autoloader magazine (5 x 10)
  SP1-RACK-IN    SP-1 input rack, 20 position
  SP1-RACK-OUT   SP-1 destination rack, 20 position
  SP1-RACK-EXC   SP-1 exception rack, 20 position, red

Third-party racks are not supported. Rack RFID tags are read at load; an
unrecognised rack raises P-304 on the SP-1 and is ignored by the analyser
autoloader.
