--- Resolution ---
Root cause: SP-1r1 feed mechanism tolerance interaction with short-cap 5mL
tubes (VBB-0038), compounded by feed pawl wear from 87 jam events.
Actions: feed mechanism replaced (FSV-2025-0271); SP-1r2 retrofit
(SP1-KIT-R2UP) fitted 2025-09-30 at 50% of list under the VBB-0055 goodwill
programme; firmware 2.3.0.
Customer jam log was the evidence basis for the goodwill decision.
See also: VBST-3795, VBST-3812, VBB-0038, VBB-0055.
