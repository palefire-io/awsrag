VERIDIA BIOSYSTEMS - PRODUCT AVAILABILITY BULLETIN
=====================================================
Reference: VBB-0055
Date Issued: 2025-07-30
Type: Hardware Revision Availability
Affected Product: VeriPrep SP-1 Automated Sample Preparation Station
New hardware revision: SP-1r2
Related: VBB-0038 (barcode/jam advisory), SP-1 firmware 2.3.0

SUBJECT: VeriPrep SP-1r2 now shipping; firmware 2.3.0 released; retrofit kit
         available for r1 units

-----------------------------------------------------
SP-1r2 - WHAT HAS CHANGED
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Tube feed mechanism (SP1-MEC-141). Redesigned feed pawl with a compliant
   tip and a widened tube height acceptance band (68.0 to 82.5 mm capped
   height, against 73.5 to 80.0 mm on r1). This addresses the short-cap 5 mL
   tube interaction described in VBB-0038.

2. Repositioned barcode scanner window, lowered 3 mm and widened 4 mm, with a
   second scan attempt at a 12-degree offset before P-101 is raised.

3. Gripper jaw material changed from acetal to a glass-filled PPS with a
   textured contact face. Expected service interval for SP1-MEC-160 extends
   from 12 to 24 months.

4. Lid interlock switch relocated away from the decontamination spray path.
   This addresses a small number of P-501 false trips reported after
   decontamination cycles.

No change to: footprint, power requirements, throughput (120 tubes/hr),
aliquot volume specification, host protocols, or consumables.

-----------------------------------------------------
VALIDATION DATA
-----------------------------------------------------
5,000 tube presentations, mixed tube population including 1,200 short-cap 5 mL
tubes from three suppliers previously associated with jams:

                          SP-1r1 (FW 2.2.0)   SP-1r2 (FW 2.3.0)
  P-101 barcode no decode      2.4%                0.11%
  P-105 tube height error      3.1%                0.00%
  P-112 feed jam               1.8%                0.02%
  Mean uninterrupted run       142 tubes           4,300 tubes

-----------------------------------------------------
FIRMWARE 2.3.0
-----------------------------------------------------
Released 2025-07-30. Required on SP-1r2. Available for r1 units, where it
provides the second-attempt barcode scan and improved jam recovery but cannot
provide the mechanical tolerance improvement.

IMPORTANT: SP-1 firmware 2.3.0 or later is required for integration with
VeriScan firmware 4.3.0 and above, which uses host protocol version 2. An SP-1
on firmware 2.2.0 or earlier connected to a VeriScan on 4.3.0 will raise P-407
(host protocol version mismatch) and refuse to hand off samples. Plan the two
updates together.

-----------------------------------------------------
RETROFIT FOR EXISTING r1 UNITS
-----------------------------------------------------
SP1-KIT-R2UP, list GBP 1,150.00, lead time 28 days. Includes SP1-MEC-141,
revised harness, scanner bracket, gripper jaw set and the firmware 2.3.0
licence. Fitting requires a Veridia engineer, approximately 6 hours on site,
and a post-fit verification run of 200 tubes.

Sites with an open jam-related ticket raised before 2025-07-30 are eligible for
the retrofit at 50 per cent of list under the goodwill programme agreed by
James Okafor. Contact support quoting the original ticket number.

Note that the r1 tube feed mechanism SP1-MEC-140 remains available as a
like-for-like spare until 2028 for sites that do not wish to retrofit.
