[2025-06-20 11:00] Olu Adeyemi (Reagent Manufacturing Lead):
Looked at the photos. The hairline crack on the collar of cartridge 2 is
consistent with an impact rather than with fitting - it runs radially from the
outer edge, which is what you get from a drop or a crush, not from
over-torquing, which cracks circumferentially.

Question for the site: how are your reagent cartons stored and moved between
your central store and the bench? Any chance they've been stacked more than
three high or moved on a trolley over a threshold?

D5-2025-0158 had 5,000 units and this is the only complaint against it, which
argues against a manufacturing issue.

[2025-06-20 15:30] Suneet Kaur (Senior BMS):

[2025-06-24 16:00] Kasia Nowak (Field Service Engineer):
Attended (FSV-2025-0284). Inspected the instrument reagent bay and cartridge
port - no damage, no misalignment, port seal in good condition. No instrument
fault.

Inspected the central store with the site's stores supervisor. Cartons stacked
six high against a maximum of three marked on the carton. Trolley route
crosses a 40 mm threshold at the loading bay.

Opened and inspected the four remaining cartridges from D5-2025-0158: one has
a hairline collar crack not visible through the carton. Quarantined it.

So: three damaged cartridges out of six from one delivery, all with impact
damage, and a storage and handling route that would explain it.

Recommendations left with the stores supervisor and with Suneet:
- observe the max stack height marked on the carton
- inspect cartridges for collar cracks before fitting, as part of SOP-VBS-002
  section 4.4
- ramp or re-route the trolley path

Time on site 2.0 hours.

[2025-06-25 11:00] Suneet Kaur (Senior BMS):

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: impact damage to cartridge collars from storage stacked six high
(limit three) and trolley transit over a 40 mm threshold. No manufacturing
defect; no instrument fault (FSV-2025-0284).
Actions: storage and handling corrected; pre-fit inspection reinforced against
SOP-VBS-002 s.4.4; three replacement cartridges supplied as goodwill; carton
stack-height marking to be made more prominent.
