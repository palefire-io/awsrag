[2025-03-26 12:10] Danny Whitcombe (Support Engineer):
That'll be it. A burr on the aperture catches fibrin and builds a nucleus for
the next blockage, which is why the soak works temporarily and the problem
returns.

Probe and seal kit need replacing - VS4-MEC-011 and VS4-SP-021. Both are
field-replaceable but the probe alignment afterwards is fiddly and I'd rather
an engineer did it on a single-analyser critical site.

Callum Frazer can attend on 2 April. Can you manage until then with the unblock

[2025-03-26 13:05] Fiona Sutherland (Chief BMS):
We can manage. It's an irritation rather than a crisis.

Out of interest - what causes a burr? We've had this probe since install.

[2025-03-26 14:20] Danny Whitcombe (Support Engineer):
Most often repeated piercing of caps from a tube type with a harder stopper
than the probe was designed around, or occasionally a single event where the
probe met the rim of a mis-seated tube. Two years of routine use shouldn't do
it on its own.

Worth asking whether your tube type changed. It's caught out a few sites.

[2025-04-02 15:40] Callum Frazer (Field Service Engineer):
Attended (FSV-2025-0203). Probe assembly VS4-MEC-011 replaced, seal kit
VS4-SP-021 replaced. Old probe has a visible burr at the 4 o'clock position on
the aperture, consistent with a strike rather than wear - photographed for the
returns record.

Alignment set and verified. Fluidics primed. 40-sample run with no E-104.
Carryover check 0.2%. QC both levels in range.

Asked about tube types: site changed from a 4mL to a 3mL tube in January for
a paediatric-heavy workload. The 3mL tube has a slightly domed stopper and
sits marginally higher. Advised that this is within specification but that the
site should watch for a repeat, and showed them how to check the probe tip
monthly.

Also demonstrated the clot check procedure - two of their staff were running
the Probe Unblock cycle without first checking whether the sample was clotted,
which means a clotted sample can go straight back in and block it again.

Time on site 3.5 hours.

[2025-04-03 09:15] Fiona Sutherland (Chief BMS):
No further E-104 since. The clot check point was well made - we'd got sloppy

Thanks both.

--- Resolution ---
Root cause: burr on the sample probe aperture (strike damage) causing repeated
fibrin accumulation.
Actions: probe and seal kit replaced (FSV-2025-0203); alignment verified; staff
retrained on clot check before running Probe Unblock; monthly probe tip
inspection advised.
