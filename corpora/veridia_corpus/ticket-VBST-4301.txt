--- Resolution ---
Root cause: protein deposit on the flow cell optical faces, probably from
approximately 20 citrate-anticoagulated samples run in error three weeks
earlier. Not recoverable by cleaning.
Actions: flow cell VS4-OPT-220 replaced (FSV-2025-0447); background and
carryover verified; customer advised on sample type checks and on immediate
cleaning after a known contamination event.
Chargeable - out of warranty, Bronze contract, contamination not failure.
