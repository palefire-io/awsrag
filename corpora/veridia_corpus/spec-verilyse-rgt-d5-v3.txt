-----------------------------------------------------------------
INTENDED USE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
RGT-D5 is an isotonic diluent for use with VeriScan 400 and VeriScan 400 Pro
haematology analysers. It dilutes the whole blood sample, provides the
conductive medium for impedance counting of erythrocytes and platelets, and
maintains cell volume during measurement. For in-vitro diagnostic use by
trained laboratory personnel.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
LOT NUMBERING SCHEME
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Format: D5-YYYY-NNNN[/R]

  D5      product code
  YYYY    year of manufacture
  NNNN    sequential fill number within the year, not reset by campaign
  /R      formulation revision suffix, present from revision C onwards
          (revision B lots carry no suffix)

Example: D5-2025-0158/C is the 158th fill of 2025, formulation revision C.

The lot number appears in three places: the cartridge label, the outer carton
label, and the RFID tag read by the instrument. If the three disagree, do not
use the cartridge; quarantine it and raise a support ticket with photographs.

Current released lots are listed in log-reagent-lots-2025.csv.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
STORAGE AND STABILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Unopened, stored 2-25 C          | 18 months from date of manufacture
On board after first pierce      | 90 days
Do not freeze                    | Freezing causes salt precipitation and
                                 | irreversible change in conductivity
Protect from direct sunlight     | Yes
Transport                        | Ambient, -10 to 50 C for up to 14 days

The on-board 90-day limit is enforced by the instrument. E-304 is raised when
the limit is reached. From firmware 4.3.0 the instrument displays a countdown
and warns at 7 days (W-035).

Do not decant, top up, or transfer between cartridges. The cartridge septum
and RFID tag are matched to the fill; a topped-up cartridge will report an
incorrect remaining volume and may allow use beyond the stability limit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Not classified as hazardous under Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP).
No hazard pictogram required. Treat as potentially infectious once it has
contacted a sample.

First aid: eye contact - rinse with water for 10 minutes; skin - wash with
soap and water; ingestion - rinse mouth, seek medical advice if a large
quantity is swallowed.

Disposal: dispose of used diluent and waste as clinical/biohazardous waste in
accordance with local regulations. Unused product may be disposed of to drain
with copious water where permitted locally.

Full safety data sheet: SDS-RGT-D5 rev 4, available at
www.veridiabio.com/sds or on request. Summary in
spec-verilyse-safety-data-summary-v2.txt.
