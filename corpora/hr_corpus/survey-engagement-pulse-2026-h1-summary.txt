-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. HEADLINES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Overall engagement index 71 (H2 2025: 68). The three-point rise is
     driven almost entirely by the Commercial function.

1.2  eNPS +14 (H2 2025: +4). Detractors fell from 24% to 18%.

1.3  Two items fell. "I understand how my pay is decided" dropped to 44%
     favourable (H2 2025: 51%), the lowest score in the survey. "I have the
     tools I need to do my job" fell to 58% in Field Service.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. BY FUNCTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Commercial          184      78      +9     Pay transparency (47%)
Engineering         142      73      +2     Career progression (55%)

Field Service        68      61      -4     Tools and equipment (58%)

2.1  Field Service is the clear outlier and has now declined for three
     consecutive cycles. It is also the only function where "I would
     recommend Veridia as a place to work" is below 50%.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. FREE-TEXT THEMES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Pay transparency dominates, 147 mentions. The common thread is not the
     amount but the absence of explanation: people cannot see how a band is
     set or what moves them through it.

3.2  Field Service tooling, 61 mentions, concentrated on van stock and the
     diagnostic laptop refresh that slipped from Q4.

3.3  Hybrid working, 44 mentions, largely positive; the complaints are about
     inconsistent application between managers rather than the policy.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Publish the levelling guides and band midpoints to all employees. The
     guides already exist and are currently manager-only; that is the
     cheapest available intervention on the lowest-scoring item.

4.2  Field Service tooling: bring the laptop refresh forward and report
     back to that function specifically, by name, within one cycle.

4.3  Do not run another pulse before acting. Two consecutive surveys without
     visible follow-through is the most reliable way to depress the response
     rate permanently.
