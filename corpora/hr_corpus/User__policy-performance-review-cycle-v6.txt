WHAT CHANGED IN VERSION 6
  - Numeric ratings removed. Replaced with four descriptive outcomes.
  - Forced distribution abolished. There is no curve and no quota.
  - Self-assessment shortened from eleven questions to three.
  - Peer feedback made optional rather than mandatory.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CYCLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Continuous     One-to-one at least fortnightly. This is where performance
                 is actually managed. Everything below is administration on
                 top of it.
  April          Mid-year check-in. No paperwork. A conversation about
                 trajectory.
  October        Year-end review. Written.
  November        Calibration.
  December       Outcomes and pay review communicated.
  1 January      Pay changes effective.

Promotion decisions run on their own two windows - March and September - and
are described in User__career-framework-overview-v5.txt section 6.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS ASSESSED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Two things, weighted equally.

  WHAT       Did you deliver what your role and level require? Measured
             against goals agreed at the start of the year and adjusted
             honestly when circumstances changed - and they always change.

  HOW        Did you work in a way consistent with the code of conduct? This
             is not a soft factor. Sustained delivery achieved by making
             colleagues' lives worse does not produce a good review at
             Veridia, and we have acted on this at senior levels.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE FOUR OUTCOMES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  EXCEPTIONAL
    Performance well beyond the expectations of the level, sustained across
    the year. Typically 5-10% of people. There is no cap.

  STRONG
    Doing the job well. Meeting the expectations of the level consistently.
    THIS IS THE NORMAL OUTCOME AND IT IS A GOOD OUTCOME. The majority of
    people at Veridia are here and should be. Version 5 called this "meets
    expectations" and people read it as a criticism, which is why the words
    changed.

  DEVELOPING
    Not yet consistently meeting the expectations of the level. Common and
    entirely appropriate for someone recently promoted or recently joined.
    Not a warning.

  BELOW EXPECTATIONS
    Sustained shortfall against the level. Triggers a documented support plan
    with a defined period and clear success criteria. It should never be a
    surprise - if this appears in your written review and you have not been
    told before, your manager has failed.

3.1  Numeric ratings were removed because six years of data showed managers
     were calibrating to the number rather than describing the performance,
     and because a 3 out of 5 reads as a failure to most people no matter
     what the definition says.

3.2  There is no forced distribution. If everyone in a team is genuinely
     strong, everyone gets Strong. Calibration exists to make the bar
     consistent, not to ration outcomes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT YOU WRITE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Three questions. That is all.

  1. What are you most proud of this year, and why did it matter?
  2. What did not go as you hoped, and what did you take from it?
  3. What do you want to be doing differently in a year, and what do you need
     from us to get there?

Version 5 asked eleven questions across four competency dimensions. It took
people a working day to complete and produced worse conversations, not better
ones.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. FEEDBACK FROM OTHERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  You may nominate up to five colleagues. Your manager may add up to two,
     and must tell you who.

5.2  Optional from version 6. If you would rather not, say so and it will not
     be held against you.

5.3  Feedback is shared with you in substance. It is not anonymous to your
     manager. We do not run anonymous peer review; it produces sharper
     comments and worse outcomes.

5.4  Feedback should be specific and about work. "Sometimes difficult in
     meetings" is not usable. "In the March design review, interrupted three
     times before the proposal was finished" is.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. CALIBRATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Managers within a function meet to compare outcomes, chaired by a
     Director with the People team present.

6.2  Purpose: the bar is the same across teams. A Strong in support should
     mean what a Strong in firmware means.

6.3  The People team checks for patterns by gender, ethnicity, part-time
     status, and family leave taken during the year. Where a pattern appears,
     the panel is asked to justify it. This has changed two outcomes since
     2023.

6.4  Your manager must tell you if calibration changed your proposed outcome,
     and why.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. HOW THIS CONNECTS TO PAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Your review outcome is one input to your pay review. The others are where
     you sit in your band, market movement for your role, and the total budget.

7.2  A Strong outcome does not guarantee an increase, and this is the thing
     people find hardest. Someone at the top of their band performing strongly
     may receive little or nothing, while someone lower in the band performing
     equally strongly receives more. This is how bands work and we would
     rather explain it than obscure it.

7.3  A Below Expectations outcome normally means no increase.

7.4  Bonus, where your role has one, is governed separately.

7.5  Pay outcomes are communicated in December by your manager, in person,
     with the reasoning. Not by email and not by a number in a system.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. IF YOU DISAGREE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Talk to your manager first, then ask the People team for a review. A review
looks at the evidence with fresh eyes and can change the outcome. If you
believe the outcome was affected by discrimination, that is a grievance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. FOR MANAGERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Nothing in a year-end review should be new. If it is, that is your
     failure, not theirs.

9.2  Write about behaviour and evidence, not personality.

9.3  Deliver a difficult outcome in person, early in the day, with time
     afterwards. Do not do it on a Friday afternoon by video call.

9.4  Do not use "budget" as the explanation for a pay outcome you have not
     understood. Ask Denise Kaur to explain it to you first.
