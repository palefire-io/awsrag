WHAT CHANGED IN VERSION 4
  - Pregnancy loss leave extended to either parent and to any gestation,
    removing the previous 24-week threshold.
  - Neonatal care leave introduced.
  - Kinship care leave introduced.
  - Secondary carer leave increased from 4 weeks to 8 weeks (UK).
  - "Primary carer" language replaces maternity/paternity throughout.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRINCIPLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Veridia's family leave is deliberately generous and deliberately
     gender-neutral. We do not have a maternity policy and a lesser paternity
     policy. We have a primary carer entitlement and a secondary carer
     entitlement, and which you are is your family's decision, not ours.

1.2  Two people at Veridia who are both parents of the same child may split
     the primary and secondary roles however they wish, including swapping
     part way through.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. UK ENTITLEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY CARER
  26 weeks at 100% of full pay
  followed by 13 weeks at the statutory rate
  followed by up to 13 weeks unpaid
  Total up to 52 weeks.

  Available from day one of employment. No qualifying service period. This is
  more generous than the statutory position, which requires 26 weeks' service
  for enhanced pay, and it is deliberate: someone who joins us pregnant should
  not be worse off for having changed job.

SECONDARY CARER
  8 weeks at 100% of full pay, taken in blocks of not less than one week,
  within 12 months of the birth or placement.

  This rose from 4 weeks in version 4. The reason is straightforward: the
  evidence that secondary carer leave affects long-run division of care is
  strong, and 2 weeks - the statutory position - is not enough to matter.

ADOPTION, SURROGACY AND FOSTER-TO-ADOPT
  Identical to birth, on identical terms, including the primary/secondary
  split. Leave starts at placement.

SHARED PARENTAL LEAVE
  Available in addition to and interacting with the above. It is genuinely
  complicated. Speak to Rachel Duffy before you plan around it, and we will
  model your options properly rather than hand you a leaflet.

3. SWITZERLAND
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Maternity            20 weeks at 100%   (statutory: 14 weeks at 80%, capped)
  Second parent        8 weeks at 100%    (statutory: 2 weeks at 80%, capped)
  Adoption             Same as birth
  Care of seriously
  ill child            14 weeks at full pay (Betreuungsurlaub)
  Care of sick child   Statutory 3 days per event; Veridia does not cap it in
                       practice

Swiss law prohibits work in the 8 weeks following birth and maternity leave
cannot begin before the birth. Family allowances (Kinderzulagen) are paid
through payroll but must be claimed - see the Swiss benefits handbook.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. PREGNANCY LOSS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  2 weeks paid leave, for either parent, following pregnancy loss at any
     gestation. From day one of employment.

4.2  No medical evidence is required. No form. Tell your manager or the People
     team that you are taking pregnancy loss leave and that is the end of the
     administrative process.

4.3  It is recorded separately from sickness absence and does not count
     towards any absence measure.

4.4  Version 3 applied only from 24 weeks and only to the person who was
     pregnant. That was wrong, it was raised through the speak-up channel in
     2025 by someone who had been affected by it, and it was changed. We are
     grateful to them.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. NEONATAL CARE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Where a baby is admitted to neonatal care within 28 days of birth and stays
for 7 continuous days or more, additional paid leave is available matching the
period of neonatal care, up to 12 weeks. It is in addition to primary and
secondary carer leave and does not shorten either.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. KINSHIP CARE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8 weeks at full pay where you take on the care of a child who is not your own
- most often a relative's child - under a formal or informal arrangement. Speak
to the People team; we will not require a court order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. FERTILITY TREATMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Up to 5 days paid leave per treatment cycle, for the person undergoing
     treatment and for a partner attending appointments.

7.2  Additional flexibility around appointment timing, which in practice
     matters more than the leave. IVF appointments are scheduled at short
     notice and cannot be moved.

7.3  Recorded separately from sickness absence.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHILE YOU ARE ON LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Your benefits continue in full: pension contributions on your normal
     salary, private medical, life assurance, share option vesting.

8.2  Annual leave continues to accrue and may be taken immediately before or
     after your family leave.

8.3  Keeping in Touch (KIT) days: up to 10, optional, paid at normal daily
     rate. Optional means optional.

8.4  You will be included in anything affecting your role - restructures, pay
     reviews, promotion rounds. Being on leave does not remove you from
     consideration for anything, and pay reviews apply to you on the same
     basis as everyone else.

8.5  Your manager should agree with you before you go how much contact you
     want and through what channel. The default is none.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. COMING BACK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  You return to the same job. If that is genuinely not possible after a
     period of more than 26 weeks, you return to a suitable equivalent on the
     same terms.

9.2  Phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours on full pay.

9.3  Flexible working requests from returning parents are approved unless
     there is a substantial business reason not to. In practice we have
     approved every one since 2022.

9.4  Facilities for expressing and storing milk are provided at Cambridge and
     Basel. Ask Facilities; you do not need to explain why.

9.5  Return-to-work coaching (3 sessions) is available on request.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. IF YOU DO NOT RETURN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
There is no clawback of enhanced family pay if you decide not to return. Some
employers require repayment; we do not, and we are not going to start.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
11. HOW TO ARRANGE IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
UK: tell us by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth where you
can. Adoption: within 7 days of being matched. In practice, tell us when you
are ready to.

Contact Rachel Duffy (UK) or Lena Hofstetter (CH). We will write to you
confirming your dates, your pay schedule and what happens to your benefits,
so that you have it in writing and are not relying on a conversation.
