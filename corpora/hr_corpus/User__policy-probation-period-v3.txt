-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. LENGTH
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  UK: six months from your start date, with the substantive decision taken
     at day 90 and confirmed at month six unless extended.

1.2  Switzerland: three months, being the maximum permitted under the Swiss
     Code of Obligations. It cannot be extended beyond three months by
     agreement or otherwise.

1.3  The date of your probation decision is in your contract. If you cannot
     find it, ask the People team rather than guessing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT PROBATION IS FOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  It is a period in which both parties establish whether the appointment
     was the right one. Both. It is not a one-way assessment and you are
     entitled to conclude that the job is not what you were told it was.

2.2  It is assessed against the goals set in your first week and against the
     expectations of your level in the career framework. It is not assessed
     against how quickly you got up to speed relative to someone else.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT HAPPENS DURING IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 7    Manager check-in
  Day 30   Structured review plus People team survey
  Day 60   Honest trajectory conversation
  Day 90   Decision (UK: substantive; CH: final)
  Month 6  Confirmation (UK only)

3.1  THE DAY 60 CONVERSATION IS THE IMPORTANT ONE. If there is a concern, it
     is raised at day 60 with specifics and with what needs to change. A
     manager who raises a new concern for the first time at the decision point
     has not followed this policy, and the People team will say so.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. OUTCOMES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  CONFIRMED. The normal outcome. Your notice period moves to the
     contractual period and you are confirmed in post. In 2025, 47 of 52
     probations were confirmed at the first decision point.

4.2  EXTENDED (UK only). Where more time is genuinely needed - often because
     of extended absence, a change of manager, or a role that changed shape
     under the person. An extension must:
     - be for a defined period, normally three months and never more than six;
     - state in writing what needs to be demonstrated and how it will be
       measured;
     - be agreed with the People team before it is put to you.

     An extension is not a soft way of avoiding a difficult conversation. Two
     of the five extensions in 2025 were refused by the People team for
     exactly that reason and the managers were asked to make a decision.

4.3  NOT CONFIRMED. Employment ends with notice as set out in your contract
     (UK: one week during probation; Switzerland: seven days during the
     probationary period). This is not a disciplinary outcome and is not
     recorded as one.

     You are entitled to:
     - a meeting at which the decision is explained;
     - to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative;
     - a written summary of the reasons;
     - a factual reference confirming dates and job title.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT PROBATION DOES NOT AFFECT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
You have full access from day one to:
  - Annual leave, accrued and bookable
  - Statutory and company sick pay per the sick pay policy
  - Pension auto-enrolment (UK) or BVG enrolment (CH)
  - Private medical insurance and all other benefits
  - Family leave entitlements
  - The Employee Assistance Programme
  - The grievance procedure and the speak-up route
  - Learning and development budget

Nothing is held back during probation. We are occasionally asked whether it is
"safe" to take sick leave or raise a grievance during probation. It is. Using
an entitlement is never a legitimate reason for a probation failure and any
suggestion that it might be should be reported to the People team immediately.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. SPECIAL CASES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  ABSENCE DURING PROBATION. Significant absence may be grounds for
     extension in the UK. It is never grounds in itself for non-confirmation.
     Where absence relates to a disability, we consider reasonable adjustments
     first and the probation second.

6.2  CHANGE OF MANAGER. If your manager changes during probation, the new
     manager takes over the assessment and must review the previous manager's
     notes with you. A probation decision may not be taken by a manager who
     has managed you for less than four weeks without People team involvement.

6.3  INTERNAL MOVES. An internal move to a new role carries a three-month
     settling-in period, not a probation. Your continuous service and
     contractual notice are unaffected, and if the new role does not work out
     the first option considered is a return to a comparable role.

6.4  TUPE TRANSFERS AND ACQUIRED EMPLOYEES. Employees who join Veridia through
     a business transfer bring their continuous service with them and are not
     placed on probation. Their existing terms transfer with them.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. FOR MANAGERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Set month one goals before the start date. Book the day 30, 60 and 90
     conversations before the start date too.

7.2  Write things down as you go. A probation decision defended on memory
     alone is a decision that will not survive scrutiny.

7.3  Talk to the People team the moment you have a doubt, not a fortnight
     before the decision. Early involvement almost always produces a better
     outcome for everyone, including a confirmed one.
