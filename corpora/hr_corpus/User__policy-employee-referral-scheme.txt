1. THE AWARD
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Standard roles          GBP 2,000 / CHF 2,200
  Hard-to-fill roles      GBP 3,500 / CHF 3,800
  Contractor conversion   GBP 1,000 / CHF 1,100

The hard-to-fill list is published on the intranet and reviewed quarterly. As
at January 2026 it contains: Field Service Engineer (all regions, and
especially Scotland and the South West), Firmware Engineer, Regulatory Affairs
Specialist, and QC Analyst (Basel).

Paid in two halves: 50% when your referral passes probation, 50% at twelve
months. Paid through payroll and subject to tax and NI in the normal way.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO REFER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Send the name and CV to referrals@veridiabio.com, or submit through
     PeopleHub, BEFORE they apply. A referral logged after someone has already
     applied through another route does not qualify.

2.2  ASK THEM FIRST. Sending someone's CV without their knowledge is a data
     protection problem and an awkward phone call for the recruiter.

2.3  Tell us honestly what you think. A referral that says "brilliant engineer,
     struggles with ambiguity, would need a structured team" is worth ten that
     say "great person".

2.4  You may refer as many people as you like. The record is four in one year.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHO QUALIFIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Eligible to refer: all employees except the Talent Acquisition team, the
People team, and the hiring manager for that specific role.

Eligible to be referred: anyone not already in our applicant tracking system
within the last 6 months, and not currently employed by Veridia.

Former Veridia employees CAN be referred and the award is payable. We rehire
and we are pleased when it happens.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Referred candidates go through exactly the same process: same interviews,
     same structured questions, same scoring rubric, same decision.

4.2  A referral gets your candidate a guaranteed CV review by a human, and a
     response within 5 working days. That is the entire advantage and it is a
     real one.

4.3  You will not be told the detail of why a referral was unsuccessful. That
     is your friend's information, not yours. They may tell you themselves.

4.4  You cannot sit on the interview panel for someone you referred.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. A HONEST NOTE ABOUT REFERRALS AND DIVERSITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Referral schemes tend to reproduce the existing shape of a company, because
people know people like themselves. Ours is no exception - 31% of our hires in
2025 came through referral, and that cohort was less diverse on every measure
we track than our direct applicants.

We are not going to close the scheme, because it produces good hires and good
retention. What we do instead:
  - Referred candidates go through the same structured process, with no short
    cuts, which is where the real risk of bias would otherwise sit.
  - We monitor referral hiring separately in our diversity data.
  - We would particularly encourage referrals into field service and firmware,
    which are our two least diverse functions, from anyone whose network does
    not look like our current team.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
"My referral was hired into a different role than I referred them for."
  You still get the award, at the rate for the role they actually took.

"They were hired six months after I referred them."
  Referrals are live for 12 months. You still get the award.

"Two of us referred the same person."
  Whoever logged it first. If it was genuinely simultaneous we split it.

"They left after eight months."
  You keep the first 50%. The second instalment is not paid.

"My referral was made redundant before twelve months."
  Both instalments are paid.

"Can I refer my partner / brother / housemate?"
  Yes. Declare the relationship to the People team so that the conflict of
  interest register is accurate, and you will not be involved in the process.
  See User__code-of-conduct-v4.txt section 5.
