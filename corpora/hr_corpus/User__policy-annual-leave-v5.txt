1. YOUR ENTITLEMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
UNITED KINGDOM
  27 days plus 8 bank holidays on joining
  28 days from 3 years' service
  29 days from 5 years
  30 days from 8 years (maximum)

SWITZERLAND
  25 days on joining
  27 days from 5 years' service
  30 days from 10 years (maximum)
  Plus Basel-Stadt cantonal public holidays

Additional service days apply from 1 January following the anniversary, not
from the anniversary itself.

Part-time: pro-rated on your contracted days, including bank holidays. If you
work Monday to Wednesday you receive a proportion of bank holidays whether or
not they fall on your working days, so that you are neither advantaged nor
disadvantaged by the days you happen to work.

Leave year: 1 January to 31 December.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. YOUR BIRTHDAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
You get your birthday off and it is not deducted from your entitlement.

If it falls at a weekend or on a public holiday, take another day that week.
If operational cover means you cannot take it on the day, take it within the
month. If you would rather work on your birthday, take a different day - we
are not going to police this.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. BOOKING LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Book in PeopleHub. Your manager approves.

3.2  Notice we ask for:
       1 to 2 days      as much as you can manage
       3 to 5 days      2 weeks
       1 to 2 weeks     4 weeks
       More than 2 wks  8 weeks

     These are requests, not conditions. Life does not schedule itself. If you
     need a day tomorrow, ask for a day tomorrow.

3.3  A manager may decline leave only for a genuine operational reason, must
     say what it is, and must offer alternative dates. "The team is busy" is
     not by itself a reason; the team is always busy.

3.4  You may not be required to cancel booked leave except in a genuine
     emergency, and if you are, Veridia covers any non-refundable costs you
     lose. This has happened twice in nine years and both times we paid.

3.5  Approval is not needed to take leave you are entitled to. Approval is
     about timing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. COVER AND THE FIELD SERVICE ROTA
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Teams agree their own cover. The rule is that cover exists, not that a
     particular person provides it.

4.2  Field service, support and manufacturing operate a rota because they must.
     Rotas are published a quarter ahead and leave requests inside the rota
     are handled by the team lead. If the rota is systematically preventing
     people taking leave, that is a resourcing problem and Priya Anand or
     James Okafor want to hear about it.

4.3  Christmas and New Year: Cambridge and Basel offices close between
     Christmas and New Year. The days between the public holidays ARE deducted
     from your entitlement - this is not additional leave, and it is the
     single most misunderstood line in this policy. Field service and support
     maintain an on-call rota through the period, compensated per the on-call
     arrangement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. CARRY OVER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  UK: up to 5 days may be carried to 31 March. Anything beyond that is lost
     unless you were prevented from taking it by long-term sickness, family
     leave, or an operational refusal by your manager - in which case it
     carries and no one has to argue about it.

5.2  Switzerland: statutory leave should be taken in the year it is earned.
     Carry over is permitted by agreement but the legal position is that
     untaken leave does not simply expire, so we manage it actively rather
     than letting balances accumulate. Lena Hofstetter reviews balances
     quarterly.

5.3  We do not pay in lieu of untaken leave except on termination. This is not
     meanness; leave exists so that you rest.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. BUYING EXTRA DAYS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Buy up to 5 additional days, once a year, in the January window.

6.2  Cost is your daily rate, deducted evenly across the year. Salary
     sacrifice, so it costs slightly less than the headline.

6.3  Bought days cannot be carried over or refunded. If you leave mid-year,
     unused bought days are refunded in your final pay.

6.4  We do not operate a sell-back scheme.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. TAKING LEAVE PROPERLY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  We expect you to take all of it. In 2025, 34 UK employees left days on
     the table and that is a failure of ours as much as a choice of theirs.

7.2  While on leave you are not expected to read email, respond to messages,
     or join calls. Your manager should not contact you except in a genuine
     emergency, and "a customer is asking" is not one.

7.3  Managers: you set this by example. A manager who answers email from a
     beach teaches their team that leave is negotiable.

7.4  Switzerland: at least two weeks must be taken consecutively (Art. 329c
     CO). This is law, not preference.

7.5  If you have not booked anything by 30 June, your manager will ask you
     about it. This is not a reprimand; it is a prompt.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. OTHER LEAVE THAT IS NOT ANNUAL LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Do not use annual leave for any of these:

  Sickness             See User__policy-sick-pay-and-absence-v5.txt
  Family leave         See User__policy-family-leave-v4.txt
  Bereavement          Up to 10 days paid, more by agreement
  Pregnancy loss       2 weeks paid, either parent, any gestation
  Fertility treatment  Up to 5 days paid per cycle
  Dependant emergency  Reasonable unpaid time off is a statutory right in the
                       UK. In practice Veridia pays it and we have never
                       counted it.
  Jury service         Paid, difference between jury allowance and salary
  Public duties        Reasonable paid time off - magistrate, school governor,
                       reservist
  Reservist training   Up to 15 days paid annually
  Moving house         1 day paid, once a year
  Volunteering         2 days paid a year
  Religious observance Speak to your manager. We will accommodate where we
                       can, and this often works better as flexible hours than
                       as leave.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. UNPAID LEAVE AND SABBATICALS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Unpaid leave up to 4 weeks: agreed with your manager and the People team.

9.2  Sabbatical: after 4 years' service you may request up to 3 months unpaid,
     once every 4 years. Benefits continue. Your role is held open. Requests
     go to your Director and are rarely refused - 6 of 7 requests since 2022
     were approved and the one refusal was about timing, not principle.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. LEAVE ON JOINING AND LEAVING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Accrues from day one at 1/12 of your annual entitlement per month. You
     may book from day one and you do not have to wait for probation.

10.2 You may go slightly into deficit in your first year with your manager's
     agreement.

10.3 On leaving, accrued untaken leave is paid in your final pay. Leave taken
     in excess of accrual is deducted.
