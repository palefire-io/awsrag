1. THE PRINCIPLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Spend Veridia's money as you would spend your own, if you were reasonably
well off and in a hurry. Do not be extravagant. Do not be miserly at the
expense of your own health or safety, particularly if you are a field engineer
driving home at nine at night.

Where this policy is silent, apply that test and expect to be supported.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SUBMITTING A CLAIM
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Through Expensify, within 60 days of the expense. Claims older than 90
     days need CFO approval and are sometimes refused.

2.2  Receipts required for everything over GBP 25 / CHF 25. Photograph them at
     the time; a fortnight later you will not have them.

2.3  Approved by your manager, then Finance. Reimbursed with the next payroll
     run, or within 10 working days for claims over GBP 500 where you ask.

2.4  IF YOU ARE LEAVING, SUBMIT EVERYTHING BEFORE YOUR LAST DAY. Claims after
     departure are genuinely difficult.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. TRAVEL - GETTING THERE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
RAIL
  Standard class as default. First class permitted where it is cheaper than
  standard on the day (which happens more than people expect), or on a journey
  over 3 hours where you need to work, or where you are travelling to a
  customer meeting starting within an hour of arrival.
  Book in advance where you can. Field service: we know you cannot.
  Season tickets and railcards funded where they pay back within a year.

AIR
  Economy for flights under 5 hours.
  Premium economy permitted over 5 hours, or where you land and work the same
  day.
  Business class needs Director approval and is rarely given. It has been
  given for a 14-hour flight to a customer escalation.
  Book 14 days ahead where possible.

CAR
  Field service engineers have company vehicles. Fuel card provided. Personal
  mileage must be declared monthly.
  Own car on business: 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles, 25p after
  (UK, HMRC rates); CHF 0.70 per km (CH).
  YOU MUST HAVE BUSINESS USE ON YOUR INSURANCE. Check. Commuting cover is not
  business cover and your insurer will decline a claim.
  Taxis and ride-hailing: fine for airport transfers, late-night travel,
  heavy equipment, or where public transport is impractical.

DRIVING SAFETY - NOT NEGOTIABLE
  Do not drive more than 4 hours without a 30-minute break.
  Do not drive more than 8 hours in a day on business.
  Do not start a drive home after 21:00 following a full working day. Stay
  over. Veridia pays. This is a standing instruction from Priya Anand and it
  overrides any cost consideration and any customer's convenience.
  Do not take a call while driving, hands-free or otherwise, if it requires
  concentration. Pull over.
  Report every incident, however minor, the same day.

4. ACCOMMODATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  UK, outside London        up to GBP 140 per night
  London                    up to GBP 210 per night
  Switzerland               up to CHF 220 per night
  Rest of Europe            up to EUR 190 per night
  US                        up to USD 260 per night

Room and breakfast. Book through the travel system where you can.

Over the cap because of an event or a city with nothing available? Book it,
explain it in the claim, and it will be approved. The cap is guidance, not a
wall.

Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel: claim GBP 30 / CHF 30 per
night as a thank-you, no receipt needed.

5. MEALS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Actual cost, receipted, within these guides per day when away overnight:

  UK          GBP 45     (breakfast 10, lunch 12, dinner 30 if not provided)
  Switzerland CHF 60
  Europe      EUR 50
  US          USD 65

Alcohol: one drink with an evening meal is claimable. Beyond that, no. Rounds
at a bar are not claimable.

Meals while not staying overnight are not claimable unless you are entertaining
a customer or the journey exceeds 8 hours.

Team meals need manager approval. Anything over GBP 500 needs Director
approval.

6. CUSTOMER ENTERTAINMENT - READ THIS CAREFULLY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Most of our customers are publicly funded healthcare organisations. The
     rules for them are stricter than for commercial customers and the
     reputational risk runs one way.

6.2  Acceptable: modest hospitality directly attached to a legitimate business
     purpose. Lunch at a customer meeting. Refreshments at a user group.
     Dinner with a customer team at a conference.

6.3  Requires prior approval from your Director AND Sunita Raval (General
     Counsel): anything beyond a meal, anything involving travel or
     accommodation for a customer, anything at a sporting or entertainment
     event, anything over GBP 75 per head.

6.4  NEVER acceptable: cash or vouchers; anything to an individual involved in
     an active procurement decision; anything at all while a tender is live;
     anything you would be uncomfortable seeing described in a freedom of
     information response.

6.5  All customer entertainment is recorded in the hospitality register held
     by the General Counsel, regardless of value.

6.6  If in doubt, ask before. Nobody has ever been criticised for asking.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. TRAVEL SAFETY AND WELFARE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Lone working: field engineers use the check-in system for any visit
     outside normal hours or to an unfamiliar site.

8.2  Travel insurance covers all business travel including medical
     repatriation. Card in your wallet; policy number on the intranet.

8.3  You may decline to travel somewhere you consider unsafe. Say so. This has
     never been questioned.

8.4  If you feel unsafe at a customer site - equipment, environment or
     behaviour - leave. Ring your manager afterwards, not before. This
     includes harassment by customer staff, which is covered by
     User__policy-grievance-and-dignity-at-work-v4.txt section 6.5.

8.5  Overnight travel counts as working time for the purpose of rest periods
     even where it is not paid time.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. WHAT GETS REJECTED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The genuinely common ones:
  - No receipt over the threshold
  - Personal items bought on a company card
  - Alcohol beyond one drink
  - Parking and speeding fines
  - Commuting to your normal place of work
  - Claims over 90 days old
  - Customer entertainment without prior approval

If your claim is rejected you will be told why by a person, not by the system.
