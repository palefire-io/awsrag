-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY THIS GUIDE EXISTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  The employee-facing policy tells people what happens and when. It
     deliberately does not tell them how we weigh the decision, because that
     would invite the process to be gamed.

1.2  Use this guide alongside the policy. Where the two appear to conflict,
     the policy governs what you tell the employee and this guide governs
     how you reach the decision.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE DAY 90 CHECKPOINT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Day 90 is a substantive decision point, not a formality. By then you
     should be able to answer: would I hire this person again, knowing what
     I now know?

2.2  If the answer is no, do not defer to month six hoping it resolves. In
     the last two years every probation failure we reviewed had a documented
     day 90 concern that was not acted on.

2.3  Three outcomes are available: confirm, extend once by up to three
     months with a written improvement plan, or terminate within the
     probationary notice period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHEN TO EXTEND RATHER THAN TERMINATE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Extend where the shortfall is capability that is plausibly closeable in
     the time available, and where the employee has had clear feedback.

3.2  Do not extend where the concern is conduct, honesty, or a pattern of
     unexplained absence. Those are not capability matters and extension
     sends the wrong signal.

3.3  Do not extend twice. A second extension has never resulted in a
     confirmed appointment in our records and creates real legal exposure.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. BEFORE YOU ACT - MANDATORY CHECKS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Speak to your HR Business Partner before any termination decision. This
     is not optional and protects you as much as the company.

4.2  Check for any live occupational health referral, reasonable adjustment
     request, grievance, or pregnancy or family leave notification. Any of
     these changes the process materially and HR must be involved first.

4.3  Check the employee's start date against the correct jurisdiction. Swiss
     probation is three months by default with seven days' notice; the UK
     arrangement is six months. Applying the wrong one is the single most
     common error we see.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. RECORD KEEPING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Write up each review within two working days, in Workday, in the
     employee's own words where you are recording what they said.

5.2  Assume everything you write will be read back to you in a tribunal.
     Write accurately, and only about performance.
