1. WHAT YOU GET
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Cover from your start date. No waiting period, no probation exclusion.
  - Family cover included at no cost to you: partner and children under 21,
    or under 24 in full-time education.
  - No excess. You do not pay the first anything.
  - Medical history disregarded. Pre-existing conditions ARE covered.
  - Full outpatient cover, uncapped.
  - Mental health: up to 25 therapy sessions a year, plus inpatient and
    day-patient psychiatric treatment.
  - Cancer cover, including drugs not routinely available on the NHS.
  - Digital GP, typically same-day appointments, 8am to 8pm.
  - Physiotherapy, osteopathy and chiropractic on open referral.
  - Dental and optical cash plan.
  - Travel cover for business travel worldwide, including the US.

1.1  "Medical history disregarded" deserves a note. Most corporate schemes
     exclude conditions you already had when you joined. Ours does not. It
     costs meaningfully more and we buy it deliberately, because a scheme that
     excludes what you already have is least useful to the people who most
     need it. It is reviewed at every renewal and has survived four.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS NOT COVERED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Stated plainly so you do not find out at the point of need.

  - Chronic condition management. Private medical insurance covers acute
    conditions - things that respond to treatment and resolve. Ongoing
    management of a chronic condition returns to the NHS once it is stable.
    This is standard across the whole industry and is the single most common
    cause of surprise.
  - Emergency and accident treatment. Call 999 and go to A&E.
  - Routine pregnancy and childbirth. Complications may be covered.
  - Cosmetic procedures unless reconstructive following covered treatment.
  - Fertility treatment. Veridia provides leave for it but the insurance does
    not fund it.
  - Gender-affirming surgery is currently excluded by the insurer. We have
    raised this at the last two renewals and have not yet succeeded in
    changing it. We are telling you rather than letting you discover it.
  - Experimental or unproven treatments.
  - Self-inflicted injury, and treatment arising from alcohol or substance
    misuse other than the addiction treatment benefit.

3. TAX
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  A taxable benefit in kind. It appears on your P11D and is usually
     collected through your tax code.

3.2  Typical annual cost to you:
       Basic rate taxpayer, single      about GBP 240
       Basic rate taxpayer, family      about GBP 500
       Higher rate taxpayer, single     about GBP 480
       Higher rate taxpayer, family     about GBP 1,000

3.3  You may decline the benefit entirely, in which case there is no tax
     charge. Tell the People team. You may not exchange it for cash.

3.4  You may decline family cover and keep individual cover, which reduces the
     tax charge. A handful of people do this where a partner has better cover
     through their own employer.

4. HOW TO USE IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  For most treatment, you do NOT need a GP referral first. Bupa operates
     open referral for physiotherapy, mental health and several other pathways -
     ring them and they will direct you.

4.2  For consultant treatment: see your GP, get a referral, then ring Bupa
     BEFORE booking anything. Pre-authorisation is required and treatment
     booked without it may not be covered.

     Bupa members: 0345 609 0111. Quote policy 84471209.

4.3  Bupa Anytime HealthLine, 24/7 nurse advice: 0345 607 0007.

4.4  Digital GP through the Bupa Blua Health app.

5. YOUR PRIVACY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
This section exists because it is the most frequent question.

5.1  Veridia is NOT told that you have claimed, what for, or what the outcome
     was.

5.2  Veridia receives an anonymised annual claims summary at renewal: total
     cost, broad categories, and volume. It contains no names and no
     department breakdown. It exists so that we can negotiate the renewal.

5.3  Your manager is told nothing.

5.4  Your medical information sits with Bupa, who are the data controller for
     it. Their privacy notice governs it, not ours.

5.5  If you need time off for treatment, that is a sickness absence
     conversation with your manager and you do not have to say what the
     treatment is.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. FAMILY MEMBERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Add or remove family members at any time through the People team. Adding
     a new partner or a new child is not restricted to a renewal window.

6.2  Tell us within 3 months of a change in your household so cover is
     accurate.

6.3  Children are covered to 21, or to 24 in full-time education.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHEN YOU LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Cover ends on your last working day, for you and your family.

7.2  Bupa offers a continuation personal policy without a fresh medical
     underwriting exercise IF you apply within 30 days. This preserves your
     medical history disregarded status, which you will not get from a new
     policy elsewhere. It is at your own cost.

7.3  If anyone in your family is mid-treatment when you leave, ring Bupa
     before your last day. Continuity of an in-progress course of treatment
     can usually be arranged and cannot be arranged retrospectively.
