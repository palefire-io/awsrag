1. THE SCHEME
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Defined contribution. You and Veridia pay in, the money is invested, and
     the pot is yours. There is no promised income at retirement.

1.2  Auto-enrolment from day one. No waiting period, no probation exclusion.

1.3  Contributions are calculated on your FULL BASE SALARY, not on the band of
     "qualifying earnings" that the statutory minimum uses. This is worth
     substantially more than it sounds - on a GBP 45,000 salary, a scheme
     using qualifying earnings would base contributions on roughly GBP 38,700.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONTRIBUTION RATES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  You pay    Veridia pays    Total into your pot
  --------   ------------    -------------------
  4%         8%              12%
  5%         9%              14%
  6%         10%             16%
  7%         10%             17%
  8%+        10%             18%+

Veridia matches you up to a 10% employer cap. The best value point is 6%: the
last matched pound is at 6%, and above that you are contributing without
additional match.

WORTH READING TWICE: if you contribute 4% you receive 8%. If you contribute 6%
you receive 10%. Going from 4% to 6% costs you 2% of salary and gains you 4%.
As at January 2026, 88 of 214 UK employees were contributing 4% and most had
not made a deliberate decision about it. If you can afford the change, make
it - it is the highest-return two minutes available to you at this company.

Change your rate any time through PeopleHub. No limit on changes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. SALARY SACRIFICE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Default arrangement. Your gross salary is reduced by your contribution
     and Veridia pays the whole amount into your pension.

3.2  You save income tax AND National Insurance on the contribution, rather
     than income tax alone.

3.3  Veridia also saves employer National Insurance. WE PAY 100% OF THAT
     SAVING INTO YOUR POT. Many employers keep it. We do not, and this is why
     the effective employer contribution is slightly higher than the headline
     percentages above.

3.4  Things to know before you decide:
     - Salary sacrifice reduces your gross salary, which can affect mortgage
       affordability assessments, statutory maternity pay calculations, and
       some state benefits. Lenders vary; most now take the pre-sacrifice
       figure but not all.
     - It cannot reduce your pay below National Minimum Wage.
     - Life assurance and income protection are calculated on your
       pre-sacrifice salary, so they are unaffected.

3.5  You may opt out of sacrifice and contribute by relief-at-source instead.
     Ask the People team. Roughly a dozen people do, mostly for mortgage
     reasons.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Default fund: Aviva My Future Focus, a lifestyle strategy that shifts
     from growth assets towards lower-volatility assets as you approach your
     selected retirement age.

4.2  The default assumes retirement at 65. IF YOUR PLANS DIFFER, CHANGE IT -
     the glide path is driven by that date and a wrong date produces a wrong
     asset allocation. It takes two minutes in the Aviva portal.

4.3  Alternative funds available, including ethical and Sharia-compliant
     options.

4.4  About 79% of our members are in the default. That is normal and is not
     wrong.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. TAX
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Annual allowance: GBP 60,000 for most people, including employer
     contributions. Unused allowance can be carried forward three years.

5.2  Tapered annual allowance may apply if your adjusted income exceeds
     GBP 260,000. This affects a small number of colleagues. If you think it
     might apply to you, get advice - Veridia cannot help you with it and
     getting it wrong produces a tax charge.

5.3  Money purchase annual allowance of GBP 10,000 applies if you have already
     flexibly accessed a pension elsewhere. TELL US IF THIS APPLIES TO YOU. If
     you do not, contributions may be made that generate a charge for you.

5.4  Lifetime allowance was abolished in April 2024 and replaced by lump sum
     allowances. If you have protections from the old regime, take advice
     before changing your contributions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. YOUR BENEFICIARIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Complete an expression of wish in the Aviva portal telling the trustees
     who should receive your pot if you die.

6.2  It is not legally binding but trustees follow it in almost every case.

6.3  Without one, trustees decide from what they can find out, which is slower
     and harder for your family at the worst possible time.

6.4  Review it after any significant life change - marriage, separation, a
     child, a bereavement.

6.5  This is separate from the death in service life assurance nomination,
     which is a different form. Do both.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. OPTING OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  You may opt out. If you do so within one month of enrolment,
     contributions already made are refunded.

7.2  You will be automatically re-enrolled roughly every three years, as the
     law requires. You may opt out again.

7.3  Opting out means declining an 8-10% employer contribution. We will ask
     you once whether you are sure and then respect your decision.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. TRANSFERS IN AND PREVIOUS PENSIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  You may transfer previous pots into the Aviva scheme. Aviva handles the
     process.

8.2  Take advice first. Some older schemes carry guarantees - guaranteed
     annuity rates, safeguarded benefits - that are valuable and are lost on
     transfer. Transfers from defined benefit schemes over GBP 30,000 require
     regulated advice by law.

8.3  BE ALERT TO PENSION SCAMS. Nobody legitimate cold-calls about pensions.
     Veridia will never ask you to move your pension anywhere. If you are
     approached, check the FCA register and tell the People team.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. WHEN YOU LEAVE VERIDIA
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions stop with your final pay. Your pot stays invested with Aviva and
remains yours. Aviva will write to you. You may leave it, transfer it, or
consolidate it later. There is no rush and no deadline.
