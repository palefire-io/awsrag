-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PAY REVIEW BUDGET 2026
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  The Committee approved an overall increase budget of 3.4% of paybill
     for the April 2026 review, against an original proposal of 3.8%.

1.2  The reduction reflects the Narwhal transaction timetable. The Committee
     was explicit that this is a timing decision and not a signal on
     performance, and asked that the October cycle be revisited if the
     transaction does not complete by 30 September 2026.

1.3  Within the budget, the Committee approved a ring-fenced 0.4% for
     targeted correction of below-range salaries, prioritising Field Service
     where the H1 engagement pulse shows sustained decline.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. EXECUTIVE REMUNERATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  The Committee approved no increase to executive base salaries for 2026,
     on the Chair's recommendation, given the reduced company-wide budget.

2.2  The 2025 annual bonus outturn was approved at 62% of maximum against a
     formulaic outcome of 71%. The Committee applied downward discretion of
     nine percentage points, citing the FSN-2025-003 reagent recall and the
     associated notified body findings.

2.3  The CPO was asked to confirm that the discretion applied is disclosable
     in the same terms if the transaction proceeds.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. RETENTION ARRANGEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  The Committee reviewed the key person retention schedule and approved
     awards for eleven individuals, two more than proposed. The two
     additions are in Regulatory Affairs, where the Committee judged the
     loss risk to be understated.

3.2  Awards vest on the earlier of transaction completion plus twelve months
     or 31 December 2027. The Committee declined to add a performance
     underpin, noting that retention awards with performance conditions have
     historically failed to retain.

3.3  Individual award values are recorded in the retention schedule and are
     not repeated in this note.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. MATTERS FOR THE BOARD
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  The gender pay gap figure for 2025 will be published in April and has
     widened by 0.4 percentage points. The Committee asked that the Board
     see the narrative before publication.

4.2  The Committee noted the pension contribution increase already approved
     under delegated authority and had no further comment.
