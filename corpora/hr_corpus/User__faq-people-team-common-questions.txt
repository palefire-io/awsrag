PAY AND REWARD
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What band am I in and where do I sit in it?
A: Ask your manager. You are entitled to know both, and to know the range for
   your own level. If you do not get a straight answer, ask Denise Kaur and
   you will get one.

Q: Why are pay bands not published to everyone?
A: This is explained in full at User__career-framework-overview-v5.txt section
   8, including our acknowledgement that it is a compromise. It is reviewed
   annually.

Q: When does pay change?
A: 1 January for the annual review. Promotions take effect 1 May or
   1 November. Off-cycle adjustments happen but are rare and need Chief People
   Officer approval.

Q: I got a Strong review and no pay rise. Why?
A: Most often because you sit at or near the top of your band. Your manager
   should have explained this. If they did not, ask them, and if you still do
   not understand it ask Denise Kaur.

Q: I have a competing offer. What should I do?
A: Tell your manager before you resign, not after. Veridia does not routinely
   make counter-offers and a promotion will not be created to retain you, but
   if your pay is genuinely wrong we would rather fix it than lose you.

Q: Am I allowed to discuss my pay with colleagues?
A: Yes. Nothing in your contract prevents it and any clause purporting to
   would be unenforceable. We would rather you had accurate information than
   inaccurate assumptions.

LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Do the days between Christmas and New Year come out of my entitlement?
A: Yes. The offices close but those are your days. This is the single most
   misunderstood line in any of our policies.

Q: Can I book leave in my probation period?
A: Yes, from day one.

Q: How much notice do I need to give?
A: See the annual leave policy. Those are requests, not conditions. If you
   need a day tomorrow, ask for a day tomorrow.

Q: My manager refused my leave. Can they do that?
A: Only for a genuine operational reason, which they must state, and they
   must offer alternative dates. "We are busy" is not a reason. Tell the
   People team if you think it is being used as one.

Q: What happens to leave I have not taken by 31 December?
A: UK: up to 5 days carry to 31 March. Switzerland: managed actively by Lena
   Hofstetter because Swiss law works differently.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
BEING UNWELL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Do I need a doctor's note?
A: UK: not for the first 7 calendar days. Switzerland: from the 4th
   consecutive day.

Q: Do I have to tell my manager what is wrong with me?
A: No. "I am unwell" is a complete answer.

Q: Does my manager see my occupational health report?
A: Only with your consent, and you see it first and may comment on it before
   it is released.

Q: Is mental ill health treated the same as physical illness?
A: Yes, in certification, in pay and in how it is managed.

Q: Will using the EAP show up anywhere?
A: No. Veridia receives anonymous aggregate numbers only and is never told
   that you personally used it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WORKING PATTERN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How many days do I have to be in the office?
A: Two for most office-based roles, agreed within your team. Some roles are
   site-dependent and some are field-based; the hybrid working policy is
   honest about which.

Q: Can I work from abroad?
A: Up to 20 working days a year with approval, 4 weeks' notice. The approval
   is about tax and social security, not trust.

Q: Can I go part-time or compressed hours?
A: Ask. 17 of 18 formal flexible working requests in 2025 were approved.

Q: Is anyone monitoring whether I am at my desk?
A: No. We do not use activity tracking software and will not.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
CAREER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I get promoted?
A: See the career framework and your levelling guide. Two windows a year, no
   quota, and you can nominate yourself.

Q: Do I have to manage people to progress past L3?
A: No. That was true until version 5 of the framework and it was the most
   common complaint in the 2024 survey. There is now an IC track at L4 and
   above on the same band.

Q: Can I apply for an internal role without telling my manager?
A: Yes. Please tell them before the final stage.

Q: Can my manager block my internal move?
A: No. They can ask for a handover period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
LEAVING AND JOINING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What is my notice period?
A: In your contract. Do not rely on memory - it varies by level and by
   country.

Q: What happens to my share options when I leave?
A: Read your option agreement. There is normally a 90-day window to exercise
   vested options and it cannot be extended. This is the thing leavers most
   often get wrong.

Q: Can I come back after leaving?
A: Yes. No waiting period. Four of our 2025 hires were former employees.

Q: Will my manager see my exit interview?
A: Not attributably, and not without your explicit agreement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPANY QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How many people work at Veridia?
A: 318 as at 31 December 2025. About two thirds in the UK, one third in Basel,
   plus a small number of fully remote colleagues elsewhere.

Q: Is Veridia profitable?
A: Revenue was GBP 42M in FY2025. We are Series D funded and financial
   performance is covered at the quarterly all-hands, where you can ask Gary
   Pemberton directly.

Q: What is the FSN-2025-003 thing everyone mentions in induction?
A: A reagent recall in February 2025. A batch of lyse reagent developed a
   fault producing spurious white cell flags on the VeriScan 400. We recalled
   21 lots. It comes up in induction because of how it was found - a customer
   said "this does not look right" and a support engineer took it seriously -
   and because of what we changed afterwards.

Q: There are rumours about an acquisition. Is something happening?
A: We are asked some version of this most quarters, and it has come up more
   often in the last few months.

   Veridia does not comment on market speculation, and the People team is not
   in a position to confirm or deny anything of that kind. What we can tell
   you is this: if anything were ever to happen that affected people's jobs,
   employees would be told properly and directly by the Executive Committee,
   not through the grapevine, and there are legal obligations about
   consultation that we would follow.

   If you hear something that worries you, ask your manager or ask us. We will
   tell you what we can, including when the answer is that we cannot say
   anything. We would rather do that than pretend we have not heard the
   question.

Q: Where do I find a policy?
A: Intranet, People section. Every policy lists its owner, and the owner would
   rather you asked them than guessed.
