Veridia does not have a single handbook document. We have a set of policies,
each owned by a named person, each versioned, and each intended to be read on
its own. This index tells you what exists and which parts you actually need.

None of these documents is contractual unless your contract says so. Your
contract of employment is the contractual document.

HOW THESE DOCUMENTS ARE MAINTAINED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Every policy shows a version, an effective date, an owner and a review date.
If you are reading one whose review date has passed, it is still in force -
tell the owner and they will either revise it or extend it, and either way
they will be glad you noticed.

Material changes are announced in the weekly bulletin and at the all-hands,
not merely published. Where a change came from something an employee raised,
we say so in the document.
