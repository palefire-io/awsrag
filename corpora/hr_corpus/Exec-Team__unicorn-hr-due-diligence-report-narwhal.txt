1. SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  This report covers workforce composition and cost, terms and conditions,
     employment status, reward, pensions (in outline - the detailed report is
     separate), employee relations, litigation, compliance, HR systems and
     data, culture, and the TUPE and consultation position.

1.2  LIMITATIONS THE READER MUST HOLD IN MIND:
     a) No access to individual employees. All information is from the vendor
        or from management interviews.
     b) The HR data provided is of poor quality. See section 9. Several
        figures in this report carry an uncertainty we cannot reduce without

     d) Narwhal's HR Director, Bernadette Astley-Quinn, joined in January 2026

        findings in this report were volunteered by her rather than
        discovered.

3. TERMS AND CONDITIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  UK terms are materially less generous than Veridia's:

  Provision            Narwhal                    Veridia
  -------------------  -------------------------  ------------------------
  Annual leave         22 days + bank holidays    27 rising to 30 + BH
  Pension employer     4% (auto-enrolment
                       minimum, on qualifying
                       earnings)                  8% to 10% on full salary
  Private medical      Senior managers only       All employees, family
                       (14 people)                cover, no excess
  Life assurance       2x salary                  4x salary
  Company sick pay     Statutory only             4-13 weeks full pay
  Primary carer leave  Statutory only             26 weeks full pay
  Secondary carer      Statutory 2 weeks          8 weeks full pay
  Learning budget      None                       GBP 1,500 + 5 days

3.2  THIS IS THE HARDEST PRACTICAL PROBLEM IN THE TRANSACTION AND IT IS NOT
     THE PENSION.

     On a TUPE transfer, transferring employees keep their existing terms.
     Harmonising downwards is unlawful where the reason is the transfer.
     Harmonising upwards is lawful, is expensive, and is what we would do.

     Cost of harmonising the 265 UK employees to Veridia terms:
       Pension (4% to 8% on full salary)      GBP 1.42M per annum
       Annual leave (22 to 27 days)           GBP 0.61M per annum equivalent
       Private medical                        GBP 0.31M per annum
       Life assurance and income protection   GBP 0.09M per annum
       Company sick pay (accrual estimate)    GBP 0.18M per annum
       Family leave (accrual estimate)        GBP 0.12M per annum
       Learning budget                        GBP 0.40M per annum
       -------------------------------------  ----------------------
       TOTAL ANNUAL COST OF HARMONISATION     GBP 3.13M per annum

3.3  GBP 3.13M per annum against modelled synergies of GBP 3.1M annualised.
     The harmonisation cost cancels the entire synergy case.

3.4  THE OPTIONS, STATED PLAINLY:

     (a) Do not harmonise. Run two sets of terms indefinitely. This is lawful.
         It is also, in the CPO's assessment, corrosive: two people doing the
         same job on the same site, one with family private medical and 27
         days and one with neither. Veridia has no experience of operating
         this way and its culture is not built for it.

     (b) Harmonise fully. GBP 3.13M per annum. The synergy case does not
         survive it and the Board would be approving a transaction on a
         different financial basis than the one modelled.

     (c) Harmonise partially and over time - pension and leave first, benefits
         over three years. Approximately GBP 1.9M in year one rising to
         GBP 3.13M by year four. This is the CPO's recommendation and it is
         still not cheap.

     (d) Reduce Veridia's own terms to converge. NOT RECOMMENDED AND THE CPO
         WILL NOT SUPPORT IT. It would be a fundamental breach of trust with
         318 existing employees to fund the acquisition of a company they did
         not ask us to buy.

3.5  Recommendation: option (c), costed into the model at Gate 2, with the
     pension and annual leave harmonised at completion and the remainder over
     36 months. The Board should be told that the synergy case is materially
     weaker than modelled.

4. POLAND - WROCLAW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  145 employees. Manufacturing and machining. Solidarnosc recognised since
     2017.

4.2  A company collective agreement (Zakladowy Uklad Zbiorowy Pracy) is in
     force, registered with the District Labour Inspectorate, and provides:
       - severance above the statutory scale: 3 months' pay at under 2 years'
         service, 6 months at 2 to 8 years, 12 months above 8 years, against
         a statutory maximum of 3 months
       - a guaranteed annual increase of at least CPI
       - restrictions on outsourcing of production
       - a consultation obligation on any change affecting more than 10
         employees, with a 30-day period

4.3  The collective agreement survives a transfer of undertaking under Article
     23-1 of the Polish Labour Code and cannot be unilaterally terminated for
     12 months following the transfer.

4.4  CONSEQUENCE: the modelled Wroclaw synergy of GBP 0.4M through
     consolidation of 9 roles would cost approximately GBP 0.55M in severance
     under the collective agreement, before any consultation risk. It is
     value-destructive in year one and marginal by year three. Polish counsel
     recommends it be removed from the model.

4.5  Works council: none, but a works council may be established on the
     request of 10 per cent of employees, and Solidarnosc has raised it twice.
     A change of control is a plausible trigger.

4.6  There is one Polish labour court claim outstanding. See section 6.

5. EMPLOYMENT STATUS - THE CONTRACTOR POPULATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Narwhal engages 34 field service engineers as self-employed contractors
     through personal service companies. They have been engaged in this way
     since 2016.

5.2  On the documents reviewed, these engagements have the following features:
       - Narwhal-branded uniform and vehicle livery
       - Narwhal allocates the work; the contractor cannot decline
       - No right of substitution in practice; the contract contains one but
         Narwhal confirmed it has never been exercised and management said it
         "wouldn't be allowed"
       - Narwhal-provided tools, parts stock and diagnostic equipment
       - Contractors appear on the Narwhal customer-facing engineer list
       - Average engagement length 4.7 years; longest 9 years
       - Several work exclusively for Narwhal

5.3  MARCHMONT SAYERS' ASSESSMENT: at least 26 of the 34 are very likely to be
     employees or workers at law, and the off-payroll working rules (IR35)
     apply to the engagements from April 2021 with Narwhal as the client
     responsible for the status determination. No status determination
     statements have been issued.

5.4  Exposure:
       PAYE and NIC, 4 years, 26 engagements     GBP 1.42M
       Interest                                  GBP 0.21M
       Penalties (assuming careless not
         deliberate)                             GBP 0.28M
       ----------------------------------------  ----------
       Estimated HMRC exposure                   GBP 1.91M

       Plus employment rights exposure: holiday pay, unfair dismissal rights,
       pension auto-enrolment arrears. Not separately quantified; Marchmont
       Sayers estimate a further GBP 0.4M to GBP 0.9M if claims were brought.

5.5  This is a known-unknown to Narwhal management. Bernadette Astley-Quinn
     raised it with her Board in March 2026 and volunteered it to us before we
     found it. Her memorandum to the Narwhal Board is in the data room and it
     is a good document. No action has been taken.

5.6  RECOMMENDATION: specific indemnity from the sellers, uncapped as to time
     for the HMRC element, backed by an escrow of not less than GBP 2.5M for
     four years. If the sellers will not give it, the price must move by at
     least that amount. Do not accept a general warranty; this is a known
     issue and a known issue is not covered by a warranty against the unknown.

5.7  POST-COMPLETION: these 26 people become Veridia's problem on day one and
     they are field service engineers, which is the group Veridia is already
     short of. The right answer is almost certainly to employ them properly
     on Veridia terms, which costs money and which they will overwhelmingly
     welcome. It also increases the Veridia field service establishment by 26
     at a stroke, which materially changes the picture described in the 2025
     stress risk assessment - in a good way.

6. LITIGATION AND EMPLOYEE RELATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Detailed schedule at UNI-DD-HR-002. Summary:

6.1  ET CLAIM 1 - unfair dismissal and disability discrimination. Former
     production supervisor, Warrington, dismissed on capability grounds
     following long-term absence. No occupational health referral was
     obtained at any point. Marchmont Sayers assess prospects of successfully
     defending as POOR. Estimated exposure GBP 45,000 to GBP 95,000.

6.2  ET CLAIM 2 - equal pay. Female quality engineer, comparator a male
     colleague at the same grade. Narwhal has no job evaluation scheme and no
     equal pay audit. Marchmont Sayers cannot assess prospects without a job
     evaluation exercise Narwhal has not done. Estimated exposure
     GBP 30,000 to GBP 180,000, and a material risk of further claims if it
     succeeds given the 21.4 per cent pay gap.

6.3  POLISH LABOUR COURT - unlawful termination during a protected period.
     Employee dismissed while on sick leave. Polish counsel assess prospects
     of defending as POOR; reinstatement is the primary remedy in Poland and
     is a realistic outcome. Exposure PLN 180,000 plus reinstatement.

6.4  HOLIDAY PAY - historic underpayment. Narwhal has calculated holiday pay
     on basic pay only, excluding regular overtime and shift premia, since at
     least 2018. Following Chief Constable of PSNI v Agnew the series of
     deductions is not broken by a three-month gap.
       Estimated exposure, UK, all affected employees   GBP 1.06M
     No claim has been brought. Narwhal is aware; the finance director
     described it as "on the list".

6.5  GRIEVANCES: 14 raised in 24 months across 410 employees. Veridia's rate
     is 7 in 18 months across 300. Adjusted for headcount, Narwhal's grievance
     rate is approximately 1.5 times ours. Of the 14, 9 concerned a manager's
     behaviour and 3 concerned the same manager, who remains in post.

7. REWARD AND PAY EQUITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  No pay bands. No job evaluation. No structured pay review. Increases are
     decided by the line manager and approved by the Finance Director, with no
     calibration and no equality analysis.

7.2  Gender pay gap (UK, April 2025 snapshot):
       Mean hourly gap        21.4%   (Veridia 11.4%)
       Median hourly gap      18.9%   (Veridia 9.8%)
       Mean bonus gap         34.1%   (Veridia 18.2%)
     Published as required. No action plan published, which is not unlawful
     but is now unusual and is noticed.

7.3  Denise Kaur's benchmarking (UNI-DD-HR-004) finds Narwhal pays below
     market median at every level, with the gap widening at senior grades. On
     transfer to Veridia bands, 41 of 265 UK employees would sit below the
     Veridia band minimum for their assessed level, requiring immediate
     uplift. Cost approximately GBP 0.38M per annum and included in the
     harmonisation figure at 3.2.

7.4  The absence of any pay structure is an opportunity as well as a risk. A
     population with no bands can be mapped onto Veridia's framework at
     completion without unpicking an existing structure. Denise Kaur's view is
     that this is materially easier than harmonising two structured systems.

8. PENSIONS - SUMMARY ONLY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Full report at UNI-DD-PEN-001. In summary: a closed defined benefit scheme
with a GBP 9.2M technical provisions deficit and a GBP 19.4M buyout deficit,
whose trustees will engage on the change of control and are likely to seek
GBP 4M to GBP 7M of mitigation, possibly with a parent company guarantee. This
is the largest single risk to the transaction and is capable of preventing it.

9. HR SYSTEMS AND DATA
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Full assessment at UNI-DD-HR-003. In summary: three systems, no single source
of truth, headcount reconciles to within 7 across sources, no records of
processing activity, retention never applied, and eleven years of leaver data
held including bank details and health records. The data protection position
is poor and Veridia would inherit it. Estimated remediation GBP 340,000 and
nine months.

10. CULTURE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 We have not been able to survey employees and will not until completion.
     What follows is assessed from documents, from turnover data, from
     grievance records and from two management interviews. It is the softest
     section of this report and it is the one the CPO regards as most
     important.

10.2 Indicators pointing one way:
       - 22 per cent turnover, 31 per cent in Wroclaw manufacturing
       - No engagement survey since 2022; the 2022 survey showed 41 per cent
         favourable on "I would recommend this company as a place to work"
       - 14 grievances in 24 months, 3 concerning the same manager in post
       - No exit interview process
       - No learning budget, no career framework, no levelling
       - A contractor population of 34 people doing employee jobs for up to
         nine years
       - Average 47.2 contracted-plus-overtime hours in manufacturing

10.3 Indicators pointing the other way:
       - R&D turnover of 8 per cent and average tenure of 7.4 years in that
         function. Something there is working.
       - Bernadette Astley-Quinn was hired in January 2026 precisely to fix
         this and is doing so. She volunteered several of the findings in this
         report.
       - Gareth Standish is well regarded by his people. Multiple documents
         reference his personal involvement in individual cases.
       - Manufacturing quality is excellent. That does not happen in a
         demoralised operation.

10.4 THE CPO'S ASSESSMENT.

     This is not a bad company. It is an under-managed one. The people
     practices are what a founder-led industrial business looks like when
     nobody has ever been given the job of building them, and a competent HR
     Director arrived four months ago and started.

     The risk is not that Narwhal's culture is toxic. It is that Veridia's
     culture is unusually good, is not written down anywhere except in
     policies that people actually read, and depends on about forty people who
     have been here since it was fifty people. Adding 410 people who have
     never experienced it, in an organisation where they will be the
     majority, is a real threat to it.

     I want it recorded that the model contains no line item for this and that
     I do not know how to put one in.

12. RECOMMENDATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  R1  Specific indemnity and GBP 2.5M escrow for the employment status
      exposure. Non-negotiable.
  R2  Specific indemnity for historic holiday pay.
  R3  Price adjustment for the pension mitigation, treated as a certainty
      rather than a contingency.
  R4  Remove the Wroclaw consolidation synergy from the model. It is
      value-destructive under the collective agreement.
  R5  Re-model the synergy case with terms harmonisation on option (c)
      included. Present the revised number to the Board at Gate 2.
  R6  Employ the 26 contractors properly at completion, on Veridia terms.
  R7  No field service headcount reduction in year one. See section 7 of this
      report and the 2025 stress risk assessment.
  R8  Bring Priya Anand inside the tent before Gate 2. The field service
      integration is the largest single people workstream and is currently
      being planned without the person who runs it.
  R9  Engagement survey at Narwhal within 90 days of completion, run by
      Veridia, with the results published to both populations.
  R10 A named executive owner for culture integration, with a budget, reporting
      to the Board quarterly for three years. The CPO's view is that without
      this, section 10 becomes a prediction rather than a risk.
