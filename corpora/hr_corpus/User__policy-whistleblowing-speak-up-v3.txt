1. WHAT THIS IS FOR
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  If you know or suspect something is wrong, this policy tells you how to
     say so, what will happen, and what protects you.

1.2  Use it for serious concerns about wrongdoing. Use the grievance procedure
     for concerns about your own treatment. If you are not sure which, use
     either - we will route it correctly rather than send you away.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT TO RAISE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - A Veridia product being unsafe or not performing as claimed
  - A quality, regulatory or vigilance obligation not being met
  - Falsification of records - quality, training, calibration, clinical,
    financial
  - Concealment of a product problem, a complaint or an adverse event
  - Danger to health and safety
  - Fraud, theft, bribery or corruption
  - Breach of competition law
  - Discrimination, harassment or victimisation, including of others
  - Misuse of personal or patient data
  - Damage to the environment
  - Deliberate concealment of any of the above
  - A criminal offence, or a failure to comply with a legal obligation

You do not need proof. You need a reasonable belief. Investigating is our job,
not yours, and you should not attempt to gather evidence yourself.

-----------------------------------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. ANONYMITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  You may report anonymously through Safecall or the web form.

4.2  We would rather you gave your name, because it lets us ask follow-up
     questions and tell you the outcome. But anonymous reports are
     investigated with the same seriousness, and two of the five reports in
     2025 were anonymous.

4.3  If you give your name, we keep it confidential as far as we can. We
     cannot always guarantee it - occasionally the nature of a concern makes
     the source obvious, and in rare cases a legal process compels disclosure.
     We will tell you honestly if we think that applies rather than promise
     something we cannot deliver.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Within 3 working days   Acknowledgement (where we can reach you)
  Within 10 working days  Initial assessment complete; you are told whether
                          it will be investigated and by whom
  Investigation           Timescale depends on complexity. You are updated at
                          least every 4 weeks.
  Conclusion              You are told the outcome in as much detail as we
                          can lawfully give. We may not be able to tell you
                          what happened to another individual.

5.1  The investigator is always independent of the concern. A concern about a
     Director is not investigated by that Director's function.

5.2  Concerns involving the Executive Committee go to the Board Audit
     Committee.

5.3  Serious concerns are reported to the Audit Committee in summary,
     quarterly, whatever their outcome.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. PROTECTION FROM RETALIATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  You are protected. No dismissal, no disciplinary action, no effect on
     pay, promotion, appraisal, work allocation, shift pattern or selection in
     a restructure.

6.2  Retaliation is gross misconduct. This includes subtle retaliation -
     exclusion, cold-shouldering, sudden reassignment - which is what
     retaliation usually actually looks like.

6.3  Protection applies even if your concern turns out to be mistaken,
     provided you raised it honestly.

6.4  Protection does not apply to a knowingly false report made maliciously.
     We have never had one.

6.5  If you think you are being retaliated against, tell Sunita Raval or the
     Chair directly. This is treated as a new and serious concern in its own
     right.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. LEGAL PROTECTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
UK: the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 gives statutory protection to
qualifying disclosures. You do not need to have exhausted internal routes to
be protected, and dismissal for making a protected disclosure is automatically
unfair with no qualifying service requirement.

Switzerland: protection is less codified but Veridia applies this policy
identically to Swiss employees as a matter of company commitment.

Nothing in your contract, in a settlement agreement, or in any confidentiality
undertaking prevents you from making a protected disclosure or from speaking
to a regulator. Any clause that appeared to do so would be unenforceable and
we do not use them.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT WE HAVE LEARNED FROM THIS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Included because a speak-up policy that has never produced anything is a
policy nobody believes in.

  2023   3 reports. One led to a change in how a distributor was managed.
  2024   4 reports. One led to a manager leaving the company.
  2025   5 reports, 2 anonymous. One led to the change in pregnancy loss leave
         now in User__policy-family-leave-v4.txt - the previous policy applied
         only from 24 weeks and only to the person who had been pregnant, and
         someone affected by it told us it was wrong. It was.
         One concerned a quality record and was investigated and not upheld;
         we told the person why, in detail, and they agreed with the
         conclusion.
