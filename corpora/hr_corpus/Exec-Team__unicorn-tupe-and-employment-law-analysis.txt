1. THE STRUCTURE AND WHY IT MATTERS FOR EMPLOYEES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  The transaction is structured as a SHARE PURCHASE: Veridia acquires 100
     per cent of the issued share capital of Narwhal Industrial Holdings Ltd.

1.2  ON A SHARE PURCHASE, TUPE DOES NOT APPLY. The employer does not change.
     Employees continue to be employed by the same legal entity on the same
     terms with the same continuous service. Their contracts are untouched.
     There is no transfer, no measures, and no obligation to inform and
     consult under regulation 13 of TUPE.

1.3  This is the single most important point in this report and it is
     routinely misunderstood, including by experienced managers. Somebody in
     the integration planning will say "we need to do TUPE consultation". At
     completion, we do not.

1.4  WHERE TUPE WOULD APPLY. If, after completion, Veridia moves activities
     between legal entities - for example transferring the Narwhal field
     service operation into Veridia BioSystems Ltd, or consolidating a
     function - that internal reorganisation would very likely be a service
     provision change or a business transfer and TUPE WOULD then apply to that
     step. Any such step requires its own analysis, its own information and
     consultation, and its own timetable.

     Integration planning must therefore treat each post-completion
     reorganisation as a separate TUPE event rather than assuming the whole
     thing was dealt with at completion.

2. WHAT EMPLOYEES ARE ENTITLED TO AT COMPLETION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  UK EMPLOYEES: no statutory entitlement to be informed or consulted about
     a share sale. There is no legal obligation to tell them anything.

2.2  That is the legal position and it is not the recommendation. See section
     6.

2.3  POLAND: DIFFERENT AND MORE ONEROUS.
     Under Article 26-1 of the Polish Trade Unions Act, an employer must
     inform the recognised trade union of matters affecting employees' working
     conditions and employment status. Kaczmarek Wiszniewski advise that a
     change of control of the parent is very likely to fall within this.

     Solidarnosc is recognised at Wroclaw. The company collective agreement
     also contains an express consultation obligation on "any change affecting
     more than 10 employees" with a 30-day period.

     PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE: the Polish site must be told, and the union must
     be engaged, on a timetable that does not fit a UK "announce at completion"
     model. Kaczmarek Wiszniewski recommend engaging Solidarnosc between
     signing and completion, under confidentiality, rather than presenting
     completion as a fait accompli.

2.4  Failure to consult in Poland does not invalidate the transaction but can
     found a claim, and more practically will poison the relationship with a
     union at a site with 31 per cent turnover and a manufacturing capability
     that is a principal reason for the acquisition.

3. THE COLLECTIVE AGREEMENT AT WROCLAW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Zakladowy Uklad Zbiorowy Pracy, registered with the District Labour
     Inspectorate, in force since 2017.

3.2  Key provisions:
       - Severance above statutory: 3 months at under 2 years' service,
         6 months at 2 to 8 years, 12 months above 8 years. The statutory
         maximum is 3 months.
       - Guaranteed annual increase of at least CPI.
       - Restriction on outsourcing production activities.
       - Consultation on any change affecting more than 10 employees, 30-day
         period.
       - Enhanced notice periods.

3.3  The agreement survives a change of control and, following a transfer of
     undertaking, cannot be unilaterally terminated for 12 months. On a share
     purchase there is no transfer, so the agreement simply continues
     indefinitely until renegotiated or terminated in accordance with its own
     terms, which requires notice and negotiation.

3.4  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE SYNERGY MODEL: the modelled Wroclaw reduction of 9
     roles carries severance of approximately GBP 551,000 against an annual
     saving of GBP 398,000. Payback of 16.6 months on a headline basis, and
     that ignores the consultation risk, the union relationship and the
     restriction on outsourcing. Polish counsel recommend the item be removed
     from the model. The CPO agrees.

4. HARMONISATION OF TERMS - WHAT IS AND IS NOT LAWFUL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Since TUPE does not apply on a share purchase, the TUPE restriction on
     varying terms by reason of the transfer does not apply either. This is
     more permissive than most people assume.

4.2  However, ordinary contract law does apply. A contractual term cannot be
     varied without consent, and imposing a variation risks constructive
     dismissal claims and claims for unlawful deduction from wages.

4.3  HARMONISING UPWARDS - improving Narwhal terms to match Veridia's -
     requires no consent and is straightforward. It is expensive. See
     UNI-DD-HR-001 section 3.

4.4  HARMONISING DOWNWARDS - reducing Veridia's terms to converge - would
     require consent from 318 employees or dismissal and re-engagement. It is
     legally possible and it would be, in Gideon Ashcombe-Ward's words, "a
     spectacularly poor idea in a company whose employee proposition is a
     retention asset". The CPO has stated she will not support it.

4.5  A THIRD OPTION sometimes proposed: leave existing employees on existing
     terms and hire all NEW employees on a single harmonised set. This avoids
     the cost and creates a two-tier workforce that persists for a decade. Not
     recommended.

5. REDUNDANCY - IF AND WHEN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  No decision has been taken and none should be represented as taken.

5.2  If redundancies are proposed post-completion:

     UK - COLLECTIVE CONSULTATION THRESHOLDS
       20 to 99 proposed dismissals at one establishment in 90 days:
         30 days' consultation before the first dismissal takes effect
       100 or more:
         45 days
       Fewer than 20: individual consultation only, no collective obligation

     The modelled 34 reductions are spread across two countries and, in the
     UK, across two establishments (Warrington and Cambridge). Whether they
     count as "one establishment" is fact-sensitive following USDAW v Ethel
     Austin and the subsequent case law. Marchmont Sayers' view is that
     Warrington and Cambridge are separate establishments, which would put
     each below 20 and avoid the collective obligation.

     THAT VIEW SHOULD NOT DRIVE THE PLAN. Structuring a redundancy programme to
     fall below a consultation threshold is legally available and is exactly
     the kind of thing that reads badly in a tribunal and worse in a leak.
     Marchmont Sayers advise conducting meaningful collective consultation
     regardless of whether the threshold is technically engaged.

     POLAND
       Collective redundancy rules apply at 10 or more dismissals in 30 days
       for an employer of Narwhal Wroclaw's size, plus the 30-day consultation
       obligation in the collective agreement, plus notification to the
       Powiatowy Urzad Pracy. Additionally, severance under the collective
       agreement rather than the statutory scale.

5.3  SELECTION. Any pool must be objectively defined and any criteria
     objectively applied and capable of evidence. Length of service alone is
     not a safe criterion. Absence records must be used with extreme care and
     disability-related absence must be discounted.

5.4  SPECIFIC WARNING ON FIELD SERVICE. Veridia's own field service function
     is the subject of a live stress risk assessment (HSRA-2025-002, October
     2025) identifying understaffing as the root cause of elevated absence,
     two occupational health referrals and 14.2 per cent turnover, and
     identifying recruitment from 36 to 42 as the substantive control
     measure.

     A redundancy in that population within twelve months of that assessment
     would be extremely difficult to defend. The assessment is a health and
     safety document, it would be disclosable, and it says in terms that the
     function is understaffed. Marchmont Sayers' advice is unambiguous: do
     not.

5.5  SPECIFIC WARNING ON INDIVIDUALS WITH LIVE HR MATTERS. Any employee on
     long-term sick leave, on family leave, with a live grievance, or who has
     made a protected disclosure, requires individual legal review before
     inclusion in any pool. There is at least one Veridia employee on
     long-term sick leave at the Basel site (case OH-2026-003) with a note on
     file requiring CPO sign-off before any organisational communication
     reaches them.

6. WHAT WE SHOULD DO EVEN THOUGH WE DO NOT HAVE TO
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  The legal minimum at completion is to tell UK employees nothing.

6.2  Marchmont Sayers and the CPO both recommend against the legal minimum,
     for the following reasons:

     (a) There is already speculation. Four of thirteen Veridia exit
         interviews in H2 2025 referred unprompted to rumours the company was
         "doing something", and the People team FAQ has carried a question
         about acquisition rumours since June 2026. Silence is not currently
         producing an absence of belief; it is producing invention.

     (b) A transaction of this size becomes public at completion regardless.
         Employees who learn about it from LinkedIn conclude that they were
         not trusted, and that conclusion is durable.

     (c) Veridia's employee proposition is built on a set of policies that
         repeatedly promise directness. The speak-up policy, the pay review
         guidance, the exit interview process and the FAQ all say some version
         of "we will tell you what we can, including when the answer is that
         we cannot say". Announcing an acquisition of 410 people with no
         warning would be inconsistent with everything else we have written
         down, and employees would notice.

6.3  The recommended approach is at UNI-COM-001. In outline: same-day
     announcement to both populations at completion, delivered in person by
     the CEO and CPO at each site, with the Polish union engaged under
     confidentiality between signing and completion, and with an explicit
     statement of what is NOT changing that is honest about what is not yet
     decided.

7. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES - EMPLOYMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Recommended, in priority order:

  W1  Specific indemnity, uncapped as to time, for the employment status and
      off-payroll working exposure, backed by escrow of not less than GBP 2.5M
      for four years. NON-NEGOTIABLE.
  W2  Specific indemnity for historic holiday pay underpayment.
  W3  Specific indemnities for each live claim, with conduct provisions giving
      Veridia control of the ACAS employment status matter.
  W4  Warranty that all employees have the right to work and that compliant
      checks were carried out - and note this warranty will almost certainly
      be qualified by disclosure, given that 22 of 40 sampled records had no
      evidence attached.
  W5  Warranty on compliance with working time and record-keeping obligations
      in both jurisdictions, given the open Polish grievance about overtime
      records.
  W6  Warranty that no employee earning above a threshold has given or
      received notice.
  W7  Warranty on the completeness of the disclosed employment terms, and
      specifically that no undisclosed contractual entitlement exists.
  W8  Data protection warranty and indemnity for pre-completion processing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. SUMMARY OF ADVICE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  8.1  TUPE does not apply at completion. It will apply to post-completion
       internal reorganisations, each of which needs its own analysis.
  8.2  There is no UK obligation to consult at completion. There is a Polish
       obligation and it starts before completion.
  8.3  Harmonising Narwhal terms upwards is lawful and expensive. Harmonising
       Veridia's downwards is lawful and inadvisable.
  8.4  Do not structure a redundancy programme around a consultation
       threshold.
  8.5  Do not touch field service headcount in year one.
  8.6  Do the communication properly even though you do not have to.
