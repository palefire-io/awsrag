1. PRINCIPLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  People leave. Most of the people who leave Veridia will work in this
     industry for another twenty years, will meet our staff at conferences,
     and may become customers, partners or colleagues again. How we handle the
     last four weeks is disproportionately what they will remember.

1.2  This policy applies whatever the reason for leaving. Resignation,
     redundancy, end of a fixed term, retirement, dismissal, or the sad ones.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. RESIGNING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Tell your manager first, in a conversation, and confirm in writing to
     your manager copying people@veridiabio.com. The written notice is what
     starts the clock.

2.2  Your notice period is in your contract. Standard periods:

       Level        UK notice        Switzerland notice
       -----------  ---------------  ------------------------------
       L1 to L2     1 month          1 month (after probation)
       L3           2 months         2 months (from 2nd year)
       L4 to L5     3 months         3 months (from 10th year)
       L6           6 months         3 months

     Swiss notice periods are the statutory minima under the Code of
     Obligations unless your contract states longer. Notice runs to the end of
     a calendar month in Switzerland.

2.3  Your manager cannot refuse a resignation and cannot require you to stay
     longer than your notice period. Equally, we can agree a shorter notice
     period by mutual consent and often do where it suits both sides.

2.4  You do not have to say where you are going. Most people do. We ask
     because it helps us understand the market, not because we intend to do
     anything about it.

2.5  COUNTER-OFFERS. Veridia does not make counter-offers as a matter of
     course. Where a manager wants to make one they need Chief People Officer
     approval. This is deliberate: a counter-offer usually addresses the
     symptom and rarely the reason, and the industry data on how long
     counter-offered employees stay is poor. If your pay is wrong, we would
     rather fix it before you resign, which means telling us before you
     resign.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. GARDEN LEAVE AND RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  We may place you on garden leave for some or all of your notice. You
     remain employed, remain paid, retain your benefits, and must remain
     available. You must not work for anyone else during it.

3.2  Garden leave is used where there is a genuine commercial concern - most
     often a move to a direct competitor from a commercial, regulatory or R&D
     role. It is not used as a punishment for resigning and it is not the
     default. In 2025, 3 of 41 leavers were placed on garden leave.

3.3  Restrictive covenants, where your contract contains them, survive your
     employment. They are in your contract; if you cannot find them or do not
     understand them, ask the People team and we will explain them plainly,
     including what we would and would not seek to enforce.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE LAST FOUR WEEKS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Week -4   People team sends you a leaver pack. Manager agrees a handover plan
          with you and starts recruitment or cover.
Week -3   Handover documents written. Not verbal handover. Written.
Week -2   Knowledge transfer sessions with whoever picks up your work.
          Customer-facing roles: introduce your successor to your accounts.
Week -1   Exit interview (section 6). Equipment return arranged. Final
          expenses submitted - after your last day this becomes painful for
          everyone.
Last day  Accounts disabled at 18:00 local time, not at 09:00. You should be
          able to say goodbye to people.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT YOU MUST RETURN
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Laptop, phone, monitors and peripherals
  - Building access card, keys, lockers emptied
  - Company credit card
  - Vehicle, fuel card and its documentation (field service)
  - Test instruments, service tools, calibration equipment
  - Any customer documentation, controlled documents or samples

Return in person where you can. Where you cannot, IT arranges a courier with
prepaid packaging. Do not put a laptop in the post uninsured.

If something is genuinely lost we deal with it sensibly. Repeated failure to
return equipment after reminders may result in a deduction from final pay
where your contract permits it, which we have done twice in nine years.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXIT INTERVIEWS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Offered to every leaver. Voluntary. Conducted by the People team, never
     by your manager.

6.2  Held in your last week, or up to four weeks after leaving if you would
     rather have the distance. Some of the most useful ones we have had were
     six weeks after the person left.

6.3  What happens to what you say:
     - Themes are reported quarterly to the Executive Committee in aggregate.
     - Nothing attributable to you is shared with your manager without your
       explicit agreement.
     - If you tell us something that discloses a safeguarding issue, a
       regulatory concern, discrimination or unlawful conduct, we will act on
       it, and we will tell you at the time that we are going to.

6.4  You may decline, and you may also do it in writing instead. About a
     quarter of leavers decline and that is entirely their right.

6.5  We will not ask you to sign anything at your exit interview.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. YOUR FINAL PAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Paid on the normal pay date for the month in which you leave, not
     earlier.

7.2  It includes: salary to your leaving date; accrued untaken annual leave;
     any bonus or commission you have contractually earned; approved expenses.

7.3  It may deduct: annual leave taken in excess of accrual; outstanding
     season ticket or cycle-to-work balances; training costs where a repayment
     agreement was signed and applies; unreturned equipment per section 5.

7.4  You will receive a P45 (UK) or the equivalent exit documentation and
     Arbeitszeugnis (Switzerland). Swiss employees are entitled to a full
     qualified reference (qualifiziertes Arbeitszeugnis) and we will provide
     one; it is a legal entitlement and not a favour.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR BENEFITS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Pension (UK)         Contributions stop with your final pay. Your pot stays
                       with the provider; they will write to you.
  BVG (Switzerland)    Vested benefit transfers to your new employer's fund or
                       to a vested benefits account. The pension fund writes
                       to you directly and you must respond.
  Private medical      Cover ends on your last day. Continuation on a personal
                       policy is available directly from the insurer at your
                       cost; ask the People team for the contact.
  Life assurance       Ends on your last day.
  Share options        Governed by the option agreement. Generally 90 days to
                       exercise vested options for a good leaver. READ YOUR
                       AGREEMENT and speak to the People team early - this is
                       the single thing leavers most often get wrong, and the
                       deadline is not extendable.
  EAP                  Available to you for three months after you leave.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. REFERENCES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Veridia gives factual references as standard: job title, dates of
     employment, and confirmation of the reason for leaving where asked and
     where accurate.

9.2  Managers may give a personal reference in a personal capacity, on
     personal paper, making clear they are not writing on behalf of the
     company. Many do.

9.3  We do not give negative references. We answer factually and we do not
     volunteer more than we are asked.

9.4  Reference requests go to people@veridiabio.com. Managers should not
     answer employment reference requests directly.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. STAYING IN TOUCH
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 The Veridia alumni list is opt-in. Ask at your exit interview.

10.2 We rehire. In 2025 four of our hires were former employees, and one of
     them is now a Head of. There is no policy against it and no waiting
     period.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
11. IF YOU ARE LEAVING FOR A DIFFICULT REASON
-----------------------------------------------------------------
11.1 Redundancy, ill health, or a dismissal are covered by their own
     procedures. This policy still applies to the mechanics of leaving.

11.2 Where an employee dies in service, the People team leads on contacting
     the family, death in service benefit and pension arrangements. Managers
     should not attempt to handle this alone. Contact
     naomi.okonjo-bright@veridiabio.com directly.
