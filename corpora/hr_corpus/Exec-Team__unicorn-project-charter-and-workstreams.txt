1. WHAT PROJECT UNICORN IS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Project Unicorn is Veridia BioSystems' evaluation of, and potential offer
     for, 100 per cent of the issued share capital of Narwhal Industrial
     Holdings Limited ("Narwhal"), a UK-headquartered manufacturer of
     pre-analytical laboratory automation systems.

1.2  Codenames used throughout project documentation:
       "Unicorn"   the transaction
       "Veridia"   Veridia BioSystems Ltd - used openly in internal documents
       "Narwhal"   Narwhal Industrial Holdings Ltd - used openly in internal

                   entries, travel bookings or expense descriptions
       "Kelpie"    Ardenmoor Capital, the private equity shareholder in
                   Narwhal
       "Selkie"    the alternative target evaluated and rejected in
                   January 2026

1.3  Calendar entries use "Unicorn review" and nothing more. Travel to
     Warrington or Wrocław is booked personally and expensed as "business
     development - confidential" with the CFO as approver.

2. STRATEGIC RATIONALE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Veridia's automation product, the VeriPrep SP-1, is a single benchtop
     station. It decaps, identifies, aliquots and sorts, and it integrates
     with our analysers. It is a good product with a well-documented weakness:
     the r1 tolerance problem with short-cap tubes cost us eighteen months of
     customer goodwill, a subsidised retrofit programme and a redesign we
     should not have needed. We did not have the mechanical engineering depth
     in-house to have got it right first time.

2.2  Narwhal has that depth. They have been building tube handling mechanisms
     for fourteen years and their tolerance engineering is materially better
     than ours. Their SortLine and ArchiveCell products sit in large
     laboratories that we do not currently reach with the SP-1.

2.3  The strategic case, in one sentence: Narwhal gives Veridia the track
     automation portfolio and the mechanical engineering capability that would
     otherwise take us five years and a lot of unhappy customers to build.

2.4  Commercially: Narwhal's installed base is 340 systems across 190
     laboratories in the UK, Germany, Poland and the Nordics. Approximately 40
     per cent of those laboratories already run at least one competitor
     haematology analyser. The revenue synergy case rests on displacement into
     that base over five to seven years, which is slower than the model
     assumes and is the single softest assumption in the financial case.

8. STANDING RISKS TO THE PROJECT ITSELF
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  MANAGEMENT ATTENTION. Six of nine Executive Committee members are
     materially engaged. The 2026 operating plan assumes their full attention
     and does not have it. Monitored monthly by the CEO.

8.2  DISCLOSURE. See section 6. The single largest risk to the project.

8.3  PENSION. The Narwhal defined benefit scheme is the largest single
     financial and structural risk in the transaction and is capable of
     preventing it. See UNI-DD-PEN-001.

8.4  CULTURE AND CONDUCT. The HR diligence has identified findings at Narwhal
     - long hours, contractor misclassification, an unaddressed pay gap, two
     tribunal claims - which are individually manageable and which
     collectively describe a company that has not invested in its people
     function. Integrating that into Veridia is a real risk to Veridia's own
     culture and it is not adequately priced in the model.

     Naomi Okonjo-Bright's minuted position of 2026-06-04: "We are good at
     this. They are not. Acquisitions do not reliably transmit the acquirer's
     culture to the target; sometimes it goes the other way. I want that
     written down before anybody signs anything."
