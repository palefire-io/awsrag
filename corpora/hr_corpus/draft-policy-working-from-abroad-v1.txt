-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  This policy sets out when an employee may work temporarily from a
     country other than their contractual country of employment, and what
     approvals are required first.

1.2  It does not create a right to work abroad. Every request is subject to
     tax, social security, immigration and data protection review, and any
     one of those may make a request impossible to approve.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS COVERED
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Short stays: up to 20 working days in a rolling 12 months, in a single
     country, while remaining tax resident in your home country.

2.2  Anything longer, or spanning more than one country, is treated as an
     international assignment and falls outside this policy. Speak to your
     HR Business Partner before making any commitment.

2.3  Holiday during which you do not work is not covered by this policy. You
     do not need permission to take annual leave abroad.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. APPROVAL ROUTE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Raise the request in Workday at least 30 calendar days before travel.

3.2  Your line manager confirms the work is practicable remotely and that
     the team can cover core hours.

3.3  People Operations then runs the tax and social security check. For
     Switzerland-based employees this includes an A1 certificate assessment.

3.4  Requests involving access to production customer data are additionally
     reviewed by Information Security, because data residency commitments in
     some customer contracts restrict where that data may be accessed from.
     See the Data Residency FAQ in the product corpus.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT WE CANNOT APPROVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Countries subject to trade sanctions, or where Veridia has no legal
     entity and the stay would create one (permanent establishment risk).

4.2  Stays that would take you over a host country's tax residency threshold.

4.3  Roles requiring physical presence: laboratory, field service and
     manufacturing roles are excluded.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR REVIEWERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Should the 20-day allowance be pro-rated for part-time employees?
5.2  Legal to confirm whether the A1 process applies to UK employees
     travelling to Switzerland post-2021.
5.3  Decide whether unapproved working abroad is a conduct matter under the
     Code of Conduct, or handled informally at first instance.
