Internal Mobility and Secondments
=================================================================
Document:      HR-POL-027
Version:       2
Effective:     2025-06-01
Owner:         Tobias Frankl, Talent Acquisition Lead
Co-owner:      Rachel Duffy, HR Business Partner

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. POSITION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Every role is advertised internally for at least 5 working days before or
     alongside any external advertising. No exceptions for senior roles.

1.2  You do not need your manager's permission to apply for an internal role.
     You do not need to tell them before you apply. We ask that you tell them
     before the final stage, so that nobody is blindsided.

1.3  A manager may not block an internal move. They may ask for a reasonable
     handover period, normally up to 8 weeks and occasionally 12 for a
     critical role.

1.4  Wanting to move is not disloyalty. A company where people can only grow by
     leaving is a company that will keep hiring replacements for people it
     already had.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. APPLYING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Internal roles are on the intranet careers page and announced in the
     weekly bulletin.

2.2  Apply through PeopleHub. A short statement is enough; you do not need a
     full CV for an internal move.

2.3  You will get a response within 5 working days and an interview or a
     reason. Internal candidates who are not progressed get a conversation
     with the hiring manager, not an email. This is the part most companies
     get wrong.

2.4  Eligibility: normally 12 months in your current role, and not on a
     performance support plan. The 12 months is a guideline and is waived
     regularly - most often where someone is clearly in the wrong role and
     both managers can see it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR TERMS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Continuous service, notice period, annual leave entitlement, pension and
     benefits all carry across unchanged.

3.2  No new probation. A three-month settling-in period applies, and if the
     move does not work the first option considered is a return to a
     comparable role rather than an exit.

3.3  Pay: a lateral move at the same level does not normally change pay unless
     the new role sits in a different band, in which case you move to the new
     band. A move that is also a promotion follows the promotion policy.

3.4  A move to a lower level, where someone chooses it, is handled
     individually. Pay is normally protected for 12 months and we will tell
     you clearly what happens after that.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. SECONDMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  A temporary move to another team, typically 3 to 12 months, with a
     guaranteed return to your substantive role.

4.2  Your terms, level and pay stay as they are. If the secondment role is at
     a higher level, an acting allowance applies - typically 8% to 12% for the
     duration.

4.3  Both managers and the People team agree it in writing before it starts,
     including the return date and what happens if the receiving team wants to
     make it permanent.

4.4  Recent examples: a support engineer seconded to firmware for six months
     after the 2025 timestamp investigation, who is now permanently in
     firmware; a QC analyst from Basel seconded to Cambridge applications for
     four months; two field engineers seconded into the product team for the
     SP-1r2 development.

4.5  Cross-site secondments between Cambridge and Basel are actively
     encouraged and Veridia funds accommodation and travel. Immigration and
     tax arrangements are handled by the People team; do not attempt to
     arrange this yourself.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. SHADOWING AND SHORT PLACEMENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Anyone may spend up to 5 days a year shadowing another team. Agree it
     with both managers. It does not come out of your learning days.

5.2  Particularly worth doing: a day with field service if you are in
     engineering, a day in support if you are in product, a day in the Basel
     QC laboratory if you are in the UK. People come back with a different
     understanding of what our customers actually deal with.

5.3  Customer site visits: anyone may request to accompany a field engineer or
     an account manager on a customer visit, subject to the customer agreeing
     and to you completing the confidentiality and site safety briefing. This
     is one of the most valuable things available to you and it is
     underused - eleven people did it in 2025.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. IF YOU ARE THINKING ABOUT MOVING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Talk to the People team confidentially first if you would rather not
     start with your manager. Rachel Duffy or Lena Hofstetter. It will not get
     back to your manager.

6.2  We will be honest with you about whether a move is realistic and what you
     would need to do to make it so. Sometimes the answer is that the gap is
     large, and we would rather tell you that than let you apply and be
     rejected.

6.3  If you are considering leaving Veridia because of your role rather than
     the company, tell someone before you resign rather than after. We can
     usually do something about the first and rarely about the second.
