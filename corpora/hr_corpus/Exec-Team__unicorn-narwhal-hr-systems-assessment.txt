-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE LANDSCAPE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Narwhal operates THREE systems holding employee data, with no single
     source of truth and no integration between any pair of them.

  SYSTEM 1 - Sage People (HRIS)
    Implemented 2019. UK employees only. Holds core records, absence, and
    an unused performance module.
    Records: 265 current UK employees plus 1,140 leavers.
    Owner: HR. Administered by one person (an HR Advisor, not a systems
    specialist).

  SYSTEM 2 - Access Dimensions Payroll
    Implemented 2013, on premise, running on a Windows Server 2016 instance
    that is out of extended support since January 2027 - eight months away.
    UK payroll only. Holds bank details, NI numbers, salary history, P11D
    data, court orders and attachment of earnings.
    Owner: Finance. Administered by the Payroll Manager.
    NO INTEGRATION WITH SAGE PEOPLE. Starters, leavers and salary changes are
    keyed into both systems by hand.

  SYSTEM 3 - "Kadry" (local Polish HR and payroll system)
    Wroclaw only. Polish-language, supplied by a local vendor, hosted by that
    vendor in Poland.
    Holds all Polish employee data including ZUS records, PPK pension, medical
    examination records (obligatory under Polish law) and working time records.
    Reconciled to group reporting quarterly BY A MANUAL SPREADSHEET.
    Owner: Wroclaw site administration.

1.2  In addition: an Excel file, held on the HR shared drive, described by the
     HR Director as "the actual org chart", which is the only place the
     reporting structure is accurately recorded. It is not access controlled
     beyond the HR folder permissions and it contains salary.

2. DATA QUALITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  HEADCOUNT DOES NOT RECONCILE.
       Sage People            410
       Payroll                413
       Statutory accounts     417 (implied from the employee note)
     A 7-person discrepancy in a 410-person company. Management attributes it
     to leavers not closed in Sage and to two employees on unpaid leave. We
     have accepted 410 with a stated uncertainty and have flagged that we
     cannot verify actual headcount until completion.

2.2  Sample testing of 40 records against source documents:
       Start date matches contract                     37 / 40
       Salary matches payroll                          36 / 40
       Job title matches contract                      29 / 40
       Manager field populated and current             31 / 40
       Right to work evidence attached                 22 / 40
       Contract on file                                34 / 40

2.3  The job title and manager findings are the most operationally significant:
     roughly a quarter of records do not accurately state who someone works
     for. This makes any consultation exercise - which requires accurate
     identification of affected populations and their managers - materially
     harder.

2.4  RIGHT TO WORK EVIDENCE ATTACHED IN 22 OF 40 RECORDS. Extrapolated, up to
     130 UK employees may have no right to work evidence on file. This does
     not mean they lack the right to work. It means Narwhal may have no
     statutory excuse, and on transfer Veridia acquires the obligation with 60
     days from the transfer date to carry out its own checks.

     This is precisely the scenario anticipated in the watch item added to
     Veridia's own right to work audit (HR-AUD-2026-001 section 5.3). Rachel
     Duffy holds a scoped plan. The exercise would cover up to 265 UK
     employees, take approximately six weeks, and require two temporary
     resources. Estimated cost GBP 28,000. IT MUST BE A DAY ONE ACTIVITY.

3. DATA PROTECTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Ruth Amankwah's assessment, on an anonymised basis.

3.1  NO RECORDS OF PROCESSING ACTIVITY (Article 30). None exists for HR data.
     This is a straightforward compliance failure for an organisation of this
     size.

3.2  RETENTION NEVER APPLIED. Sage People holds 1,140 leaver records, the
     oldest from 2013. Payroll holds records from 2013 including bank account
     details, NI numbers and, in 44 cases sampled, attachment of earnings
     orders. Nothing has ever been deleted.

     Statutory retention for payroll records is 3 years from the end of the
     tax year (6 recommended). For general HR records, 6 years after
     employment ends is the usual position. Narwhal is holding 11 to 13 years
     of data including special category and financial data with no lawful
     basis for retaining most of it.

3.3  HEALTH DATA HELD WITHOUT ADEQUATE CONTROL. Sage People contains an
     unstructured "Notes" field on the absence record. Sample review of 60
     absence records found 14 containing free-text clinical information -
     diagnoses, medication, in two cases the name of a treating consultant -
     entered by line managers. This is special category data under Article 9,
     entered by people with no training, visible to anyone with HR module
     access, and retained indefinitely.

     Ruth Amankwah's comment: "This is the single worst finding in the
     assessment. It is not a system problem. It is a training and culture
     problem and it will exist in the people, not in the database, on the day
     the data is migrated."

3.4  NO DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS for any HR processing.

3.5  NO PROCESSOR AGREEMENT with the Polish "Kadry" vendor that Ruth
     Amankwah considers Article 28 compliant. The agreement is a two-page
     services contract from 2014.

3.6  SUBJECT ACCESS. Four requests in 24 months, all from former employees in
     the context of a dispute. Two were answered outside the statutory period.
     One was not answered at all.

3.7  NO REPORTED PERSONAL DATA BREACHES. Ruth Amankwah's view is that this is
     more likely to reflect an absence of detection than an absence of
     breaches, given that there is no logging on the payroll system and the
     org chart spreadsheet is on an open shared drive.

4. SECURITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Yusuf Demir's assessment, on an anonymised basis.

4.1  Payroll on Windows Server 2016, extended support ending January 2027.
     No plan to migrate. This is a hard deadline eight months from now and it
     is independent of the transaction.

4.2  No multi-factor authentication on Sage People. Password only.

4.3  Payroll accessed by four named users. No privileged access management, no
     session logging, no periodic access review.

4.4  Backups: payroll backed up to a NAS on the same site. No offsite copy
     tested in the last 24 months. A fire in the Warrington server room would
     destroy the payroll system and its backup.

4.5  No penetration testing of any HR system, ever.

4.6  Yusuf's summary: "If this were our estate I would classify the payroll
     server as a critical finding requiring remediation inside 90 days, and I
     would not be comfortable that we would detect an exfiltration if one had
     occurred."

5. WHAT INTEGRATION WOULD ACTUALLY INVOLVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Veridia runs PeopleHub for HR and a single outsourced payroll provider
     for both UK and Swiss payroll. The target state is one HRIS and one
     payroll relationship across the combined group.

5.2  Realistic sequence:

  Phase 0 - PRE-COMPLETION (from signing)
    Right to work remediation plan finalised and resourced
    Payroll server remediation plan agreed with the vendor - this cannot wait
      for completion given the January 2027 support deadline
    Data protection remediation scoped

  Phase 1 - DAY ONE TO MONTH 3
    Run everything as-is. Change nothing. Two payrolls, three HR systems.
    Right to work exercise across the UK population. MUST complete within 60
      days of transfer.
    Immediate: turn off the Sage People free-text absence notes field and
      brief every Narwhal manager on why. This costs nothing and stops the
      worst finding getting worse.
    Retention policy applied and the 2013-2019 leaver data deleted, subject to
      any litigation hold.

  Phase 2 - MONTH 3 TO MONTH 12
    UK payroll consolidated onto the Veridia provider. This removes the
      Windows Server 2016 exposure and is the single highest-value change.
    Records of processing activity created for the combined group.
    Processor agreements remediated.
    Data quality remediation - the 40-record sample suggests roughly 25 per
      cent of records need correction, which is approximately 100 records
      requiring manual verification against source documents.

  Phase 3 - MONTH 12 TO MONTH 24
    Sage People migrated to PeopleHub.
    Polish payroll and HR reviewed. RECOMMENDATION: do not migrate Kadry.
      Polish payroll is highly specific, the local system works, and the
      integration cost exceeds the benefit. Keep it and build a proper
      reporting interface.
    Single org structure in one system.

5.3  COST AND RESOURCE

       Polish reporting interface                GBP  34,000
       Contingency 15%                           GBP  61,650

5.4  RESOURCE: one full-time HR systems lead for 24 months, plus two temporary

6. RISKS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  The payroll server support deadline of January 2027 falls between target
     signing (October 2026) and target completion (January 2027). If the
     transaction slips, Narwhal is running payroll for 265 people on an
     unsupported server and it is not our problem. If it completes on time, it
     becomes our problem in the first month.
     MITIGATION: raise it with Narwhal now, as a diligence finding, and press
     them to remediate before completion at their cost.

6.2  The right to work exposure is the one with a legal consequence and a hard
     clock. 60 days from transfer.

6.3  The free-text health data is the one with the highest reputational
     consequence. If a Narwhal employee made a subject access request post-
     completion and received their manager's free-text note about their
     medical condition, on Veridia letterhead, that would be genuinely
     damaging and entirely deserved.

6.4  Data migration always surfaces problems that diligence did not. A 7-
     person headcount discrepancy in a 410-person company is a signal that
     there will be more.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. RECOMMENDATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  S1  Correct the systems remediation figure in the model to GBP 473,000.
  S2  Right to work verification as a day one activity, resourced and
      budgeted, completing within 60 days of transfer. Non-negotiable.
  S3  Disable the free-text absence notes field on day one.
  S4  Require Narwhal to remediate the payroll server before completion at
      their cost, as a condition.
  S5  Hire an HR systems lead before completion, not after.
  S6  Do not migrate the Polish system. Build a reporting interface.
  S7  Bring Yusuf Demir and Ruth Amankwah inside the tent before
      implementation planning. Both have now assessed this landscape without
      knowing what it is, which is not a sustainable basis for planning the
      work.
  S8  Specific warranty on data protection compliance, and an indemnity for
      any regulatory action arising from pre-completion processing.
