1. PURPOSE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  To set out what happens between an offer being accepted and a new
     colleague being fully productive, and who is responsible for each part
     of it.

1.2  Onboarding at Veridia is deliberately longer than at most companies of
     our size. We make regulated medical devices. A field service engineer who
     has not understood why a quality record matters is a risk to patients and
     to the company, and that understanding is not acquired in an afternoon.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  CONDITIONAL OFFER. Every offer is conditional on satisfactory
     pre-employment screening. Screening covers identity and right to work,
     employment history, professional qualifications where the role requires
     them, and references. Roles with access to customer clinical data or to
     regulated processes carry additional checks.

     What screening involves from your side is set out in the pack sent with
     your offer. If something in your history is likely to come up, tell your
     recruiter before the check rather than after. A discrepancy that you have
     already explained is almost never a problem. One discovered by a screening
     provider always becomes one.

2.2  RIGHT TO WORK. UK: we must see and verify your right to work before your
     first day, without exception, and we cannot start you without it. This is
     a legal requirement on Veridia, not a preference. Switzerland: permit
     status must be confirmed and, where a permit application is required, the
     start date is set by the cantonal authority and not by us.

2.3  CONTRACT. Issued electronically. Please read it. If anything differs from
     what you were told verbally, say so before you sign - it is far easier to
     correct then.

2.4  EQUIPMENT. IT ships your laptop to your home address to arrive two working
     days before you start, or holds it at reception if you prefer. Tell your
     recruiter which.

2.5  WHAT YOU DO NOT NEED TO DO. You do not need to read anything, set anything
     up, or join any calls before your start date. If someone asks you to,
     tell the People team.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. YOUR FIRST DAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Start time is 10:00 in your local time zone, not 09:00, so that nobody is
     travelling in rush hour on a day that is already stressful.

3.2  Cambridge starters: reception at 41 Hurst Way. Ask for the People team.
     Basel starters: reception at Klybeckstrasse 141. Fully remote starters:
     your manager will send a calendar invite.

3.3  The day is deliberately light. Laptop setup, accounts, a building tour
     where relevant, lunch with your team, and one conversation with your
     manager about what the first month looks like. You will not be given work.

3.4  Mandatory day one items:
     - Health and safety induction (site-based roles)
     - Information security and acceptable use
     - Code of conduct acknowledgement
     - Confirmation of your emergency contact and, if you choose to share it,
       any accessibility or health information you want us to know about

     On that last point: sharing is entirely voluntary and what you share goes
     to the People team, not to your manager, unless you say otherwise. If you
     need an adjustment to do your job well, you do not have to justify it with
     a diagnosis. See User__policy-grievance-and-dignity-at-work-v4.txt
     section 9 and speak to Rachel Duffy or Lena Hofstetter.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. YOUR FIRST WEEK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The induction agenda is in User__onboarding-induction-agenda-week-1.txt. In
summary:

  Day 1   Setup, team, manager conversation
  Day 2   What Veridia makes and who buys it. Product overview: VeriScan 400
          and 400 Pro, VeriPrep SP-1, VeriLyse reagents, VeriConnect,
          VeriGuard. Non-technical; everybody attends this regardless of role.
  Day 3   Quality and regulatory induction. Why we are regulated, what a
          Field Safety Notice is, what your personal obligations are, and what
          to do if you ever think something is wrong with a product. MANDATORY
          FOR EVERY EMPLOYEE including finance, marketing and the People team.
  Day 4   Information security, data protection and customer confidentiality.
          Includes what patient data we do and do not hold.
  Day 5   Your team. Systems, tools, ways of working, first task.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE BUDDY SCHEME
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Every starter is assigned a buddy: someone outside their reporting line,
     usually 1 to 3 years in, whose job is to answer the questions you do not
     want to ask your manager.

5.2  A buddy is not a mentor and is not responsible for your training. The
     commitment is a 30-minute conversation a week for the first eight weeks.

5.3  Buddies volunteer. If you want to be one, tell the People team. It is
     recognised in performance conversations as contribution, and being a good
     buddy is legitimate evidence in a promotion case.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE FIRST 90 DAYS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Detailed in User__onboarding-first-90-days-guide.txt. Checkpoints:

  Day 7    Manager check-in. Is anything blocking you?
  Day 30   Structured review against the goals set in week one. People team
           sends you a short survey; the free-text answers are read and acted
           on, and they have changed this policy twice.
  Day 60   Manager review. Probation trajectory discussed honestly. If there
           is a concern, you hear it here and not at day 90.
  Day 90   Probation decision (see User__policy-probation-period-v3.txt).

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. ROLE-SPECIFIC ONBOARDING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  FIELD SERVICE ENGINEERS. 12-week structured programme including
     supervised customer visits. You will not attend a customer site
     unaccompanied before week 8, and not before your competency sign-off in
     any case. This is non-negotiable and applies regardless of prior
     experience elsewhere.

7.2  SUPPORT ENGINEERS. 6-week programme. Live tickets from week 2 under
     review, independent from week 5.

7.3  MANUFACTURING AND QC (BASEL). Training records are part of the regulated
     quality system. You cannot perform a task unsigned; your trainer signs
     you off per task and the record is auditable. This is not bureaucracy for
     its own sake - it is inspected.

7.4  ROLES TOUCHING THE QUALITY SYSTEM. Additional GxP training within the
     first 30 days, refreshed annually.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. RESPONSIBILITIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Hiring manager   Owns the outcome. Sets the first month's goals before day
                   one, holds the check-ins, makes the probation decision.
  People team      Owns the process, the paperwork, the week one agenda and
                   the day 30 survey.
  IT               Equipment and accounts, ready on day one.
  Buddy            Weekly conversation for eight weeks.
  You              Ask. Nobody at Veridia thinks less of a new starter for
                   asking a question in week three. Several of us think less
                   of people who guess.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. IF ONBOARDING GOES WRONG
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  If your laptop has not arrived, your accounts do not work, your manager is
     on holiday for your first week, or nobody has told you what you are meant
     to be doing - tell the People team. These things happen and we would
     rather fix them in week one than read about them in your day 30 survey.

9.2  In 2025 we changed this policy because the day 30 survey told us that 4
     starters in 12 months had a manager on leave during their first week. The
     hiring manager is now required to be present for a starter's first three
     days or to formally delegate to a named deputy.
