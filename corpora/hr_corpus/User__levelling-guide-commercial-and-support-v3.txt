Covers sales, account management, customer support, marketing, regulatory
affairs, quality, finance, legal and the People team. The engineering guide is
separate.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
L1 - ASSOCIATE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope        Defined tasks with a known approach and close support.
Sales        Qualifies inbound enquiries. Supports a named territory. Builds
             product knowledge.
Support      First-line triage against documented runbooks. Knows when a
             ticket is beyond them, which matters more than knowing the answer.
Corporate    Prepares work for review. Learns the regulatory or financial
             framework they will operate in.

The most valuable L1 behaviour across all these functions is accurate
handover: capturing what a customer actually said rather than what you assumed
they meant.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
L2 - EXECUTIVE / ENGINEER / ANALYST
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope        Owns a territory, a queue, or a defined area of a function.
Sales        Owns a territory or segment and a number. Runs a full cycle from
             qualification to close with support on commercials.
Support      Second-line diagnosis. Owns escalations to conclusion. Can hold a
             P1 with a customer whose laboratory is down and keep them
             informed rather than reassured.
Corporate    Owns a recurring process end to end - a regulatory submission
             section, a month-end close, a recruitment pipeline.

Judgement    Knows what they do not know and escalates early. In support
             specifically: knows the difference between "no fault found" and
             "cannot reproduce", and records the difference.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
L3 - SENIOR / MANAGER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope        Owns a region, a major account set, or a functional area.
Sales        Owns a region or the largest accounts. Handles a tender end to
             end. Manages a relationship through a product problem without
             losing it - which is a distinct and undervalued skill.
Support      Owns the escalation process and the quality of second line. The
             person who spots that two unconnected tickets are the same
             problem. Every significant field issue in our history was
             identified by someone doing this well.
Corporate    Owns a functional area and is the company's answer on it. When a
             notified body, an auditor or a customer's regulatory lead asks a
             hard question, you are who answers.

Judgement    Says the unwelcome thing to a customer or an executive when it is
             true. Distinguishes an unhappy customer from a customer with a
             problem, which are different and need different responses.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
L4 - HEAD OF / PRINCIPAL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope        A department, or a specialist domain that spans the company.
Sales        Owns a country or a channel, and the plan for it.
Support      Owns support as a function - process, tooling, quality, the
             relationship with engineering and regulatory.
Corporate    Owns a domain: regulatory strategy, reward, financial planning.
             Sets what the company does, not only how it complies.

Judgement    Makes decisions whose consequences land on other departments and
             carries the cross-functional conversation themselves. Balances
             the commercial and the regulatory without pretending the tension
             does not exist.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
L5 - DIRECTOR / VP
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Scope        A function, its budget, its structure, its leadership.
Impact       The company can do something it otherwise could not.
Judgement    Owns decisions publicly, including the wrong ones. Represents
             Veridia externally in a way that survives scrutiny.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
SUPPORT-SPECIFIC PROGRESSION NOTES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Written with James Okafor because support progression is frequently
misunderstood.

Ticket volume is not a levelling criterion at any level, and a high-volume L2
is not thereby an L3. What distinguishes support levels is the class of
problem you can hold:

  L1  Known problem, documented answer
  L2  Unknown problem, known method
  L3  Unknown problem, no method, and a customer under pressure
  L4  Problems that are not yet visible as problems

The last one is the hard one and it is what turns a support function from
reactive to useful. The clustering review that now runs weekly in complaint
handling exists because that kind of pattern recognition had previously
depended on one person happening to notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMERCIAL-SPECIFIC NOTES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Hitting a number is necessary at L2 and L3 and is not sufficient for
promotion. A territory hit through a single large deal that landed for reasons
outside your control is not the same evidence as a territory built.

Equally, missing a number in a year when a product problem took a quarter out
of your pipeline is not evidence against you, and calibration panels are told
this explicitly. In 2025 two commercial promotions were approved for people
who missed their number, and the reasoning was recorded.
