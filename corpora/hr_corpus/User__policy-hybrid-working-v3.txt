1. THE SHORT VERSION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Most office-based roles work two days a week on site and the rest wherever
they choose. Which two days is agreed within your team, not dictated by the
company.

Many roles cannot work this way and we are honest about that rather than
pretending otherwise.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHO CAN WORK HOW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
FULLY FLEXIBLE (2 days on site as a team norm)
  Software, firmware, cloud operations, marketing, finance, legal, People
  team, commercial office-based roles, regulatory affairs (with exceptions
  during audits and submissions).

SITE-DEPENDENT (mostly or entirely on site)
  Manufacturing and QC (Basel). The work is physical, the equipment is here,
  and the quality system requires it.
  Laboratory and applications roles requiring instrument access.
  Facilities.

FIELD-BASED (neither, really)
  Field service engineering. You are at customer sites. Your "office" days are
  administrative and can be anywhere, but your working pattern is driven by
  the visit schedule. Field engineers get a home working allowance and an
  equipment budget on the same basis as everyone else.

FULLY REMOTE
  A small number of roles are contractually remote, mostly where we hired
  someone excellent who lives nowhere near Cambridge or Basel. These are
  agreed individually and stated in the contract.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHY TWO DAYS AND NOT ZERO OR FIVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
We are asked this often enough to answer it properly.

Two days is a compromise and we know it satisfies nobody completely. The
reasoning:

  - Our products are physical. A firmware engineer who has never watched a
    VeriScan run a sample writes worse firmware. That access is not
    replicable remotely.
  - New starters onboard measurably faster with in-person contact. Our day 30
    survey data is unambiguous on this.
  - Cross-team problems get solved in corridors. The reagent recall
    investigation moved fastest in a room.
  - Against that: forcing attendance for its own sake produces people sitting
    on video calls in an open-plan office, which is the worst of both worlds
    and which we did in 2022.

We review this annually. If the evidence changes, so will the policy.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TEAMS DECIDE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Each team agrees its own anchor days and writes them down. Most teams
     land on Tuesday and Wednesday or Wednesday and Thursday.

4.2  The point of anchor days is that people overlap. Two people in the office
     on different days is not hybrid working, it is two people alone in an
     office.

4.3  Teams may vary the pattern by week where it makes sense - a design
     sprint, a customer visit, a quiet period.

4.4  Managers should not require attendance beyond the team norm without a
     reason they are willing to say out loud.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. FLEXIBLE HOURS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside those, work when you
     work best, provided your commitments are met.

5.2  Compressed hours, term-time working, annualised hours and part-time are
     all available and all in use somewhere in the company.

5.3  Switzerland: working time recording remains a legal requirement whatever
     your pattern. This is not negotiable and is not a signal of distrust.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. FORMAL FLEXIBLE WORKING REQUESTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  You have a statutory right in the UK to request flexible working from day
     one, twice in any 12 months. Veridia treats a request from anyone,
     anywhere, the same way.

6.2  We will meet you within 2 weeks and decide within 4, against a statutory
     limit of 2 months. Refusals must be for one of the statutory business
     reasons and must be explained in writing with a right of appeal.

6.3  In 2025 we received 18 formal requests and approved 17. The one refusal
     was a request for fully remote working in a role requiring instrument
     access; we offered a different role instead and the person took it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. WORKING FROM ELSEWHERE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  ANOTHER VERIDIA SITE. Just go. Book a desk.

7.2  ANOTHER COUNTRY, SHORT PERIOD. Up to 20 working days a year from another
     country, subject to approval from your manager and the People team.
     The approval is not about trust, it is about tax and social security -
     an employee working from an EU country can create a permanent
     establishment risk and a personal tax liability, and neither of us wants
     to discover that afterwards.

     Tell us at least 4 weeks in advance. Some countries we will decline, and
     we will explain why.

7.3  MOVING PERMANENTLY. Talk to us early. We can employ people in the UK and
     Switzerland directly. Elsewhere is complicated and sometimes impossible.
     We have made it work three times and been unable to twice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. YOUR HOME WORKSTATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  GBP 400 (UK) or CHF 450 (CH) on joining towards desk, chair and
     accessories. Refreshed every 3 years.

8.2  Display screen equipment self-assessment on joining and annually. If it
     flags a problem, we fund an assessment and the equipment.

8.3  Veridia provides laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset. You are
     not expected to use your own.

8.4  We do not contribute to household bills beyond the statutory allowance
     you can claim yourself. Ask Finance how.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
9. WHAT WE ASK OF YOU
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Be contactable during core hours, or say when you will not be.
  - Keep your calendar accurate. It is the only way a distributed team works.
  - Do not work from a location where you cannot take a confidential call. A
    field engineer discussing a customer's instrument fault from a train
    carriage is a confidentiality problem.
  - Secure your workspace. Customer data and regulated documents do not go on
    kitchen tables in shared houses.
  - Come in when it matters, even if it is not your day: a new starter's first
    week, a workshop, a colleague's last day.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT WE WILL NOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Monitor your keyboard, mouse or screen. We do not use activity tracking
    software and we will not.
  - Measure your performance by attendance.
  - Require a camera on for internal calls.
  - Ask you to justify a day at home.
