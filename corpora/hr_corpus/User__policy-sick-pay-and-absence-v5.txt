1. PRINCIPLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  If you are ill, do not work. This is not a courtesy, it is what we want.
     Someone working through illness makes mistakes, takes longer to recover,
     and in shared offices and laboratories makes other people ill.

1.2  We do not operate trigger points that automatically lead to disciplinary
     action, and we do not use return-to-work interviews as an interrogation.
     Absence is managed as a conversation, and where a pattern emerges the
     first question is always what is causing it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. TELLING US
-----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Contact your manager as early as you can on the first day, by whatever
     means you normally use. A message is fine; you do not have to telephone
     and you do not have to speak to anyone if you feel awful.

2.2  Tell us roughly how long you expect to be off, if you know. Keep in touch
     - a short message every few days is enough.

2.3  You do not have to give a diagnosis. "I am unwell" is a complete answer.
     Many people volunteer more and that is their choice.

2.4  Record it in PeopleHub when you are well enough, or ask your manager to.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. CERTIFICATION (UK)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  1 to 7 calendar days     Self-certification. No doctor's note.
  8 days or more           Fit note from a GP or other approved professional.

We do not ask for a doctor's note for short absences. GP appointments for a
note you do not clinically need waste NHS capacity, and several of our
customers are the NHS.

If a fit note says "may be fit for work" with adjustments, we will act on the
adjustments. That is the whole purpose of the form and we treat it seriously.

4. COMPANY SICK PAY (UK)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Service at first day
  of absence         Full pay          Then half pay
  -----------------  ----------------  ---------------
  Under 6 months     4 weeks           4 weeks
  6 to 24 months     8 weeks           8 weeks
  Over 24 months     13 weeks          13 weeks

Inclusive of Statutory Sick Pay, calculated on a rolling 12 months.

Beyond company sick pay, SSP continues to its statutory limit, and income
protection may begin at 26 weeks at 75% of base salary. See
User__benefits-handbook-uk-2026.txt section 4.

Company sick pay is discretionary in the sense that it may be withheld where
absence is not genuine. In nine years we have withheld it once.

5. RETURNING TO WORK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  A return-to-work conversation happens after every absence. It takes five
     minutes for a two-day absence. Its purpose is: are you actually better,
     do you need anything, and what did you miss.

5.2  After a long absence we will discuss a phased return. Phased returns are
     paid at full pay for the reduced hours for up to four weeks. We do not
     make people use annual leave to come back gradually.

5.3  Your manager will not ask for details you have not volunteered.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. LONGER OR RECURRING ABSENCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Where absence is long-term or forms a pattern, the People team becomes
     involved. The first purpose is support, not process.

6.2  OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH. We may ask for your consent to an occupational
     health referral. You may refuse. If you consent:
       - You see the report first and may comment on it before it is released.
       - The report tells us what you can do and what adjustments would help.
         It does not tell us your diagnosis unless you agree.
       - Refusing consent will not of itself lead to any detriment, though it
         does limit what we can do to help.

6.3  REASONABLE ADJUSTMENTS. If you have a disability within the meaning of
     the Equality Act 2010, or a condition that affects your work whether or
     not it meets that definition, we will consider adjustments. You do not
     need a formal diagnosis to ask, and you do not need to use the word
     disability.

     Adjustments we have made include changed hours, changed duties, equipment,
     a different workstation, reduced travel, remote working, and in one case
     a change of team. Your manager is told what to do, not why.

6.4  ILL-HEALTH CAPABILITY. In rare cases, where someone cannot return and no
     adjustment makes the role possible, we consider ill-health capability.
     This is a formal process with the right to be accompanied and a right of
     appeal, and it is never a first step. Redeployment is always considered
     first. Two cases in nine years, both of which ended in redeployment.

7. MENTAL HEALTH
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Mental ill health is sickness. It is certified the same way, paid the
     same way and managed the same way.

7.2  Support available:
       - Employee Assistance Programme, 24/7, free, confidential, and Veridia
         is not told you used it. 0800 028 0199 (UK), 0800 55 44 10 (CH).
       - Bupa mental health cover, up to 25 therapy sessions a year (UK).
       - Trained Mental Health First Aiders in Cambridge and Basel; the list
         is on the intranet.
       - Occupational health referral where useful.

7.3  Managers: you are not a clinician and you are not expected to be. What
     you are expected to do is notice, ask, listen and adjust. If you are
     worried about someone, ring Rachel Duffy or Lena Hofstetter.

7.4  In 2025 the field service team was the subject of a stress risk assessment
     following an unusually heavy year - the reagent recall, the firmware
     issue and the SP-1 workload landed together. Changes to on-call
     compensation and rota planning came out of it. If your team's workload
     feels unsustainable rather than merely busy, say so; the mechanism for
     doing something about it exists.

9. SWITZERLAND
-----------------------------------------------------------------
9.1  Notify your manager on the first day. A medical certificate
     (Arztzeugnis) is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.

9.2  Salary continuation is provided through collective daily sickness benefit
     insurance (KTG): Veridia pays 100% for the first 60 days, then the
     insurer pays 90% for up to 720 days within 900 days. This is
     substantially better than the statutory Basel scale.

9.3  Swiss law provides strong protection against termination during illness
     (Sperrfrist): 30 days in the first year of service, 90 days in years 2 to
     5, and 180 days from year 6. Notice given during a protection period is
     void.

9.4  Absence caused by accident is covered by UVG accident insurance rather
     than KTG. Report accidents to Lena Hofstetter immediately, including
     those outside work, because non-occupational accident cover applies.

10. YOUR MEDICAL INFORMATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Your manager knows your absence dates and whatever reason you chose to
     give. Detailed medical information, fit notes and occupational health
     reports are held by the People team and are not shared with your manager
     without your consent.

10.2 Sickness records are retained for 6 years after employment ends.

10.3 To see what is held about you, contact dpo@veridiabio.com.
