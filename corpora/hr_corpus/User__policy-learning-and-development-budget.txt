1. WHAT YOU GET
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  UK           GBP 1,500 per calendar year
  Switzerland  CHF 1,800 per calendar year
  Plus         5 working days of learning time per year, for everyone

Available from day one. Rolls over for one year only, to a maximum of two
years' allowance. Pro-rated for part-time hours but not for a partial first
year - a September starter gets the full amount.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IT COVERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Anything that makes you better at your job now, or at a job you could
plausibly do next.

  Conferences and their travel        Yes
  Online courses and subscriptions    Yes
  Books                               Yes, and do not ask first
  Technical certifications            Yes
  Coaching                            Yes
  Language learning                   Yes - particularly EN/DE across our
                                      two sites
  Public speaking or writing training Yes
  Management training                 Yes, and see section 5
  A course in something adjacent      Usually yes. Ask.

We interpret this broadly. A support engineer who wanted to learn statistics
because half their escalations involve QC charts got it approved in an hour.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IS FUNDED SEPARATELY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
These do NOT come out of your budget:

  Professional body membership   IBMS, RSC, IET, CIPD, ACCA, IEEE and similar.
                                 Fully funded, one body, renewed annually.
  Mandatory role training        GxP, quality system, health and safety,
                                 information security, driver training,
                                 first aid.
  Regulatory and technical
  training required for your job Fully funded.
  Onboarding and induction       Obviously.
  Team training                  Funded from the team budget.

If someone tells you your mandatory training comes out of your personal
budget, they are wrong.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE FIVE DAYS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Five working days a year, paid, for learning. Not annual leave.

4.2  Use them in blocks or in hours. A day a quarter, or a Friday afternoon a
     month, or a full week for a course.

4.3  They do not carry over. Use them.

4.4  In 2025 the median number used was 2. That is too few and it is mostly a
     signal about workload and permission rather than interest. Managers: book
     them in with your people rather than waiting to be asked.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. LARGER QUALIFICATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Degrees, master's, professional qualifications with multi-year fees and
     chartership routes are funded case by case, outside the annual budget.

5.2  Approved by your Director and the People team. What we look at: does it
     serve a real need, is the timing workable, and is there a plan for the
     time it will take.

5.3  Normally accompanied by a repayment agreement:
       Leave within 12 months of completion   repay 100%
       Within 24 months                       repay 50%
       After 24 months                        nothing

5.4  THE AGREEMENT IS EXPLAINED AND SIGNED BEFORE THE COURSE STARTS, never
     afterwards. If someone presents you with one mid-course, do not sign it
     and tell the People team.

5.5  Repayment is waived if you are made redundant, and is considered
     sympathetically in cases of ill health or family circumstance.

5.6  Currently supported: two part-time MSc students, four IBMS registration
     portfolios, one CIPD Level 7, three chartered engineer applications.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO SPEND IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Under GBP 250 / CHF 300: tell your manager, buy it, expense it.

6.2  Over that: agree with your manager first. It is a conversation, not an
     application form.

6.3  Conferences: agree with your manager. If you go, we ask you to share
     something with the team afterwards - fifteen minutes is fine.

6.4  Speaking at a conference about your work: encouraged, funded from the
     team budget rather than yours, and needs Director approval for the
     content.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. MENTORING AND COACHING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Internal mentoring   Free. Ask the People team to match you. We have 24
                       active pairings.
  External coaching    From your budget, or funded separately for L4 and
                       above.
  Buddy scheme         For new starters, see the onboarding policy.
  Peer learning        Monthly Veridia Talks. Anyone can present.
                       Recordings on the intranet.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT WE WILL NOT FUND
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Anything that is really a holiday with a lecture attached
  - Vendor training for a product we do not use and are not evaluating
  - Anything you have already bought without discussing it, if it is over the
    threshold. Ask first; we say yes to almost everything.
