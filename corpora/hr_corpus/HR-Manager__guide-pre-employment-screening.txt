The candidate-facing summary of this process is in the offer pack. This
document is the operational guide and contains the decision framework, which
is not published to candidates.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRINCIPLES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Every offer is conditional on satisfactory screening. Every one, at every
     level, including executive appointments and including internal
     candidates moving into a regulated role.

1.2  We screen because we make medical devices under a regulated quality
     system, because our staff enter NHS premises and handle patient data, and
     because a falsified qualification in a regulatory or quality role is a
     genuine patient safety exposure. We do not screen to find reasons to
     withdraw offers.

1.3  A discrepancy is a conversation, not a verdict. The overwhelming majority
     of discrepancies are innocent - a wrong month, a job title the candidate
     remembers differently from how the employer recorded it, a gap they were
     embarrassed to explain.

1.4  Proportionality. The screening level is set by the role, not by seniority
     for its own sake.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCREENING LEVELS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 1 - STANDARD. All roles.
  - Identity verification
  - Right to work (UK) or permit status (CH)
  - Employment history, 3 years, with gaps over 31 days accounted for
  - Two references, at least one from the most recent employer
  - Basic financial sanctions and adverse media check
  - Qualifications where claimed and material to the role

LEVEL 2 - ENHANCED. Regulated roles: Quality, Regulatory Affairs, R&D,
Manufacturing, Applications, Field Service, Support engineering.
  - Everything in Level 1, plus
  - Employment history, 5 years
  - Professional registration verification (IBMS, HCPC, chartered status)
  - Basic criminal record check (DBS Basic in the UK; Strafregisterauszug in
    Switzerland)
  - Regulatory debarment check (MHRA, FDA, EU EudraGMDP)

LEVEL 3 - FINANCIAL AND EXECUTIVE. Finance, Legal, People team with pay
access, all L5 and L6.
  - Everything in Level 2, plus
  - Directorship and disqualification search
  - Credit and insolvency check (UK: with explicit consent, and see 4.4)
  - Adverse media, extended

LEVEL 4 - CUSTOMER SITE ACCESS. Roles requiring routine unaccompanied access
to NHS clinical areas.
  - Everything in Level 2, plus
  - Enhanced DBS where the trust requires it and the role meets the statutory
    eligibility criteria. NOTE: most of our field roles do NOT meet the
    eligibility criteria for Enhanced DBS. Where a trust asks for one and the
    role is not eligible, we say so and offer a Basic check instead. Applying
    for a check the role is not eligible for is an offence.
  - Occupational health clearance where the trust requires immunisation
    evidence
  - Trust-specific requirements as they arise

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT WE DO NOT DO
-----------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  We do not ask about salary history. Anywhere, at any stage, for any role.
     This was stopped in 2023 and the gender pay gap analysis subsequently
     identified starting-salary negotiation as a principal driver of the
     within-band residual gap.

3.2  We do not ask about health at any point before an offer is made, other
     than to ask what adjustments a candidate needs for the recruitment
     process itself. Post-offer health enquiries are limited to what is
     necessary for the role.

3.3  We do not credit check candidates for roles outside Level 3.

3.4  We do not check social media as part of screening. Adverse media checks
     are conducted by the provider against defined criteria and do not include
     personal social accounts.

3.5  We do not use spent convictions in decision-making. Under the
     Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 a spent conviction generally may not
     be taken into account, and Basic DBS certificates do not disclose them.

3.6  We do not contact a candidate's current employer without their written
     permission, ever, including where they have listed a manager as a
     referee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE DECISION FRAMEWORK FOR DISCREPANCIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  This is the part of the guide that matters. A discrepancy is graded, and
     the grade determines the route.

  GRADE A - IMMATERIAL. Resolve and proceed.
    Dates out by up to 3 months. Job title variance. A referee who has left
    the employer. A qualification grade misremembered. An unexplained gap that
    turns out to be travel, caring, illness or unemployment.
    Action: note it, resolve it, proceed. No escalation. No record beyond the
    screening file.

  GRADE B - MATERIAL BUT EXPLICABLE. Escalate to the People team.
    Dates out by more than 6 months. An omitted employer. A qualification
    claimed at a higher classification than achieved. A professional
    registration lapsed rather than held.
    Action: the People team puts it to the candidate directly and in writing,
    with the evidence, and gives them a fair opportunity to respond. Decision
    by the People team with the hiring manager. Most Grade B findings end in
    the offer proceeding.

  GRADE C - SERIOUS. Escalate to the Chief People Officer.
    A qualification that does not exist. An employer that does not exist or
    denies the employment. A directorship disqualification. A regulatory
    debarment. A criminal record disclosure relevant to the role.
    Action: CPO decision, with the General Counsel where there is any legal
    dimension. The candidate is told what has been found and given an
    opportunity to respond before any decision is taken. This is not optional
    and it is not a courtesy - a decision taken without putting the finding to
    the candidate is indefensible.

4.2  IN EVERY CASE the candidate is told what has been found and is given a
     fair opportunity to respond before a decision is made. There is no
     circumstance in which we withdraw an offer on a screening finding the
     candidate has not seen.

4.3  A criminal record disclosure is assessed against the role: what the
     offence was, how long ago, its relevance to the duties, and what the
     candidate says about it. A conviction is not an automatic bar and has not
     been treated as one in any case to date.

4.4  Adverse credit is relevant only to Level 3 roles and only where it
     indicates a specific risk. Financial difficulty is not evidence of
     dishonesty and treating it as such is both wrong and, in a company where
     a fifth of staff used the EAP's debt guidance service last year,
     hypocritical.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WITHDRAWING AN OFFER
-----------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Only with Chief People Officer approval. Never by a hiring manager
     acting alone and never by the recruiter.

5.2  The candidate is told the reason, in writing, and given the evidence.

5.3  Where the finding is Grade C and the candidate has an existing employer
     they may be about to resign from, we tell them BEFORE they resign
     wherever we possibly can. Recruiters must flag any candidate who has
     given notice while screening is outstanding.

5.4  Three offers withdrawn since 2022, all Grade C, all approved by the CPO:
     one fabricated degree, one undisclosed regulatory debarment, one where
     the named employer confirmed no record of the individual.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. TIMESCALES AND START DATES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  Target 10 working days from offer acceptance. Median actual in 2025 was
     14 days. The bottleneck is almost always a previous employer's HR
     department, not the provider.

6.2  A CANDIDATE MAY NOT START BEFORE RIGHT TO WORK IS VERIFIED. No exceptions
     and no manager discretion. This is a legal obligation on Veridia with
     civil penalties of up to GBP 60,000 per illegal worker.

6.3  Other elements of screening may be outstanding at start date with People
     team approval, with a written note of what is outstanding and by when.
     This is used two or three times a year, usually for a reference from an
     employer who has gone into administration.

6.4  If screening cannot be completed at all, the People team decides. It has
     never resulted in a withdrawal where the failure was the previous
     employer's rather than the candidate's.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
7. INTERNAL MOVES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Existing employees moving into a regulated or Level 3 role are screened
     for the incremental elements only - professional registration, DBS,
     directorship search as applicable. We do not re-verify employment history
     for someone who already works here.

7.2  Right to work is re-checked where the employee's permission to work is
     time-limited. See the right to work audit.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
8. DATA PROTECTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Legal basis: legitimate interests for most checks, with the substantial
     public interest condition under Schedule 1 Part 2 of the Data Protection
     Act 2018 for criminal record data.

8.2  Retention: screening files for unsuccessful candidates are destroyed
     after 6 months. For employees, retained for the duration of employment
     plus 6 years. Criminal record certificate details are recorded as a
     yes/no outcome and the certificate itself is NOT retained.

8.3  Candidates are told what will be checked, by whom, and on what basis,
     before screening begins.
