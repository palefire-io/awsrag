Leaver Checklist - Your Copy
=================================================================
Document:      HR-CHK-009
Version:       4
Effective:     2026-01-01
Owner:         Ollie Kettering, People Operations Coordinator
Audience:      Employees who have resigned or are otherwise leaving

Your manager and the People team have their own versions of this. This one is
yours. Print it or do not, but work through it - the items people forget are
almost always the share options and the expenses.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
AS SOON AS YOU KNOW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Resignation confirmed in writing to your manager, copying
    people@veridiabio.com
[ ] Last working day agreed and confirmed in writing
[ ] Check your notice period against your contract - do not rely on memory
[ ] Check your contract for restrictive covenants and any training repayment
    agreement. Ask the People team if anything is unclear. Ask now, not in
    your last week.
[ ] SHARE OPTIONS. Find your option agreement. Work out how many options have
    vested and what the exercise window is after your leaving date (usually 90
    days). If you intend to exercise, start the process now. This is the item
    leavers most often get wrong and the deadline cannot be extended.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
FOUR WEEKS OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Agree a written handover plan with your manager
[ ] List everything only you know how to do. Be honest and be
    unflattering about how much of it is undocumented.
[ ] List your recurring commitments - approvals you give, meetings you chair,
    on-call rotas, systems where you are the only administrator
[ ] Identify anything where you are a single point of failure and say so
[ ] Field service and support: list your open cases and their state
[ ] Anyone with customer relationships: list your accounts and their current
    temperature, including the awkward ones

-----------------------------------------------------------------
THREE WEEKS OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Write the handover documents. Written, not verbal. Verbal handover
    evaporates within a fortnight of your leaving.
[ ] File your documents somewhere your team can find them, not in your
    personal drive
[ ] Transfer ownership of any shared documents, dashboards or scripts
[ ] Hand over any admin rights, service accounts or credentials you hold.
    Credentials go to your manager or to IT, never in a chat message.
[ ] Regulated roles: confirm your training records and any open quality
    records are complete. An unsigned training record after you leave is a
    finding at the next audit and someone else has to explain it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
TWO WEEKS OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Knowledge transfer sessions with whoever is picking up your work
[ ] Introduce your successor or cover to your key contacts, internal and
    external
[ ] Customer-facing roles: agree with your manager what customers are told and
    by whom. Do not tell customers you are leaving before this is agreed - not
    because it is secret, but because they should hear who is looking after
    them next in the same sentence.
[ ] Book your exit interview with the People team (or decline it, which is
    fine)

-----------------------------------------------------------------
ONE WEEK OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] SUBMIT ALL EXPENSES. Anything unsubmitted on your last day becomes
    genuinely difficult and occasionally impossible.
[ ] Reconcile your company credit card and hand it back
[ ] Arrange equipment return with IT - laptop, phone, monitors, peripherals,
    access card, keys
[ ] Field service: arrange return of vehicle, fuel card, test instruments,
    service tools and any customer parts stock
[ ] Empty your locker, desk and pedestal
[ ] Cancel or reassign any recurring travel or accommodation bookings
[ ] Update your out of office with who to contact instead of you
[ ] Confirm the personal email address you want us to use after you leave

-----------------------------------------------------------------
LAST DAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Final handover conversation with your manager
[ ] Equipment returned and receipted - get the receipt, it protects you
[ ] Building access card handed in
[ ] Exit interview if not already done
[ ] Accounts disabled at 18:00 local time

-----------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER YOU LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Final pay lands on the normal pay date for your leaving month
[ ] P45 (UK) or exit documentation and Arbeitszeugnis (Switzerland)
[ ] Pension provider will write to you. Swiss employees MUST respond to the
    pension fund about their vested benefit - it does not sort itself out.
[ ] Share option exercise window closes (check your date - usually 90 days)
[ ] Private medical cover ended on your last day; continuation is available
    directly from the insurer at your cost
[ ] EAP remains available to you for three months
[ ] Alumni list - opt in at your exit interview if you want to

-----------------------------------------------------------------
THINGS PEOPLE ASK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
"Can I keep my laptop?"
  No, including if you offer to buy it. Devices hold customer and regulated
  data and are wiped and redeployed or securely disposed of.

"Can I take copies of work I did?"
  Your own performance records and payslips, yes. Company documents, customer
  information, source code, designs, regulatory documentation and anything
  containing personal data, no. If you want something for a portfolio, ask -
  we will usually find a way to give you something you can show.

"Will my manager see my exit interview?"
  Not attributably, and not without your explicit agreement.

"Can I come back?"
  Yes. We rehire and there is no waiting period.

"Do I have to work my full notice?"
  Contractually yes, but we frequently agree something shorter by mutual
  consent. Ask.
