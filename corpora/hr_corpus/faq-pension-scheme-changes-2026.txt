-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS CHANGING
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q. What is actually changing?
A. Two things. From 1 July 2026 the employer core contribution rises from
   5% to 6% of pensionable pay, and the matching tier above that changes
   from 1:1 up to 3% to 1:1 up to 4%. The maximum combined contribution
   therefore rises from 13% to 15%.

Q. Do I need to do anything?
A. No, if you are happy with your current contribution rate. The core
   increase is applied automatically. If you want to capture the larger
   match you will need to increase your own contribution to 4%, which you
   can do in the pension portal at any time before 20 June 2026.

Q. Is the definition of pensionable pay changing?
A. No. It remains basic salary only. Bonus, commission, shift allowance and
   the car allowance continue to be excluded.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU ARE AFFECTED BY ALLOWANCES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q. What about the annual allowance?
A. The increase may take some higher earners closer to the annual allowance.
   We cannot give you individual tax advice. If your total pension input is
   likely to exceed the allowance, the scheme provider offers a free
   guidance session - book through the portal.

Q. What if I have enhanced or fixed protection?
A. Contact Reward before the change takes effect. An automatic increase in
   employer contribution can invalidate some protections, and we can exclude
   you from the uplift if that applies to you. Email Ruth Amankwah on
   ruth.amankwah@veridiabiosystems.example, or call 020 7946 0231.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
LEAVERS, JOINERS AND FAMILY LEAVE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Q. I am on parental leave in July. Am I included?
A. Yes. Contributions during paid family leave are based on your normal pay,
   not your reduced pay, so you are not disadvantaged.

Q. I am in my notice period. Does the increase apply?
A. It applies to pensionable pay paid on or after 1 July 2026, including
   during notice, up to your leaving date.

Q. I joined in May 2026 and I am still in the opt-out window.
A. The change applies to you on the same basis. Opting out remains your
   right and does not affect any other benefit.
